# Vox Mana - RobDevPass

This is the practical usage guide for the repo-local RobDev skill. The governing authority is [RobDevPass](../../../docs/dev/RobDevPass.md); when this guide and the authority differ, the authority wins.

## 1. Authority

Use: explicit owner instruction and accepted decisions -> active work card -> applicable specialist authority -> authored source and producer contract -> current implementation and tests -> official technical documentation when needed.

Existing code is evidence of current behavior, not independent product, semantic, placement, evidence, or MTG authority. Stop and report conflicts.

## 2. Pre-flight

Before editing:

- confirm repo root, branch, and Git status;
- identify dirty and untracked work;
- identify the active card and locked owner decisions;
- read the handoff index and recent relevant handoffs;
- read relevant durable learnings and incident records;
- locate the owning source, producer, nearest implementation, consumers, and tests;
- identify generated, vendor, archive, and frozen-authority boundaries;
- do not overwrite or absorb unrelated work.

## 3. Pre-edit Contract

Identify:

- product or repository outcome;
- current behavior;
- changed behavior;
- protected behavior;
- owning authority and authoritative producer;
- nearest reusable component, adapter, presenter, resolver, generator, data shape, or state owner;
- consumers and blast radius;
- relevant failure, empty, recovery, keyboard, focus, responsive, and repeat-use states;
- smallest complete implementation;
- non-goals and stop conditions;
- unresolved owner judgment or learning objective when specified.

## 4. Authority And Producer Routing

Trace a visible value backward through its consumer, presenter, runtime projection, generator, and authored source. Change the earliest authorized owner that is actually wrong.

- Authored source outranks generated projections.
- Certified semantic, evidence, placement, and MTG-fact authorities keep their own roles.
- Structural traceability does not prove semantic support.
- Presentation may translate approved truth; it may not invent or strengthen it.
- Regenerate through the existing producer when the data contract requires it.

## 5. Reuse Before Machinery

Prefer extending current adapters, components, presenters, resolvers, generators, validators, fixtures, storage owners, and route seams.

Do not create a second engine, parallel modal, generic framework, speculative service layer, global normalization rule, branch, or worktree because the bounded change is inconvenient.

## 6. Product Boundaries

Prefer:

input or route intent -> validation or adaptation -> authoritative domain transition -> deterministic state -> presentation.

Keep:

- placement, scoring, qualification, and frontier behavior separate from labels and layout;
- certified identity meaning separate from player-facing composition;
- approved evidence relationships separate from canonical card facts;
- authored inputs separate from generated and debug outputs;
- shared semantic cores separate from route-local side effects.

## 7. Data And Truth Invariants

Protect stable IDs, canonical card facts, approved relationships, source precedence, richer committed fields, deterministic ordering, and valid references.

Never hand-edit a generated projection when its source or producer owns the correction. Never fill missing authority with model memory, generic prose, or a convenient runtime fallback.

## 8. Smallest Complete Journey

Implement the narrowest vertical slice that honestly satisfies the card, including the real visible route or repository consumer and every relevant disabled, invalid, empty, error, recovery, accessibility, responsive, and repeat-use state.

Do not expand into adjacent cleanup, content enrichment, placement tuning, semantic authoring, migration, or redesign.

## 9. Shared Consumers

When shared code, CSS, navigation, storage, data, generators, or presenters change:

- identify all material consumers;
- group materially different behaviors;
- inspect a representative of each group;
- preserve route-local exceptions and compatibility contracts;
- place the fix at the narrowest owner that knows the necessary context.

## 10. Stop Conditions

Stop rather than invent when:

- authorities or locked decisions conflict;
- a different product promise or semantic claim is required;
- placement, scoring, identity, evidence, migration, security, deployment, or destructive scope appears;
- a major architecture or dependency change emerges;
- a new branch or worktree seems necessary;
- missing evidence would require unsupported content;
- unrelated dirty work is at risk.

Surface the exact blocker and required authority. Do not compensate with larger architecture or larger test runs.

## 11. Development Validation

Inspect the actual diff and exercise the real changed path. Run focused static, schema, parser, unit, integration, or rendered checks that protect the implemented behavior.

Do not run expensive suites ceremonially. RobQA owns final tier and suite selection.

## 12. RobQA Handoff

Provide:

- card ID and repo state;
- files and authoritative owner or producer;
- changed and protected behavior;
- reused machinery or justified narrow seam;
- consumers inspected;
- realistic risks and implemented states;
- tests and development evidence;
- source or official documentation consulted;
- untouched areas and non-goals;
- limitations and unresolved owner judgment.

## 13. Completion

RobDev candidate -> repo-local RobQA skill -> owner judgment when required -> integration or accept/modify/reject.

Passing implementation checks proves only that the candidate is ready for proportionate QA. It does not prove owner acceptance, semantic authority, placement validity, or production integration.
