Vox Mana Privacy Policy

Vox Mana is an MTG alignment and deck-start tool. This policy explains what information is used when you take a reading, save a result, or sign in with Google.

Effective Date

Effective May 5, 2026.

What We Collect

Vox Mana may collect the following information when you use the site:

  • Your reading answers, interview responses, and resulting guild or college placement.
  • Your selected preferences such as format interest, budget band, and experience level.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, account details made available through authentication such as your email address, display name, profile image, and a provider-specific user identifier.
  • Basic technical and security information needed to operate the site, such as timestamps, request metadata, and anti-abuse logs generated by hosting, authentication, or API providers.

How We Use Information

Vox Mana uses this information to:

  • generate and display your MTG faction reading;
  • save your reading so you can return to it later;
  • restore your saved result after Google sign-in;
  • operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service;
  • limit abuse of the interview and authentication flows.

How the Interview Works

If you use the deeper Scrying Terminal interview, your prompts and responses may be processed by Vox Mana's server-side providers to generate a placement result. Please do not submit passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in interview responses.

Third-Party Services

Vox Mana relies on third-party services to operate. These may include:

  • Google, for optional account sign-in;
  • Supabase, for authentication, database storage, and edge functions;
  • AI processing providers used to generate interview-based readings;
  • third-party sites you choose to visit through outbound deck and card links.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

Vox Mana does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to run the service, complete authentication, process AI-powered readings, comply with law, or protect the service from abuse.

Retention

Saved readings and related account data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service or maintain account continuity. Operational and security logs may be retained for a shorter period based on provider defaults and security needs.

Your Choices

  • You can use Vox Mana without signing in for a guest reading.
  • You can choose whether to save your result with Google.
  • You can retake a reading and replace a prior saved result.
  • You can sign out at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your use of Vox Mana, contact the site operator through the support or contact method listed on the site or the OAuth consent screen.

By continuing to use Vox Mana, you acknowledge this privacy policy.
