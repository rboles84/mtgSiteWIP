Vox Mana Privacy Policy

Vox Mana is an unofficial Magic: The Gathering fan tool for Commander color identity and placement readings. This policy explains what information is used when you take a reading, save a result, or sign in with Google.

Effective May 5, 2026 Unofficial fan project Optional Google sign-in No sale of personal information
Plain-English Summary

What this page is saying

Vox Mana uses a limited set of information to run readings, restore saved results, receive optional page feedback, and operate the site. Google sign-in is optional, and outside providers help with authentication, storage, feedback delivery, and security.

  • Reading answers, placements, and selected preferences may be used to restore your account experience.
  • You can use Vox Mana without signing in, or choose Google sign-in if you want a saved result.
  • Google and Supabase help operate authentication, storage, and backend flows.
  • Optional feedback is delivered to the Vox Mana feedback inbox through Web3Forms, with page context so the note can be traced to the relevant screen or section.
  • Technical and security logs may be created by the service or its providers to keep the site running and limit abuse.
  • Questions about this policy can be directed through the public source repository or the contact method listed on the site.
01

Effective Date

Effective May 5, 2026.

02

At a Glance

Vox Mana is an unofficial fan project. Most use can happen without signing in, but optional Google sign-in can be used to save and restore a reading. Account-backed deck-link saving is currently deferred and unavailable in the active public flow. The service and its providers may also generate technical and security information needed to operate the site.

Vox Mana does not sell your personal information.
03

What Vox Mana Collects

Vox Mana may collect the following information when you use the site:

  • Your reading answers and resulting Commander identity or placement reading.
  • Your selected preferences such as format interest, budget band, and experience level.
  • Vox Mana does not currently collect private saved deck links because account-backed deck-link saving is deferred.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, account details made available through authentication such as your email address, display name, profile image, and a provider-specific user identifier.
  • If you send page feedback, the feedback text, optional reply email, page label, path, hash, visible section or section id when available, simplified browser and device label, viewport size, and timestamp.
  • Basic technical and security information needed to operate the site, such as timestamps, request metadata, and anti-abuse logs generated by hosting, authentication, or API providers.
04

How Vox Mana Uses Information

Vox Mana uses this information to:

  • generate and display your Commander identity reading;
  • save your reading so you can return to it later;
  • restore your saved result after Google sign-in;
  • receive and respond to optional page feedback;
  • operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service;
  • limit abuse of reading, save, and authentication flows.
05

How the Archscry Reading Works

Archscry evaluates the answers you choose in the quick reading against a curated placement model built from published Magic: The Gathering sources and Vox Mana's interpretive Commander identity taxonomy. The result is guidance for color identity, play-pattern fit, and what to look for when browsing Commander tools elsewhere, not an objective diagnosis or official Wizards canon.

06

Third-Party Services

Vox Mana relies on third-party services to operate. These may include:

  • Google, for optional account sign-in;
  • Supabase, for authentication, database storage, and edge functions;
  • Web3Forms, for optional feedback delivery to the Vox Mana feedback inbox and provider-side spam protection such as hCaptcha when enabled;
  • third-party sites you choose to visit through outbound deck and card links.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

07

Sharing and No Sale

Vox Mana does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to run the service, complete authentication, process readings, comply with law, or protect the service from abuse.

08

Retention

Saved readings and related account data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service or maintain account continuity. Feedback is delivered to the Vox Mana feedback inbox through Web3Forms. Web3Forms may retain submission history or log data according to its current plan and privacy terms; as of the 2026-06-27 check, the free plan listed 30 days of form submission history. Operational and security logs may be retained for a shorter period based on provider defaults and security needs.

09

Your Choices

  • You can use Vox Mana without signing in for a guest reading.
  • You can choose whether to save your result with Google.
  • You can retake a reading and replace a prior saved result.
  • You can sign out at any time.
