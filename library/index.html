The Implicit Maze

Every reading Vox Mana produces is grounded here — in the lore, the stories, the community scholarship, and the official record of twenty-plus years of Ravnica and Strixhaven.

// curated source library · not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast

Vox Mana does not invent lore. Every faction description, affinity signal, decree voice, and archetype note in this tool traces back to something in this library — a card, a story, an official article, or documented community scholarship.

The sources below are what the curated lore database is built from. AI is used to evaluate your responses against that research, not to generate content from nothing. If you want to go deeper on any faction, these are the same places we went.

About the Lore Database How This Research Becomes a Reading

The sources above were used to build a structured lore database covering all 15 factions — ten Ravnica guilds and five Strixhaven colleges. For each faction, the research was compiled into philosophy summaries, core tensions, affinity signals, interview tells, disambiguation notes, and faction-specific voice examples for the Decree of Initiation.

When you take a reading, your responses are evaluated against that structured research. The AI component matches what you say to patterns in the database — it does not generate new lore, invent card names, or produce content outside what the sources support. The reading you receive reflects the same factions these sources describe.
