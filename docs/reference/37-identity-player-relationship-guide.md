# The 37-Identity Player Relationship Guide

## Purpose

This is a lightweight way to compare a person with Vox Mana's 37 active identities. It is not a personality test, a placement score, or a claim that a deck's colors diagnose its pilot.

The useful question is not simply, "Which colors do they like?" It is:

> What do they want, how do they pursue it, what neighboring methods can they accept, what creates friction, and what would make this identity an outright false fit?

For each identity below:

- **Resonates** names affirmative fit.
- **Connects** names the nearest identities and the distinction between them.
- **Pushes back** names a productive value conflict.
- **Rejects** names a condition that makes the identity a false primary placement.

"Rejects" rejects the placement claim, not the person. A player can also express different identities through different decks.

## Relationship Skeleton

Mark Rosewater's color-pie essays provide a useful base grammar:

- White seeks peace, Blue perfection, Black power, Red freedom, and Green acceptance.
- Allied pairs connect through restraint (WU), choice (UB), individualism (BR), intuition (RG), and responsibility (GW).
- Enemy pairs argue across group versus individual (W/B), head versus heart (U/R), free will versus destiny (B/G), freedom versus security (R/W), and nature versus nurture (G/U).

Vox Mana then makes those broad relationships specific:

- the ten guilds and five colleges are setting expressions, not synonyms for color pairs;
- shards and wedges have a lead-color center, not merely three-color accumulation;
- the four-color identities are defined by the worldview pressure of the missing color;
- Colorless stands deliberately outside WUBRG, while Five-Color makes all five colors negotiate.

The supplied *Magic Player Atlas* adds a separate player layer: motivation, aesthetic, process, deck behavior, and table conduct should be recorded independently from color/setting identity. A Johnny/Jenny, Spike, Timmy/Tammy, Mel, Vorthos, System Architect, Master Planner, or Social Negotiator can meaningfully fit many different identities for different reasons.

## The 37

### Guilds And Colleges

1. **Azorius Senate (WU) - public order through procedure.**
   - **Resonates:** The person trusts documented rules, precedent, enforceable fairness, and institutions that prevent harm before crisis.
   - **Connects:** White supplies protection and Blue supplies understanding. It is near White, Blue, Boros, Selesnya, and Orzhov, but uniquely insists that accountability be public and procedural.
   - **Pushes back:** It distrusts private exceptions, impulsive intervention, and justice that cannot be made legible to everyone.
   - **Rejects:** A successful rule-breaker, secret broker, or battlefield protector is not Azorius when public procedure is treated as disposable.

2. **House Dimir (UB) - hidden information as leverage.**
   - **Resonates:** The person observes first, controls disclosure, values anonymity, and prefers advantage that does not require public credit.
   - **Connects:** Blue supplies knowledge and Black supplies agency. It touches Azorius through information, Orzhov through leverage, Grixis through weakness analysis, and Sultai through secrets, but keeps the method hidden.
   - **Pushes back:** It resists transparency as an automatic virtue and visible legitimacy as the only valid form of power.
   - **Rejects:** Generic control, mill, theft, or evasive play is not Dimir without secrecy or information asymmetry as the active method.

3. **Cult of Rakdos (BR) - appetite made public through spectacle.**
   - **Resonates:** The person values performance, provocation, sensation, release, and an act whose audience can see both intensity and cost.
   - **Connects:** Black and Red meet through individualism. It overlaps Prismari in performance, Gruul in directness, and Grixis/Jund in appetite, but the Rakdos center is transgressive public spectacle.
   - **Pushes back:** It rejects respectability, hidden pressure, and restraint that disguises what people actually want.
   - **Rejects:** Private cruelty, ordinary aggression, sacrifice mechanics, or random chaos are not Rakdos when there is no act, audience, or consequence.

4. **Gruul Clans (RG) - wild belonging resisting imposed civilization.**
   - **Resonates:** The person trusts instinct, authenticity, clan, territory, and direct action rooted in a concrete loss or endangered wild place.
   - **Connects:** Red and Green meet through intuition. Gruul is near Red, Green, Naya, Jund, and Temur, but its defining pressure is anti-city grievance and clan autonomy.
   - **Pushes back:** It distrusts institutions that broke their promises, abstraction that ignores material damage, and patience demanded by beneficiaries of the system.
   - **Rejects:** Generic rage, stompy play, or destruction is not Gruul without wildness, territory, clan, or resistance to imposed order.

5. **Selesnya Conclave (GW) - belonging through shared living purpose.**
   - **Resonates:** The person wants harmony, collective care, nature, preservation, consensus, and a community larger than private ambition.
   - **Connects:** Green and White meet through responsibility. Selesnya is close to White, Green, Naya, Bant, and Ink, but centers freely joined communal voice rather than hierarchy or public administration.
   - **Pushes back:** It resists self-only ambition, conflict for its own sake, and procedure, debt, or force replacing belonging.
   - **Rejects:** Tokens, counters, enchantments, or pleasant company are not Selesnya when individual distinction remains more important than shared care.

6. **Orzhov Syndicate (WB) - obligation converted into institutional leverage.**
   - **Resonates:** The person treats deals, hierarchy, status, repayment, and long consequences as real sources of power.
   - **Connects:** It shares structure with Azorius, agency with Black, and social pressure with Silverquill. Unlike them, its center is collectible obligation backed by religious-business legitimacy.
   - **Pushes back:** It distrusts obligation-free sentiment, chaotic power that cannot be collected, and impartial rules that ignore available leverage.
   - **Rejects:** Lifegain, drain, sacrifice, taxes, or recursion are not Orzhov without debt, deal, tithe, hierarchy, or enforceable obligation.

7. **Izzet League (UR) - discovery through experiment and invention.**
   - **Resonates:** The person follows questions, enjoys prototypes and repeated testing, treats being wrong as information, and prefers the next experiment to the finished answer.
   - **Connects:** Blue and Red stage the head/heart conflict as productive iteration. Izzet is near Blue, Red, Prismari, Quandrix, and Simic, but asks what can be discovered or built through testing.
   - **Pushes back:** It resists premature closure, untested repetition, and emotional certainty that substitutes for investigation.
   - **Rejects:** Spellslinger volume, artifacts, or novelty are not Izzet when there is no experiment, discovery, prototype, or useful breakthrough.

8. **Golgari Swarm (BG) - survival through decay, reuse, and renewal.**
   - **Resonates:** The person finds meaning in reclamation, patience, discarded material, self-sustaining cycles, and life emerging from death.
   - **Connects:** Black and Green negotiate free will and destiny through the material cycle. Golgari is near Witherbloom, Sultai, Abzan, and Green, but turns death into civic ecology and survival.
   - **Pushes back:** It resists denial of mortality, preservation that blocks renewal, and hierarchies pretending they do not decay.
   - **Rejects:** Graveyard value, morbidity, resilience, or sacrifice is not Golgari without a death-to-life cycle that feeds continued survival.

9. **Simic Combine (GU) - living systems improved through adaptation.**
   - **Resonates:** The person studies organisms, iterates pragmatically, and accepts biological intervention when it helps life adapt, heal, or endure.
   - **Connects:** Green and Blue stage nature/nurture inside living bodies. Simic is near Quandrix, Izzet, Selesnya, and Green, but keeps an organism or ecosystem at stake.
   - **Pushes back:** It resists untouched-nature absolutism, disposable-life engineering, and abstraction detached from living consequences.
   - **Rejects:** Counters, ramp, card draw, lands, or optimization are not Simic without biological study, adaptation, medicine, clade, or organism-level change.

10. **Boros Legion (RW) - organized force in service of protection.**
    - **Resonates:** The person wants visible harm answered now, values loyalty and moral clarity, and accepts discipline when it makes protection effective.
    - **Connects:** Red and White stage freedom/security through accountable intervention. Boros is near White, Red, Azorius, Mardu, and Lorehold, but acts in the present to protect civic life.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists bureaucratic delay, moral ambiguity used as an excuse, and rules that shield the wrong people.
    - **Rejects:** Combat, equipment, soldiers, or righteous anger are not Boros when force is no longer answerable to protection and public duty.

11. **Lorehold College (RW) - history recovered through scholarship and fieldwork.**
    - **Resonates:** The person studies ruins, records, relics, and the dead because the past contains usable lessons for the present.
    - **Connects:** It shares action with Boros and documentation with Azorius. Its distinctive bridge is physically adventurous historical inquiry rather than present-tense enforcement.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists sanitized history, purely theoretical learning, and dismissal of old knowledge as irrelevant.
    - **Rejects:** Red-White action, graveyard value, artifacts, or Spirits are not Lorehold without history, archaeology, records, relics, or learning from the past.

12. **Prismari College (UR) - expression given form through artistic craft.**
    - **Resonates:** The person wants an idea or emotion to be felt through a chosen medium and accepts practice, technique, and revision as part of authentic expression.
    - **Connects:** It shares creation with Izzet, performance with Rakdos, rhetoric with Silverquill, and dramatic experience with Red. Its center is art, not experiment or transgression.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists technique without feeling, feeling without form, and usefulness or proof treated as the only standards of seriousness.
    - **Rejects:** Big spells, spectacle, novelty, or performance are not Prismari when expressive purpose and disciplined medium are absent.

13. **Witherbloom College (BG) - life and death treated as practical essence craft.**
    - **Resonates:** The person learns through bodies, remedies, poisons, medicine, mortuary practice, and cost-aware movement of life energy.
    - **Connects:** It shares the life/death boundary with Golgari, living systems with Simic, and extraction with Black. Unlike Golgari, it studies and manipulates vitality as a craft.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists sanitized abstraction, optimization blind to bodily cost, and graveyard aesthetics with no living-essence practice.
    - **Rejects:** Lifegain, life payment, pests, sacrifice, decay, or nature are not Witherbloom without study, brewing, healing/harm, or vitality exchange.

14. **Quandrix College (GU) - reality understood through mathematical structure.**
    - **Resonates:** The person finds beauty in proof, patterns, symmetry, models, precision, and problems whose underlying structure takes patience to reveal.
    - **Connects:** It shares inquiry with Izzet and growth with Simic, but seeks formal pattern rather than prototype or organism-level intervention.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists approximation when rigor is available, intuition replacing evidence, and commitment before proof.
    - **Rejects:** Doubling, X spells, tokens, counters, or scaling are not Quandrix without mathematics, pattern, model, proof, or abstract structure.

15. **Silverquill College (WB) - language used as visible social action.**
    - **Resonates:** The person leads, persuades, inspires, criticizes, or pressures through precise spoken, written, signed, or magical expression.
    - **Connects:** It shares public influence with Prismari, hierarchy with Orzhov, and information with Dimir. Its active method is rhetoric and language in the room.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists vague communication, words dismissed as decoration, and influence hidden behind debt, secrecy, or procedure.
    - **Rejects:** Ambition, politics, counters, aggression, or charisma are not Silverquill without language or rhetoric doing the work.

### Monocolors

16. **White (W) - peace made durable through structure.**
    - **Resonates:** The person values shared standards, protection of the vulnerable, dependable obligations, and group safety over private exception.
    - **Connects:** Azorius formalizes White, Boros mobilizes it, Selesnya communalizes it, Orzhov weaponizes obligation, and Bant idealizes honorable responsibility.
    - **Pushes back:** White challenges Black's self-priority and Red's unrestrained immediacy, while Green can question whether White's systems listen to living reality.
    - **Rejects:** If rules are inherently oppression, private power owes nothing to others, or impulse should outrank communal safety, mono-White is a false center.

17. **Blue (U) - possibility expanded through knowledge and improvement.**
    - **Resonates:** The person studies before committing, builds models, practices deliberately, and wants tools or education to overcome starting limits.
    - **Connects:** Azorius proceduralizes Blue, Dimir conceals it, Izzet tests it, Simic applies it to life, and Esper turns it toward designed perfectibility.
    - **Pushes back:** Blue challenges Red's immediate feeling and Green's acceptance of the given, while Black asks whether knowledge produces usable agency.
    - **Rejects:** If instinct, fate, or the present impulse should be final and further understanding only delays truth, mono-Blue is a false center.

18. **Black (B) - agency secured through power, cost, and leverage.**
    - **Resonates:** The person accepts hard costs, refuses to wait for fairness, and converts resources, loss, loyalty, or death into self-determination.
    - **Connects:** Dimir hides Black's leverage, Rakdos performs its appetite, Golgari cycles its costs, Orzhov institutionalizes them, and Grixis uses them to survive.
    - **Pushes back:** Black challenges White's group-first morality and Green's destiny/acceptance, while Red shares individualism but rejects excessive calculation.
    - **Rejects:** If power must never serve the self, paying a cost is inherently corrupt, or dependence is always more virtuous than agency, mono-Black is a false center.

19. **Red (R) - freedom and feeling converted into immediate motion.**
    - **Resonates:** The person trusts emotion as information, acts before overthinking kills the moment, and values freedom from artificial restraint.
    - **Connects:** Rakdos publicizes Red's appetite, Gruul roots it in instinct, Izzet turns it into experiment, Boros disciplines it for protection, and Jund treats it as self-truth.
    - **Pushes back:** Red challenges White's security and Blue's deliberation, while Black can sharpen its self-interest and Green can root its intuition.
    - **Rejects:** If every variable must be approved, feeling is unreliable by definition, or safety always outranks the living moment, mono-Red is a false center.

20. **Green (G) - flourishing through acceptance, nature, and belonging.**
    - **Resonates:** The person trusts organic growth, old patterns, land, kinship, patience, and the living order more than redesign for its own sake.
    - **Connects:** Gruul makes Green defiant, Selesnya makes it communal, Golgari makes it cyclical, Simic tests its adaptability, and Naya makes abundance relational.
    - **Pushes back:** Green challenges Blue's nurture/engineering and Black's free-will domination, while White shares responsibility and Red shares intuition.
    - **Rejects:** If every limit must be engineered away, life and land are merely fuel, or belonging is only a constraint on individual will, mono-Green is a false center.

### Shards And Wedges

21. **Bant (WUG) - White-led honorable order inside a living community.**
    - **Resonates:** The person wants excellence, rank, protection, and refinement to remain visibly answerable to the whole community.
    - **Connects:** It shares procedure with Azorius, belonging with Selesnya, and refinement with Simic, while remaining a distinct Alaran shard centered on White-led public trust and responsible champions.
    - **Pushes back:** It resists Black-style private leverage and Red-style unsanctioned impulse; internally it must keep discipline open to correction.
    - **Rejects:** Generic value, denial control, hierarchy for its own sake, or a lone champion with no communal trust is not Bant.

22. **Esper (WUB) - Blue-led perfectibility through ordered, controlled design.**
    - **Resonates:** The person treats potential as a system to understand, coordinate, optimize, and deliberately rebuild.
    - **Connects:** It shares ordered procedure with Azorius, information leverage with Dimir, and control/obligation texture with Orzhov, while remaining a distinct Alaran shard centered on Blue-led perfectibility.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects Red impulse and Green acceptance as final answers; internally it must keep improvement from turning the unoptimized into an error to erase.
    - **Rejects:** Generic control, artifacts as decoration, or power without an applied-knowledge improvement project is not Esper.

23. **Grixis (UBR) - Black-led survival through analysis and urgent action.**
    - **Resonates:** The person names survival first, maps the weakness, creates leverage, and moves before a hostile opening closes.
    - **Connects:** It shares analysis with Dimir, pressure with Rakdos, and experiment/action texture with Izzet, while remaining a distinct Alaran shard centered on Black-led self-advocating survival.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects White moral/order permission and Green renewal/acceptance as governing constraints; internally it risks treating every weakness as exploitable.
    - **Rejects:** Generic evil, villain aesthetics, spellslinger, control, graveyards, or aggression are not Grixis without calculated survival and urgent leverage.

24. **Jund (BRG) - Red-led instinct, appetite, and consequence.**
    - **Resonates:** The person treats gut feeling as honest information, accepts appetite and consequence, and acts without polite permission.
    - **Connects:** It shares appetite with Rakdos, material survival with Golgari, and instinct with Gruul, while remaining a distinct Alaran shard centered on Red-led self-truth in motion.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects White order and Blue overthinking; internally it must distinguish honest instinct from overconsumption.
    - **Rejects:** Generic violence, monsters, sacrifice, devour, or competitive "Jund" play is not the shard identity without Red-led self-truth fed by Black appetite and Green instinct.

25. **Naya (RGW) - Green-led abundance, care, and instinctive belonging.**
    - **Resonates:** The person experiences life, creatures, place, scale, loyalty, and growth as membership in a larger living whole.
    - **Connects:** It shares instinct with Gruul, community with Selesnya, and urgent loyalty with Boros, while remaining a distinct Alaran shard centered on Green-led abundance and living relation.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects Blue control and Black extraction; internally it must keep abundance from becoming mere size or overgrowth.
    - **Rejects:** Big creatures, tokens, go-wide play, or nature aesthetics are not Naya without care, bond, place, and Green-centered belonging.

26. **Abzan Houses (WBG) - White-led endurance through family and ancestor duty.**
    - **Resonates:** The person values kinship, intergenerational protection, ancestor memory, defensive patience, and continuity that survives real cost.
    - **Connects:** It shares obligation with Orzhov, community with Selesnya, and continuity with Golgari, while remaining a distinct Tarkir clan centered on White-led house and ancestor duty.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects Red immediacy; internally it must keep inherited duty from becoming exclusion, rigidity, or obligation without consent.
    - **Rejects:** Toughness, counters, recursion, family language, or same-color goodstuff is not Abzan without White-led house continuity and ancestor responsibility.

27. **Temur Frontier (GUR) - Green-led survival through attunement and earned strength.**
    - **Resonates:** The person listens to terrain, body, instinct, elemental memory, and pattern before taking decisive action.
    - **Connects:** It shares instinct with Gruul, living-system attention with Simic, and perception/action texture with Izzet, while remaining a distinct Tarkir clan centered on Green-led attunement and survival.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects White imposed order and Black opportunism; internally it must keep hard-earned strength from becoming isolation or force without wisdom.
    - **Rejects:** Monsters, ferocity, elements, ramp, or same-color goodstuff is not Temur without Green-led attunement, mental fortitude, and action after listening.

28. **Sultai Brood (BGU) - Black-led opportunity through calculated resource conversion.**
    - **Resonates:** The person sees secrets, bodies, graveyards, costs, and openings as material that can be selectively converted into power.
    - **Connects:** It shares secrecy with Dimir, material-cycle texture with Golgari, and adaptation with Simic, while remaining a distinct Tarkir clan centered on Black-led ruthless opportunity and conversion.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects White moral order and Red impulsive release; internally it must stop calculation and ambition from becoming domination.
    - **Rejects:** Graveyard value, ramp, control, poison, or same-color goodstuff is not Sultai without Black-led opportunism and planned conversion.

29. **Mardu Horde (RWB) - Red-led speed bound to martial oath.**
    - **Resonates:** The person values total commitment, coordinated attack, war names, oaths, and taking the opening while it exists.
    - **Connects:** It shares formation with Boros, obligation with Orzhov, and appetite with Rakdos, while remaining a distinct Tarkir clan centered on Red-led speed under Horde code.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects Blue delay; internally it must keep speed from outrunning oath into conquest, cruelty, or overextension.
    - **Rejects:** Aggro, warriors, tokens, sacrifice, or same-color goodstuff is not Mardu without Red-led commitment disciplined by martial identity.

30. **Jeskai Way (URW) - Blue-led insight trained into responsible action.**
    - **Resonates:** The person values practice, breath, timing, cunning, precision, compassion, and action that has been trained before release.
    - **Connects:** It shares insight with Izzet, formal discipline with Azorius, and decisive action with Boros, while remaining a distinct Tarkir clan centered on Blue-led trained action and restraint.
    - **Pushes back:** It rejects Green acceptance; internally it must keep discipline from becoming empty delay that never serves the moment.
    - **Rejects:** Prowess, spells, monks, control, martial aesthetics, or same-color goodstuff is not Jeskai without Blue-led trained action and restraint.

### Four-Color Identities

31. **Yore / Artifice (WUBR, no Green) - agency through constructed intervention.**
    - **Resonates:** The person rebuilds systems, bodies, archives, contracts, or machines rather than accepting inherited limits as destiny.
    - **Connects:** Yore is near Esper's design, Grixis's refusal to be erased, Izzet's invention, and Orzhov's durable structure, but adds all four into engineered continuity.
    - **Pushes back:** Green asks Yore to respect cycles, organic belonging, and limits that may contain wisdom; Yore replies that nature is not the final blueprint.
    - **Rejects:** Generic artifacts, recursion, optimization, or four-color breadth is not Yore without a deliberate missing-Green commitment to constructed agency over natural acceptance.

32. **Glint / Chaos (UBRG, no White) - adaptive power without civic restraint.**
    - **Resonates:** The person learns, feeds, improvises, escalates, and stays alive in the storm without asking public order to authorize the outcome.
    - **Connects:** Glint touches Grixis leverage, Jund appetite, Temur vitality, and Sultai adaptation, but refuses to let any White-style common order stabilize them.
    - **Pushes back:** White asks who protects the public from appetite and power; Glint asks whether civic safety has become a cage around honest possibility.
    - **Rejects:** Chaos, cascade, value, or four-color goodstuff is not Glint without a deliberate missing-White rejection of peace-through-law, duty, and civic restraint.

33. **Dune / Aggression (BRGW, no Blue) - embodied presence claiming ground.**
    - **Resonates:** The person trusts bodies already moving, territorial solidarity, formation, multiplication, and pressure applied before contemplation cools the opening.
    - **Connects:** Dune touches Mardu's charge, Naya's bodies, Abzan's line, and Jund's appetite, but removes Blue's detached analysis from the common front.
    - **Pushes back:** Blue asks for distance, models, and scenario mapping; Dune answers that presence and coordinated motion can themselves establish truth.
    - **Rejects:** Tokens, combat, go-wide pressure, or four-color goodstuff is not Dune without a deliberate missing-Blue commitment to embodied action over detached contemplation.

34. **Ink / Altruism (RGWU, no Black) - abundance circulating through a defended commons.**
    - **Resonates:** The person wants resources, care, knowledge, memory, and prosperity to move through a community with consent and protection from capture.
    - **Connects:** Ink touches Selesnya belonging, Bant public trust, Naya abundance, and Blue open knowledge, but refuses Black-style private leverage as the center.
    - **Pushes back:** Black asks whether the individual may keep enough power to remain free; Ink asks when private accumulation breaks the living circuit of the commons.
    - **Rejects:** Group hug, friendliness, generosity, politics, or four-color goodstuff is not Ink without a deliberate missing-Black resistance to hoarding and self-first leverage.

35. **Witch / Growth (GWUB, no Red) - inevitability through patient cultivation.**
    - **Resonates:** The person protects small investments, compounds knowledge and resources, and trusts disciplined systems to become overwhelming over time.
    - **Connects:** Witch touches Abzan endurance, Esper planning, Bant structure, and Sultai ambition, but removes Red impulse from the engine.
    - **Pushes back:** Red asks whether the plan is still alive if it cannot answer the present moment; Witch asks why a momentary feeling should be allowed to burn the future.
    - **Rejects:** Counters, proliferate, control, value, or four-color goodstuff is not Witch without a deliberate missing-Red commitment to patient, calculated cultivation.

### Endpoints

36. **Colorless - deliberate outside-WUBRG constraint.**
    - **Resonates:** The person is drawn to strict non-color construction, precise mana distinctions, artifact machinery, Wastes austerity, alien scale, or an outside perspective kept in clearly separate branches.
    - **Connects:** It can resemble Yore through machinery or Blue through detachment, but it is not another color philosophy and does not inherit those value commitments automatically.
    - **Pushes back:** Every color asks which value guides the answer; Colorless asks whether the answer must be expressed in that value grammar at all.
    - **Rejects:** Generic artifacts, generic mana, Eldrazi, Devoid, Phyrexia, or lack of colored pips do not prove Colorless; superiority over WUBRG is an explicit false fit.

37. **Five-Color / WUBRG - every color given a meaningful voice.**
    - **Resonates:** The person wants coalition, synthesis, broad tools, and real negotiation among structure, knowledge, agency, motion, and belonging.
    - **Connects:** WUBRG brings all five color philosophies into one frame and omits none of them. That does not automatically make every guild, college, shard, wedge, four-color identity, or Colorless perspective present.
    - **Pushes back:** Each color must accept limits imposed by the other four: order must listen, knowledge must act, agency must coexist, freedom must take responsibility, and belonging must permit change.
    - **Rejects:** Generic goodstuff, access for its own sake, accumulation, superiority, or a deck where one color is decorative rather than meaningful is not the Five-Color synthesis.

## How To Use The List For One Person

Write a short evidence sentence before comparing identities:

> They seek **[experience/expression/improvement]**, find **[structure/meaning/both]** beautiful, prefer **[process]**, usually pursue advantage through **[deck behavior]**, and at the table they reliably **[observable conduct]**.

Then make four passes:

1. Mark identities with a real **Resonates** match supported by something the person repeatedly chooses or says.
2. Use **Connects** to distinguish near-neighbors that share colors or methods.
3. Treat **Pushes back** as a useful interview question, not an automatic exclusion.
4. Use **Rejects** to remove attractive-but-false matches based only on mechanics, aesthetics, a favorite commander, or a single game.

A useful final result is usually a relationship statement rather than a lone label:

> "Primary Grixis: survival first, analysis as leverage, and action before the opening closes. Strong Dimir connection through observation and selective disclosure. Izzet connection through fast iteration. Clear pushback against Azorius permission structures and Green acceptance. Not Rakdos, because spectacle and audience are not the point."

That statement explains similarity, adjacency, friction, and rejection without pretending to be a calibrated probability.
