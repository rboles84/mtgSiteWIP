# VM-551 — Gate B1 Real Placement Engine

Status: Complete with blocking evidence findings

## Objective

Implement the approved Gate B1 instrument as a deterministic, pure-testable 37-identity placement engine with honest ranked results, adaptive routing, guaranteed termination, bounded uncertainty, refinement recommendations, and exhaustive model reports.

## Authorized scope

- Versioned B1 engine source/model generated from the approved 16 constructs, 35 questions, 110 answers, 37 identities, 123 confusion pairs, and guarded lens contract.
- Pure observation, ranking, question selection, stopping, alternatives, refinement, and result finalization logic.
- Minimal production quick-reading adapter and additive Gate A result fields.
- Unit/property-style validation, generated journeys, reachability/pair/robustness/sensitivity/recovery reports, owner summary, Kanban, and handoff.

## Evidence boundary

- General answers remain bounded Commander observations and may establish structural relevance only.
- Only the 40 approved evidence-required directional uses may supply identity support or contradiction.
- Dependency groups count once; neutral/conditional/unknown answers never become positive identity evidence.
- `IDENTITY_LENS_SELF_REPORT` remains a separate secondary ledger and never changes behavioral ranking or creates a named result.
- Any identity lacking an approved path to primary is reported as a blocker rather than supplied with invented semantics.

## Protected boundaries

No question/answer rewriting, stable-ID changes, Atlas/profile placement evidence, dossier redesign, presentation/CSS work, visual QA, player validation, recruitment, shadow testing, migration, deployment, certification, push, or merge.

## Stop condition

Stop after one scoped clean local engine commit with exhaustive deterministic reports and a direct owner-readiness answer.

## Completed

- Added a versioned model builder and pure deterministic evidence engine with observation, ranking, routing, stopping, alternatives, refinement, replay, and Gate A-compatible result finalization.
- Preserved the approved 16 constructs, 35 behavioral questions, 110 answer contracts, 37 identities, 123 confusion pairs, stable IDs, dependency groups, 40 directional mapping hypotheses, and guarded lens contract.
