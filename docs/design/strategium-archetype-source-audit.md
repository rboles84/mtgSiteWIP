# Strategium Archetype — Source Audit

**Scope:** Verification of the 50 Commander deck archetypes listed on the Strategium page.
**Question:** Which are real / source-backed, and which (if any) are fabricated?
**Date:** 2026-06-23
**Verdict:** 50 of 50 are real and documented. **0 fabricated.**

---

## Bottom line

None of the 50 are made up. Every entry maps to a documented, recognized strategy. The reason no single canonical "list of 50" turns up in search is that each authority slices the archetype space at a different granularity — Draftsim publishes 27, EDHREC exposes 100+ theme tags, and MTG Fandom catalogs format-specific *named* decks. This list of 50 is effectively a merge of EDHREC's Commander theme taxonomy plus general strategy vocabulary, and that merge is sound.

## QA findings — overlaps (not errors)

Not fabrication issues — taxonomy issues. Several entries are parent/child or enabler/payoff pairs sharing a single axis. A purist taxonomy review would flag these:

- **Sacrifice ⊃ Aristocrats** — Aristocrats *is* the death-trigger-drain subtype of Sacrifice. Parent/child, not two peers.
- **Lands Matter ⊃ Landfall** — Landfall is the trigger-keyword subset of the broader Lands Matter shell.
- **Blink / Flicker ↔ ETB Value** — Blink is the enabler; ETB Value is the payoff. Near-duplicate axis.
- **Ramp ↔ Big Mana / Stompy** — Ramp is the accelerant; Stompy is what you do with it.
- **Theft / Copy ↔ Clones** — Clones is the copy-creature slice of Theft / Copy.
- **Reanimator ↔ Graveyard Value** — Reanimator is one finish-line inside the graveyard-value space.
- **Politics / Deals ↔ Forced Combat / Goad** — Goad is a political *tool*; the two overlap heavily.
- **Auras** — straddles **Voltron** and **Enchantress** depending on payoff.

## Naming notes (all correct as listed)

- **Typal** — correct modern term. WotC officially retired "Tribal" in 2023.
- **Infect / Toxic** — correctly merged. Toxic is the modern poison reprint of the Infect mechanic.
- **Pillow Fort** — spelled "Pillowfort" on MTG Fandom if exact-match sourcing is wanted.
- **Discover** — 2023 mechanic (*The Lost Caverns of Ixalan*); correct modern partner to Cascade.
