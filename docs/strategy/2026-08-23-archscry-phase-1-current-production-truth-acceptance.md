## Phase 2 Product-Contract Direction

Phase 2 is authorized to define a new Archscry product contract around three distinct roles:

- `self_reported_prior`
- `observed_gameplay_fit`
- reconciliation through agreement and disagreement

`self_reported_prior` is contextual information, not automatic placement evidence. It must not secretly award identity score or qualification.

Yore is a mandatory Phase 2 design and test case because it demonstrates a meaningful state where self-identification and behavior-only placement may not cleanly converge.

This authorization covers product-contract planning only. It does not start Phase 2 implementation and does not authorize self-identification UI.

## Freeze And Reopen Rules

Phase 1 is frozen for redesign planning. A different Phase 2 design does not reopen it.

Reopen Phase 1 only when at least one of these occurs:

- a newly discovered current product defect materially invalidates Phase 1 evidence;
- engine behavior changes;
- dossier authority materially changes;
- identity authority changes;
- new empirical/player evidence directly invalidates an accepted conclusion;
- an authorized future mapping or questionnaire change requires recertification.
