# White — Intra-Color Audit Report

## Executive Summary

White presents the most internally consistent identity in the WUBRG corpus. Its core drive of stability through imposed structure, its deontological ethics, and its mechanical signatures (board wipes, tokens, taxation, protection) align tightly across all three document layers—primer, metaphysics markdown, and metaphysics PDF. The only notable discrepancies are a minor Aggression rating variance between implied primer values and the explicit metaphysics matrix, and slight Vox Mana Read expansions across document tiers. No structural contradictions compromise White's design integrity.

## Intra-Color Consistency

**Document hierarchy**: Primer → Metaphysics → Schema is fully respected. The primer introduces White's collectivist deontology, the metaphysics markdown deepens it with axiological terminology and ludological matrix ratings, and the PDF condenses it into a reference card. The system-level documents (06, 07, 08) reference White consistently in comparative contexts.

**Core Drive alignment**: All three tiers agree on stability, structure, and protection as White's foundational motivation. The metaphysics markdown uses more academic language ("systemic stability," "structural predictability") but the semantic content is identical.

**Ethical framework alignment**: The primer identifies White's dual consequentialist/deontological tension. The metaphysics markdown reinforces this through its axiological layer. The Color Pie Framework (06) classifies White as "Collectivist Deontology." All are consistent.

**Mechanical alignment**: Board wipes, tokens, protection, and taxation are identified as White's four strategic pillars in every document, with the metaphysics markdown providing the most detailed structural analysis (naming them Symmetrical Reset, Modular Army Scaling, Proactive Systemic Taxation, Absolute Exclusion).

## Mechanical Analysis

White's four strategic pillars are structurally consistent across all documents:

**Symmetrical Reset (Board Wipes)**: Primer cites Wrath of God, Day of Judgment, Doomskar. Metaphysics MD cites Wrath of God, Farewell. No mechanical conflict; examples are complementary.

**Token Lattices (Modular Army Scaling)**: Primer cites Raise the Alarm, Battle Screech, Intangible Virtue. Metaphysics MD describes cooperative scaling with static anthems. Consistent framing of collective over individual.

**Taxation/Stax**: Primer cites Thalia, Leonin Arbiter, Rule of Law. Metaphysics MD cites Thalia, Smothering Tithe. Smothering Tithe also appears in the Commander evolution document (08), where it is identified as White's most impactful catch-up engine. All aligned.

**Protection/Exile**: Primer cites Teferi's Protection, Guardian Angel, Unbreakable Formation. Metaphysics MD cites Swords to Plowshares, Leyline Binding. The metaphysics markdown explicitly distinguishes exile from destruction—a nuance absent from the primer but not contradictory.

## Metaphysical Analysis

White's axiological structure is internally coherent:

**Peace as supreme good**: Consistent across all layers. Primer frames it as "peace is the ultimate state of existence"; metaphysics MD frames it as "the supreme good is peace, achieved exclusively through systematic order."

**The Ego vs. Ecosystem conflict**: The metaphysics MD introduces this framing explicitly. The primer uses different language ("Collective Over the Individual") but conveys the same tension. The PDF does not address this at its summary level, which is expected.

**Dogmatism as weakness**: All documents identify inflexibility as White's core vulnerability. The primer, Color Pie Framework, and metaphysics MD all agree that rigid rules prevent adaptation.

## Issues and Flags

| Issue | Type | Severity | Details |
|-------|------|----------|---------|
| Aggression rating variance | Formatting mismatch | 2 | Primer summary table implies low aggression ("Control, tokens, protection, taxes"); metaphysics MD rates Aggression at 5/10. The primer does not provide an explicit numeric rating, so this is an inference gap rather than a contradiction. |
| Vox Mana expansion | Terminological variance | 1 | Metaphysics MD extends the Vox Mana Read with additional sentences. The core statement matches the primer and PDF. The expansion adds nuance without altering meaning. |
| Commander evolution framing | Missing element | 1 | The metaphysics MD does not discuss Commander-format adaptations (catch-up draw, Smothering Tithe). This content lives in the primer (01) and system document (08). Not a contradiction—expected scope separation. |

---

## Recommendations

1. **Standardize Vox Mana Read**: Choose either the short-form (primer/PDF) or long-form (metaphysics MD) as canonical and mark the other as variant. Both are valid; consistency aids downstream use.

2. **Explicitly state ludological ratings in the primer**: The primer uses qualitative descriptions but omits the numeric matrix present in the metaphysics MD. Adding the matrix to the primer would eliminate inference gaps.

3. **Cross-reference Commander adaptations**: The metaphysics MD could include a brief note pointing to the Ludic Evolution document for format-specific analysis, maintaining its scope while acknowledging the evolution.
