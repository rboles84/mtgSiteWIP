# VM-565 Curated Player Vocabulary Education — Owner Report

Status: **OWNER REVIEW**. Deterministic audit status: **DETERMINISTIC_AUDIT_PASS**.

## Executive summary

VM-565 reviewed **449 candidate expressions** across all **37 identities**: 259 strategy-label occurrences plus 190 independently discovered vocabulary decisions. It accepted **42 education actions** and deliberately left **148 candidates** undecorated.

The implementation adds **23 canonical definitions**, **14 aliases**, and **34 new exact teaching targets**. It reuses approved definitions for **7 better teaching-location selections**, for **41 total exact target entries**. One definition was corrected: Mana rocks now teaches what the artifact is, rather than repeating the generic Ramp definition.

The current all-37 result contains **202 single-concept teaching allocations**, with **0 unresolved targets**, **0 duplicate canonical teachings**, and **0 blanket text-only surface allocations**.

The result intentionally does not chase glossary utilization or whole-phrase coverage. Compound labels such as Protective Tokens remain undecorated when their useful component concepts are already taught, and Vox Mana editorial labels remain prose.

The useful outcome is qualitative: terms such as Dredge, Impulse draw, Enchantress, Group Hug, Politics, Treasure, Typal, and colorless mana now have one intentional teaching moment where the player is choosing or interpreting a deck direction.
