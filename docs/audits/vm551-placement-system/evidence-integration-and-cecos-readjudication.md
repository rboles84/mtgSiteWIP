`cecos-conclusion-adjudication.csv` classifies every major conclusion as implementation-derived, CECOS-confirmed, CECOS-revised, CECOS-withdrawn, or unresolved. ## Draft.4 boundaries applied

- CECOS governs corpus evidence before conversion into questions, scoring, placement, copy, or recommendations.
- Archscry scores/placements/recommendations are derived product outputs, not corpus classifications or evidence.
- A corpus observation cannot directly become an identity label, stable trait, motive, emotional state, deck behavior, or predicted action.
- Direct, directly entailed, context-dependent, and unresolved support must remain distinct.
- Unknown, mixed, contradictory, insufficient, and limitation states cannot be silently forced into a named result.
- Player-language frequency is not prevalence; repeated wording is not independent confirmation.
- Question prompts and closed answers are product-authored instruments, not player language.
- Derived summaries/claims need identified inputs, method, counterevidence, uncertainty, version, and review state.
- Numeric probability requires a separately approved calibration method; draft.4 itself does not define an Archscry confidence model.

## Evidence-integration matrix

`evidence-integration-matrix.csv` covers CRIT-001, Apocrypha, CECOS draft.4, Strategium, Magic Math, tutor/combo research, Partner research, current questionnaire answers, runtime model observations, and future player-pilot evidence across fourteen uses.

Key rulings:

- CRIT-001 is direct authority for certified identity definitions/boundaries, not questionnaire validity or prevalence.
- CECOS draft.4 is direct authority for corpus/provenance/claim boundaries and supporting methodology for derived scoring/confidence/adjacency contracts; it does not define those product algorithms.
- Apocrypha can expose evidence roles but is prohibited as direct scoring/confidence/adjacency authority.
- Strategium is hypothesis-only or prohibited direct use for scoring/confidence/public factual claims.
- Tutor/combo evidence may challenge stereotypes and wording; tutor/combo use cannot directly assign motive, competitiveness, or identity.
- Current questionnaire answers are product-authored hypotheses and prohibited as direct public factual/recommendation authority.
- Runtime observations directly establish what the implementation does, not whether its semantics are correct.
- Future pilot evidence can support bounded comprehension/confusion/reliability findings but not population prevalence without representative sampling.

Every classification concerning scoring, confidence, adjacency, recommendations, or public claims contains its explanation in the CSV.

## Hearthhull reconciliation

The rejected audit used web browsing to obtain an official Edge of Eternities release-note rule statement. No exact local copy, source record, or hash was preserved as a VM-551 input. That was a process and authority violation.

The external source and the specific statement that Hearthhull is currently legal are withdrawn from VM-551 authority. This remediation does not browse and makes no independent legality determination.

The locally demonstrable conclusion is narrower:

- the committed recommendation corpus contains `Hearthhull, the Worldseed`;
- the committed local Commander index does not contain it;
- the local detector documents that it is a bounded practical detector rather than a complete solver for commander-enabling rules.

Therefore the recommendation’s specific legality is unresolved inside this local-authority audit, and the pipeline needs an explicit unresolved/special-rule status. The generic detector/recommendation conflict remains; the external legality proof does not.

## Independent review and certification wording

Draft.4 directly requires independent review and release validation for governed corpus artifacts. A later Archscry implementation candidate also needs independent exact-SHA product review under Vox Mana governance, but that must not be mislabeled as CECOS corpus certification. Production certification remains a separate owner-authorized gate after implementation, review, source/generated reconciliation, and deployed-truth validation.
