## Executive verdict

Vox Mana implements a substantial and deterministic 37-identity placement experience, but it is not yet trustworthy as an accuracy claim. All identities are reachable and the recovered CRIT-001 semantics are present in generated records; however, the live question-to-score contract is a separately authored heuristic bank with no answer-level provenance, no enforced minimum affirmative evidence, no calibrated confidence, and no meaningful adjacency contract.

The dominant issue is not “copy.” It is a chain-of-evidence defect:

```text
forced abstract answer
  -> unproven multi-identity effects
  -> additive score + suppression
  -> softmax share labeled confidence
  -> numeric rank labeled primary/adjacent
  -> broad behavioral and Commander claims
```

Copy, recommendations, and presentation amplify that chain, but they do not originate it. Public accuracy validation should not begin until Gate A trust blockers are repaired and independently reviewed.

## Required conclusions

### What placement model is actually implemented?

A deterministic adaptive weighted-scoring classifier: equal initial log scores, nearest-table likelihood-to-delta conversion, positive effects, suppressions, lateral inhibition, softmax normalization, adaptive Hall/Crucible selection, and rank-based serialization.

### Is it legitimately Bayesian?

No. The repository does not establish that likelihoods are conditional probabilities, that deltas are log likelihood ratios, that dependencies are modeled, or that the softmax share is calibrated. The model should be described as adaptive weighted scoring unless and until a Bayesian contract is implemented and validated.

### Are all 37 identities reachable and comparably represented?

All 37 are primary- and rank-two-reachable across 26,891 exhaustive valid terminal paths. Thirty-six are rank-three-reachable; Colorless is not. They are not comparably represented: primary share ranges 8.22% to 0.62% and support opportunity ranges 59 to 8 answers. The rejected audit's claim that all 37 could win with zero direct positive evidence was caused by an invalid evidence-trail field lookup and is withdrawn. Corrected analysis finds zero genuinely negative-only winners but 2,901 primary paths below the generated minimum-hit metadata under the explicit strong-authored-hit proxy.

### Which questions or answers create bias?

The four mandatory Gate questions are the largest structural bias. A Gate answer positively affects 21.3 identities and suppresses 12.75 identities on average; 16 of 20 support more than five identities. The metaphorical answers blend social, psychographic, philosophical, and aesthetic preference, then the builder propagates color loadings into identity effects. Hall bias follows current top-five rank, so Gate effects determine which targeted evidence is even asked. All 356 answers suppress identities, and none supports neutral/mixed/unsure.

### Are primary placements trustworthy?

Not as public accuracy claims. Golden paths and reachability pass, but 2,901 primary paths fall below the generated minimum-hit metadata under the strong-authored-hit proxy, exact ties silently default by identity key, free-text false-positive contracts are inert, one-answer matched sensitivity is 32.7781%, and opportunity is unequal. The result may still feel useful as an editorial exploration heuristic, but that is a lower and more honest claim.

### Are adjacent placements meaningful?

Not reliably. `adjacent_matches` is exactly numeric ranks two and three. It does not encode philosophical, behavioral, color, mechanical, or family adjacency. The representative runtime labeled a 1% runner-up adjacent and simultaneously called it strong and emerging.

### Is confidence calibrated or decorative?

Decorative/unvalidated. It is the top softmax share of arbitrary additive scores, with gap-derived stopping and prose bands. It does not measure evidence authority, amount, agreement, distinctiveness, stability, or empirical correctness. Legacy compatibility additionally fabricates 0.66/0.6 when confidence is missing.

### Which public claims are unsupported?

Every live answer-to-signal effect lacks a claim/source reference. The highest-risk unsupported public claims are causal personality/motivation statements, generalized deck behavior, opponent/table perception stated as fact, calibrated-sounding percentages, and adjacent explanations without independent support. Raw identity claims are often well sourced, but they do not automatically prove player behavior.

### How much placement copy is genuinely identity-specific?

All 37 dossiers contain identity-specific content and no normalized exact duplicate was found. Yet decree structure is highly shared: 609 of 666 identity pairs exceed 0.65 token Jaccard after identity-name normalization. The deeper full-dossier text is more distinct. The larger defect is claim entailment and repeated structure, not literal identity-name substitution alone.

### Which recommendations are evidence-backed?

Color-identity compatibility is strongest: 82 locally indexed commander candidates all matched their identity colors. Behavioral/strategic fit is editorially grounded in identity records and taxonomy, not selected-answer evidence. Popularity is not systematically backed. The committed recommendation corpus contains Hearthhull while the local Commander index does not; the specific legality claim is unresolved inside this local-authority audit because the rejected audit obtained its rule proof through prohibited web browsing. Eighteen deck links also flow through the commander-recommendation collection.

### What are the highest-risk pipeline defects?

1. Live Hall/Crucible effects are hard-coded separately from canonical recovered evidence contracts.
2. No answer-level provenance, stable IDs for 336 answers, or controlled signal schema.
3. Recovered minimum-hit/guardrail semantics are inert at runtime.
4. Weak placement-model schema and multiple manual authorities permit drift.
5. Result provenance identifies a model version, not exact canonical input hashes.
6. Recommendation legality uses a stale, intentionally incomplete detector.

### What must be fixed before broader public validation?

Gate A: honest model terminology, canonical evidence/signal contracts, enforced affirmative evidence and unknown/tie states, meaningful adjacency, calibrated or nonnumeric confidence, and source/generated validation. The decision-critical Gate B work must then pass all-37 opportunity, sensitivity, edge-identity, and claim-entailment gates.

### What can wait until after MVP user testing?

Lower-value template diversification, long-term schema ergonomics, full historical-result migration, deep hardening automation, minor mobile tab affordance polish, and nonessential dossier repetition can wait. Uncalibrated percentages, zero-evidence winners, hidden tie defaults, forced directional answers, and unsupported public claims cannot.

### What should the implementation sequence be?

Gate A trust blockers -> Gate B MVP correctness -> Gate C interpretation quality -> Gate D hardening. Detailed requirements and validation gates are in `requirements-specification.md`. This audit does not authorize implementation planning or work.

## Evidence limitations

- Exhaustive enumeration covers every path reachable under current question-selection code, not every arbitrary sequence of all 113 questions.
- Winner-share distribution is a model property, not a population prevalence estimate.
- The dimension classifier in the question matrix is transparent audit routing and requires owner/editor review.
- Raw-claim official/editorial classification uses recorded source metadata; it does not supersede claim-level source review.
- Recommendation currentness is bounded by the committed 2026-05 Scryfall snapshot. The rejected audit's focused web rule check is recorded as a process violation and removed as VM-551 authority.
- Browser inspection covered one representative WUBRG primary, two adjacent options, primary-to-adjacent transition, desktop/mobile layouts, refresh/direct/partial state, metadata, headings, keyboard activation, overflow, and console logs. All 37 copy surfaces were generated and compared offscreen; 37 interactive browser runs were unnecessary because there are no 37 standalone routes.
- The existing Archscry visual compare has no committed baselines, and the aggregate test requires an absent raw Scryfall bulk file; both are recorded as validation limitations rather than repaired here.
- No empirical player-response/correctness corpus exists in scope, so placement accuracy and confidence calibration cannot be certified.
- CECOS authority is the exact draft.4 Git object and checksum in `audit-input-authority.md`. Draft.4 governs corpus/derivation boundaries; it does not itself define Archscry scoring or certify this product audit.
