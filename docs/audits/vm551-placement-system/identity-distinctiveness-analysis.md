## Shards and wedges

Shard/wedge records contain certified center-color, setting, and boundary semantics, but many current answer prompts expose adjacent color philosophy, mechanics, mood, or lore anchors rather than the full discriminator cluster. Their nearest combinatorial competitors are often mono-colors, four-color expressions, or colleges rather than the structurally expected shard/wedge neighbors. This demonstrates that numeric rank proximity and conceptual family proximity diverge.

The first pilot should not treat a shard/wedge name or same-color mechanic as evidence. It must test the full center-color/behavior boundary and permit an insufficient result when the cluster is absent.

## Similar gameplay and philosophy boundaries

Similar gameplay cannot establish the same philosophy: sacrifice may express Orzhov debt, Golgari reclamation, Witherbloom essence craft, or Black resource conversion. Conversely, similar philosophy does not establish the same Commander expression: community protection can appear in White, Selesnya, Bant, Naya, Ink, or Boros through different deck and social behaviors.

The placement contract therefore needs two independent layers:

1. a bounded observed preference/behavior construct;
2. a reviewed identity discriminator explaining why that construct favors one identity over its closest competitors.

Commander recommendations remain a third, downstream layer and cannot be used to make the identity distinction true.
