Official material also treats Ravnica guilds, Alara shards, and Tarkir clans as separate faction expressions. The shared colors and themes support comparison, not literal composition.

> Replace "joins/combines [guilds]" with "shares bounded [theme] texture with [guild], while remaining a distinct [Alara shard/Tarkir clan]."

### Finding B - WUBRG claims automatic containment of all identities

Severity: Medium documentation scope overreach.

Affected claim: `VM554-37-03`.

Current wording:

> WUBRG can contain the questions of every preceding identity, but its distinctive demand is that none of the five colors be treated as the complete answer.

Why it fails:

- No official source establishes WUBRG as a personality doctrine containing every faction identity.
- Upstream WUBRG boundaries explicitly say a guild or college overlap is not Five-Color unless all five colors remain present and meaningful.
- Colorless is one of the preceding 36 guide entries and is definitionally not contained by WUBRG totality.

Recommended replacement:

> WUBRG brings all five color philosophies into one frame and omits none of them. That does not automatically make every guild, college, shard, wedge, four-color identity, or Colorless perspective present.

Responsible layer: relationship guide only. The upstream WUBRG center and boundary records remain sound.
