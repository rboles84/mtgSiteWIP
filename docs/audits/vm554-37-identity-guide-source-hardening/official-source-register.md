# VM-554 Official Source Register

## Scope Rule

Only official Wizards of the Coast pages are admitted as external corroboration in VM-554. The supplied Player Atlas, local PDF captures, community summaries, search-result prose, and deck-statistics sites are excluded as audit authority.

Official material does not silently override the certified Vox Mana source hierarchy. It is used to determine whether a guide statement is official-direct, a conservative synthesis, a clearly labeled project synthesis, or in conflict with upstream semantics.

## Registered Sources

### OFF-COLOR-INDEX - Let's Talk Color Pie

- Source: [Let's Talk Color Pie](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/lets-talk-color-pie), Mark Rosewater, 2021.
- Audit use: official index tying the five mono-color philosophies, ten color-pair philosophies, ten Ravnica guilds, five center-color shard articles, five wedge articles, ally-color relationships, and enemy-color conflicts into one source map.
- Supports: using color philosophy as a broad relationship grammar; treating shards as center-color lenses; treating wedges as three-color interactions.
- Does not support: a complete official 37-player taxonomy, Vox Mana placement claims, WUBRG personality, or automatic identity-to-identity adjacency.

### OFF-PIE-FIGHTS - Pie Fights

- Source: [Pie Fights](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/pie-fights-2016-11-14), Mark Rosewater, 2016.
- Audit use: direct mono-color goals and enemy conflicts.
- Supports: White/peace and structure, Blue/perfection and deliberate change, Black/power and individual choice, Red/freedom and action, Green/growth/acceptance and natural systems; W/B group-individual, U/R deliberation-action, B/G choice-destiny, R/W freedom-security, and G/U nature-nurture tensions.
- Does not support: guild, college, shard, wedge, or player placement by color alone.

### OFF-ALLIED-COLORS - Thank You for Being a Friend

- Source: [Thank You for Being a Friend](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/thank-you-being-friend-2017-03-20), Mark Rosewater, 2017.
- Audit use: direct ally-color common ground and residual disagreements.
- Supports: bounded mono-color `Connects` synthesis.
- Does not support: treating an allied color pair as synonymous with one Ravnica guild.

### OFF-CITY-HIGHLIGHTS - City Highlights, Part 2

- Source: [City Highlights, Part 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/city-highlights-part-2), Mark Rosewater, 2023.
- Audit use: direct boundary that a Ravnica guild is one factional expression of a two-color pair and that multiple philosophical expressions of the same colors are possible.
- Supports: preserving guild/college differentiation and rejecting mechanics-only identity proof.
- Critical boundary: a guild is not the whole color pair. The guide must not treat Ravnica guilds as literal ingredients of Alara shards or Tarkir clans.

### OFF-STRIXHAVEN-DISTINCTION - In the Strixhaven, Part 2

- Source: [In the Strixhaven, Part 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/strixhaven-part-2-2021-04-05), Mark Rosewater, 2021.
- Audit use: direct design requirement that each college differ from the same-color Ravnica guild and be built around the conflict of its two colors rather than the guild's overlap model.
- Supports: the guide's Izzet/Prismari, Golgari/Witherbloom, Boros/Lorehold, Simic/Quandrix, and Orzhov/Silverquill discrimination.
- Does not support: using identical colors as proof that a guild and college are near-identical.

### OFF-STRIXHAVEN-GUIDE - Planeswalker's Guide to Strixhaven

- Source: [Planeswalker's Guide to Strixhaven](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-strixhaven-2021-04-01), Doug Beyer and Ari Zirulnik, 2021.
- Audit use: direct creative centers and internal color tensions for the five colleges.
- Supports: language/eloquence for Silverquill, history/archaeomancy for Lorehold, and the corresponding documented centers for Prismari, Witherbloom, and Quandrix.
- Does not support: generalizing those colleges into complete player personalities.

### OFF-THREE-COLOR - Exploring Wedges and the official color-pie index

- Sources: [Exploring Wedges](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/exploring-wedges-2014-08-27), Marshall Sutcliffe, 2014; [Let's Talk Color Pie](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/lets-talk-color-pie), Mark Rosewater, 2021.
- Audit use: direct core-color construction for wedges, the official WBG/URW/BGU/RWB/GUR clan order, clan centers such as Abzan endurance and Mardu speed, shard missing-color framing, and center-color shard/wedge article structure.
- Supports: the ten guide center/title rows when read as bounded Vox Mana paraphrases of certified sources.
- Critical boundary: official material treats clans, shards, and guilds as distinct faction expressions. It does not say Bant is made from Azorius/Selesnya/Simic or that Mardu is made from Boros/Orzhov/Rakdos.

### OFF-CMD2016-FOUR-COLOR - Designing Commander (2016 Edition)

- Source: [Designing Commander (2016 Edition)](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-preview/designing-commander-2016-edition-2016-10-24), Ethan Fleischer, 2016.
- Audit use: direct official four-color faction frame and missing-color contrast.
- Supports: WUBR Artifice versus Green, UBRG Chaos versus White, BRGW Aggression versus Blue, RGWU Altruism versus Black, and GWUB Growth versus Red.
- Boundary: the official source presents product/faction design themes. Vox Mana's metaphysical extensions and the names Yore, Glint, Dune, Ink, and Witch remain project synthesis unless separately sourced.
- Result: no upstream contradiction found in the five missing-color centers; the certified Vox Mana records clearly mark their expanded metaphysics as project synthesis.

### OFF-COLORLESS - Official Colorless Boundaries

- Sources: [Just the Artifacts, Ma'am](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/just-artifacts-maam-2005-02-28), Mark Rosewater, 2005; [Catching Up on Commander Changes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/catching-commander-changes-2017-08-15), Adam Styborski, 2017; [Oath of the Gatewatch Mechanics](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/oath-gatewatch-mechanics-2015-12-28), Matt Tabak, 2015.
- Audit use: direct separation of colorless from a sixth color, artifacts from a universal colorless philosophy, colorless mana from generic mana, and strict colorless Commander construction.
- Supports: the guide's rejection boundaries and its outside-WUBRG framing when explicitly labeled as Vox Mana's placement interpretation.
- Does not support: a unified official Colorless personality, Colorless superiority, or the positive Yore/Blue neighbor comparison.

### NONE-WUBRG-PHILOSOPHY-NOT-OFFICIALLY-ESTABLISHED

- Search result: no official Wizards source located that establishes Five-Color/WUBRG as a personality or as "negotiation among all five philosophies."
- Officially supportable floor: Magic has five colors with distinct philosophies; five-color mechanics and decks can require/access all five colors.
- Project-only extension: every color having a meaningful voice, coalition, synthesis, and negotiation are Vox Mana interpretations recorded upstream.
- Guide defect: "WUBRG can contain the questions of every preceding identity" exceeds both the official floor and the upstream WUBRG boundary. It must be narrowed to the five color philosophies and must not imply automatic containment of every guild, college, shard, wedge, four-color identity, or Colorless.

## External Corroboration Verdict

- No official evidence conflict requires an upstream Vox Mana semantic remediation in this audit.
- The official record reinforces the source hierarchy's existing warnings: colors are not destiny, a faction is one expression of its colors, four-color product themes are not complete metaphysical doctrine, Colorless is not a sixth color, and WUBRG has no official personality doctrine.
- Official evidence supports guide-level correction of the ten shard/wedge `Connects` phrasings and the WUBRG `Connects` overstatement; it does not justify changing their certified upstream centers.
