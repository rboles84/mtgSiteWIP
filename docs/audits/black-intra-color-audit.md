# Black — Intra-Color Audit Report

## Executive Summary

Black exhibits strong internal consistency with one notable philosophical tension: its Vox Mana Read shifts emphasis between documents. The primer frames Black's core axiom as active seizure of power ("Power is not given. It is taken."), while the metaphysics markdown and PDF frame it as willingness to pay costs ("You are willing to pay any cost…"). Both readings are valid facets of Black's transactional egoism, but the tonal difference—assertive conquest vs. calculated transaction—warrants editorial harmonization. All other canonical elements (core drive, mechanical signatures, metaphysical axioms) align strongly across the three-tier document hierarchy.

## Intra-Color Consistency

**Document hierarchy**: Primer → Metaphysics → Schema is followed, but with tonal variance. The primer's "Sovereign of Ambition" subtitle emphasizes dominion and conquest. The metaphysics MD's "Wielder of Power" subtitle is more neutral. The philosophical content remains consistent beneath these framing differences.

**Core Drive alignment**: All three sources agree that Black's drive centers on power and individual autonomy. The primer adds "sacrifice" as a method (which is mechanical), while the metaphysics MD adds "self-sovereignty" (which is philosophical). These are complementary.

**Transactional philosophy**: The primer's "Everything Has a Price" section and the metaphysics MD's "Axiom of Cost" are semantically identical. Both frame Black's worldview as fundamentally transactional. The PDF's "You take what you need—morality is optional" introduces a slightly more aggressive framing that leans toward seizure rather than fair exchange, creating a minor tension with the transaction model.

**"Honest villain" framing**: The primer devotes a full section ("The Honest Villain") to arguing Black is not inherently evil. The metaphysics MD supports this through its existential nihilism framing. The PDF omits this nuance at its summary level. Consistent where present.

## Mechanical Analysis

**Graveyard Recursion**: Primer cites Reanimate, Animate Dead, Living Death, Victimize. Metaphysics MD cites Reanimate, Living Death. Both frame reanimation as "subversion of death" / "defying death." Aligned.

**Life-as-Resource**: Primer cites Necropotence, Dark Confidant, Phyrexian Arena, Bolas's Citadel. Metaphysics MD cites Necropotence, Peer into Abyss. Both sources agree this is Black's defining mechanical trait. Aligned.

**Sacrifice Engines**: Primer cites Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Altar, Yawgmoth, Culling the Weak. Metaphysics MD cites Viscera Seer, Ashnod's Altar. Minor example variation; mechanical identity consistent.

**Tutors**: Primer cites Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Diabolic Intent, Imperial Seal. Metaphysics MD cites Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor. Both frame tutoring as "eliminating randomness" / "deterministic certainty." Aligned.

**Removal**: Primer cites Murder, Hero's Downfall, Deadly Rollick, Toxic Deluge. Metaphysics MD does not have a dedicated removal pillar—it subsumes removal into the broader framework. The PDF lists removal as implicit. No conflict, but structural asymmetry between documents.

## Metaphysical Analysis

**Existential nihilism**: The metaphysics MD introduces explicit nihilistic framing ("no inherent moral laws, no divine mandates"). The primer frames this as "rejection of external morality" without using the term nihilism. The Color Pie Framework (06) labels Black's paradigm as "Transactional Egoism." All three descriptions are compatible—nihilism is the ontological foundation, egoism is the ethical expression, transactionalism is the operational method.

**Zero-sum worldview**: The primer explicitly states "Black sees Magic as fundamentally zero-sum." The metaphysics MD implies this through its predator/prey language. Consistent.

**Meritocracy of power**: Primer section "Meritocracy Through Power" aligns with the metaphysics MD's framing of cost-willingness as the sole determinant of worthiness. Consistent.

## Recommendations

1. **Harmonize the Vox Mana Read**: Decide whether Black's canonical axiom emphasizes seizure ("Power is not given. It is taken.") or transaction ("You are willing to pay any cost…"). Both are valid, but one should be primary and the other acknowledged as variant. The transactional version better aligns with the overall "everything has a price" philosophy.

2. **Standardize subtitles**: Choose either "Sovereign of Ambition" or "Wielder of Power" as canonical. "Sovereign of Ambition" is more evocative and distinctly Black; "Wielder of Power" is more neutral.

3. **Align PDF Belief statement**: "You take what you need—morality is optional" introduces a blunter register than other sources. Consider revising to match the transactional framing or keeping it as a deliberate variant for the summary format.
