# Blue — Intra-Color Audit Report

## Executive Summary

Blue demonstrates the strongest internal consistency of any color in the WUBRG corpus. Its core drive of knowledge-based control, its tabula rasa epistemology, and its mechanical signatures (card draw, counterspells, bounce, cloning) are uniform across all document tiers. The Vox Mana Read is semantically identical across primer and PDF, with the metaphysics markdown expanding it into a richer second-person narrative without semantic drift. No contradictions were detected. Blue serves as the benchmark for intra-color coherence.

## Intra-Color Consistency

**Document hierarchy**: Primer → Metaphysics → Schema is cleanly followed. The primer provides the philosophical framework with mechanical examples; the metaphysics markdown adds epistemological depth and the ludological matrix; the PDF distills to a reference card. No hierarchy violations.

**Core Drive alignment**: Perfect across all tiers. "Knowledge" and "control through understanding" appear verbatim in primer, PDF, and as the semantic core of the metaphysics markdown's more expansive language.

**Tabula rasa doctrine**: The primer explicitly names Blue's "tabula rasa" position; the metaphysics MD labels it "The Axiom of Blank Slate (Tabula Rasa)"; the Color Pie Framework (06) classifies Blue as "Rationalist Tabula Rasa." Complete alignment.

**Strategic pillars**: The four pillars map one-to-one across documents:
- Card draw engines = Architecture of Information
- Counterspells = Causal Negation
- Bounce/tempo = Temporal Disruption
- Clone/copy = Structural Replication

## Mechanical Analysis

**Card Draw**: Primer cites Brainstorm, Ponder, Rhystic Study, Consecrated Sphinx. Metaphysics MD cites Brainstorm, Rhystic Study. Overlap with no conflict; primer provides broader example set.

**Counterspells**: Primer cites Counterspell, Force of Will, Mana Drain, Archmage's Charm. Metaphysics MD cites Counterspell, Force of Will. Consistent subset relationship.

**Bounce/Tempo**: Primer cites Cyclonic Rift, Unsummon, Brazen Borrower, Aether Gust. Metaphysics MD cites Cyclonic Rift, Snap. Consistent.

**Cloning**: Primer cites Clone, Clever Impersonator, Metallurgic Summonings, Mechanized Production. Metaphysics MD cites Twincast, Phantasmal Image. Different examples, same mechanical category. No conflict.

**Deck Manipulation**: The primer treats scrying/deck manipulation as a separate fifth pillar. The metaphysics MD subsumes this under the card draw pillar ("filtering out entropic variables"). This is a structural organization difference, not a contradiction—the mechanics are acknowledged in both.

## Metaphysical Analysis

**Epistemological framework**: Blue's metaphysics are internally the most rigorous in the corpus. The metaphysics MD explicitly frames Blue through an epistemological lens (knowledge-as-power) rather than axiological (values-based) or ontological (nature-of-being) lenses. This is consistent with Blue's own philosophical identity—it would describe itself through epistemology.

**The omniscience ideal**: Both primer ("The Hubris of Omniscience") and metaphysics MD ("the ultimate sin is ignorance; the ultimate virtue is total comprehension") frame omniscience as Blue's aspirational endpoint and its potential downfall. Perfect alignment.

**Anti-determinism**: Blue's rejection of predetermined destiny is consistent across all layers. The primer frames it through "The Rejection of Nature" (vs. Green); the metaphysics MD frames it through the Tabula Rasa axiom. Both arrive at the same conclusion: potential is unlimited.
