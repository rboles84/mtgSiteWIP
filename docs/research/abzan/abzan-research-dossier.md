# Abzan Research Dossier

Status: VM-200 source-bound dossier. Abzan remains non-live and review-gated.

## Approved Floor

Abzan is the White-Black-Green Tarkir wedge with White as its center. The approved design source frames Abzan around endurance, survival, defensive posture, answers, long-game planning, and growth that arrives after the clan has survived what opponents spend trying to kill it. See ABZAN-EVID-001 through ABZAN-EVID-003.

Khans-era Abzan Houses are a family, duty, trust, desert-survival, kin-tree, ancestor-spirit, and scale-symbol society. The clan is built from interconnected families, social categories such as blood-kin and bond-kin, a khan selected for defense and war, and roles such as spirit speakers, tree wardens, krumar, dragonscale infantry, and ibex cavalry. See ABZAN-EVID-004 through ABZAN-EVID-008.

Modern Dragonstorm Abzan revives Abzan Houses after Dromoka's suppression. The Council of Houses, Felothar of House Zanhar, the five major houses, perennation, Kin-Tree practices, defensive military, trade, arts, earthcarving, spirit summoning, dragonstorm adaptation, and modern locations are source-bound. See ABZAN-EVID-014 through ABZAN-EVID-025.

## Dromoka Boundary

Dromoka's brood is not Abzan Houses. It is a WG dragon clan and a suppression/contrast source for Abzan revival. Dromoka-era evidence can establish ancestor-magic prohibition, hidden kin trees, and Anafenza's execution for kin-tree worship, but it cannot be used to collapse Dromoka culture into Abzan Houses. See ABZAN-EVID-010 through ABZAN-EVID-013.

## Distinction Notes

Abzan is not generic WBG, generic defense, generic counters, generic graveyard play, Dromoka's brood, or any same-color Commander product. The safe identity separator is White-centered continuity through family, oath, ancestor memory, patient defense, practical survival, and death-transcending obligation. Broader phrasing in that direction is `Vox Mana synthesis`, not MTG canon. See ABZAN-EVID-026 and ABZAN-EVID-030.
