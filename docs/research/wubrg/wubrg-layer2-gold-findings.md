## Gold Findings

- Lore frame: Five-Color is a Vox Mana synthesis of full color access, coalition, breadth, spectrum, and totality. It is not an official faction or doctrine.
- Philosophy frame: WUBRG asks whether the user wants every color present and negotiated, not whether one color or missing-color frame dominates.
- Mechanics frame: domain, converge, sunburst, WUBRG costs, WUBRG activations, multicolor payoffs, and basic-land-type payoffs are usable support texture when manually bounded.
- Commander frame: WUBRG deck construction is source-backed through Commander color identity rules, but named commander/deck recommendations remain support-only.
- Placement frame: good fit requires explicit five-color fullness, integration, breadth, coalition, and tradeoff awareness. Poor fit includes goodstuff-only, superiority, Colorless conflation, four-color missing-color framing, and weak support-only claims.
- Maze frame: exact Commander handoff uses `id=wubrg is:commander f:commander`; support links remain support/discovery.
