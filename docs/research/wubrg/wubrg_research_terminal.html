Section 903.4

Rules & Identity Parameters

Comprehensive Rules define Color Identity under Rule 903.4. This forms the absolute baseline of our data architectures. Unlike normal game rules where a card's active state defines its colors, identity is additive, evaluating all available symbols across both faces of a card before play begins.

I
Additive Mechanics

Established globally from casting costs, indicator dots, rules text symbols, and characteristic-defining abilities.

II
Double-Faced Scope (903.4d)

Calculates color symbols found on the front side and the back side together. Crucial for MDFCs/TDFCs.

III
The Devoid Safeguard

In-game "colorless" designators (Devoid) do not override color identity calculation rules for deck building.

Comprehensive Rulebook Excerpts Tier 1 Verified
CR 903.4

"The color identity of a card is the color or colors of any mana symbols in its mana cost or rules text, plus any colors defined by its characteristic-defining abilities..."

CR 903.4d

"The color identity of a double-faced card is calculated from the faces of both sides combined..."

CR 903.5d

"A card with a basic land type may be included in a Commander deck only if each color of mana it can produce is included in the commander's color identity."
