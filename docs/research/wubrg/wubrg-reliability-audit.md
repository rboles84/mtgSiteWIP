# WUBRG Reliability Audit

Audit date: 2026-06-13

## Reliability Decision

WUBRG has enough source-backed Layer 2 material for controlled Layer 1 promotion as a placeable, dossier-visible, Maze-linked identity, but not enough to become a public Home preview, public route expansion, or Colorless/WUBRG Crucible surface.

## Useful Direction From Existing Local Files

- Public copy should prefer “Five-Color”; WUBRG remains the internal/display code.
- WUBRG should read as full color access, coalition, synthesis, breadth, spectrum, and totality.
- WUBRG is not an official faction, plane, civilization, institution, guild, college, or single personality.
- Five-color Commander identity grants access to all five colors at the deck level.
- Individual Magic cards remain governed by their own color-pie and rules context.
- WUBRG and Colorless are opposites in deck-construction shape: all five colors present versus no colored identity.
- Four-color identities are missing-color frames; WUBRG has no missing color.

## Unsupported Or Stale Claims Not Promoted

- “WUBRG is the ultimate philosophy” or superior to other identities.
- “WUBRG means balance” as an official doctrine.
- “WUBRG always means goodstuff.”
- “Any five-color commander is a lore proof of WUBRG identity.”
- “Ulalek, Eldrazi Incursion, Devoid, or five-color Eldrazi prove Colorless.”
- “Commander popularity, cEDH viability, pricing, or meta power” without dated current data.
- “Draconic Domination” and “Painbow” as anything stronger than support-only deck/product rows; VM-368 captured official/current source agreement but not lore, legality, popularity, ranking, metagame, or representative play-pattern proof.
- Any exact Oracle, legality, or card-text claim sourced only from local essays, generated files, runtime JSON, or search result snippets.

## Conflict Rule

If local WUBRG prose conflicts with official/current sources, official/current sources win. The local prose remains preserved as historical research, but the conflicting claim stays out of raw WUBRG and generated runtime data.

## Evidence Boundary

Generated/runtime files, dossier output, placement output, and builder output are downstream artifacts. They cannot support WUBRG lore, rules, Commander, deck, or public-copy claims.
