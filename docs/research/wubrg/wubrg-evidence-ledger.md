## Layer 2 Promotion Decision

WUBRG passes Layer 2 for:

- Lore/philosophy framing as Vox Mana Five-Color synthesis.
- Mechanics texture with manual-verification boundaries.
- Placement profile with positive and negative evidence terms.
- Public copy constraints that avoid official-faction, superiority, goodstuff-only, and Colorless conflation claims.
- Maze/dossier exact WUBRG Commander handoff.

WUBRG does not pass Layer 2 for:

- Home preview.
- Public route expansion.
- Colorless/WUBRG Crucibles.
- Current popularity, ranking, price, or metagame advice.
- Exact named-card legality beyond sources captured for the specific claim.
