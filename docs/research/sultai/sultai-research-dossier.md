# Sultai Research Dossier

Status: VM-209 conservative dossier. Sultai remains docs-only, non-live, and review-gated.

This dossier summarizes only the source-bound floor established by `sultai-source-ledger.md` and `sultai-evidence-ledger.md`. It is not an architecture document and must not be used to create raw-faction JSON, runtime keys, generated artifacts, routes, Maze behavior, Home previews, schemas, Supabase changes, or placement changes in VM-209.

## Identity Floor

Sultai is the Black-Green-Blue wedge in the approved MaRo source. The source frames Sultai around ruthlessness: willingness to act, to do what needs to be done, and to pursue victory through resource denial, theft, graveyard use, costs, and pressure on hand or library. VM-209 records `SULTAI` as the docs key and `BGU` as metadata/query-only context, not a runtime alias or public key.

## Khans-Era Floor

The Khans-era Sultai Brood emulates dragon ruthlessness through the fang. Its power floor is exploitative: wealth, land resources, subjugated people, necromancy, sibsig labor and armies, naga rulership, rakshasa pacts, poison, mind control, and luxury built on the dead.

Queen Sidisi is the Khans-era khan and queen anchor. Safe Khans-era location anchors include Kheru Temple, Qarsi Palace, Ukud Necropolis, Gudul, Gurmag Swamps, Marang River Fortress, Sagu, Molderfang Falls, and Crocodile Pits.

## Fate Reforged And Tasigur Floor

Fate Reforged material supports an ancient Sultai condition of cruelty, treachery, rakshasa pacts, undead servants, Tasigur's Sultai fortune, and Sultai tactics against Silumgar. `Khanfall` supports a narrow transition floor: Tasigur attends crisis talks, disappears under truce conditions, and is ultimately made Silumgar's trophy in a world where the word khan is denied.

Detailed Tasigur, Shidiqi, Sidisi, Feyomsi, Taigam, and Kirada biographies remain `Manual fill required` unless later cards capture direct official sources.

## Silumgar Boundary

Silumgar clan is not the same as Khans-era Sultai Brood. It is a Dragons-era dragonlord hierarchy that embodies ruthlessness under Silumgar. It has few living followers but vast undead numbers, values power and knowledge, uses top-down dragonlord authority, and repurposes corpses and life force through necromancy. Sidisi exists in this era as Undead Vizier, not as khan.

Use Silumgar material as contrast, suppression, and timeline boundary unless a row explicitly makes a narrower bridge.

## Modern Dragonstorm Floor

Modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai are revived after resistance within Silumgar's brood and the defeat of the dragonlords. This Sultai is not simply the old Brood restored. It rejects both Silumgar and old Sultai oppression, rejects rakshasa pacts, decentralizes power, and gives honored dead a protected role through the Rite of Renewal.

The modern floor includes agriculture, fishing, exports, artisans, Panjasi merchants, spies and scouts, the Lasyd, naga protectors, the Abiding Harvest, Rite of Renewal, life-essence magic, dragonstorm scouting, opportunistic dragon capture, poison-breath clan dragons, and locations such as Kheru City, Qarsi Palace, Kishla Stilt Village, Gudul Islands, Gurmag Swamp, Marang River, Sagu Forest, and Bloomvine Jungle.

## Commander Support

They can suggest operator vocabulary such as graveyard value, theft/exile-casting, mill and rad counters, morph, Mimeoplasm-style graveyard construction, and mutate. They do not prove Sultai lore, Tarkir origin, commander legality, or any claim about Sultai Brood history.
