## 1. Core Identity

### What Esper Is

> **Promoted** - ESPER-001, ESPER-002

Esper is the white-blue-black shard addressed by Mark Rosewater's official Esper Week article, "Striving For Perfection." For Vox Mana onboarding, Esper's color direction is recorded as WUB and its center color is Blue.

The local VM-156 canon inventory classifies this Rosewater file as the primary Esper identity source in the repo. That makes the article the evidence floor for VM-163 identity claims.

### Blue As Design Center

> **Promoted** - ESPER-002, ESPER-003, ESPER-004

The Esper article is written as an interview with Blue. Its central frame is potential: Blue believes that anyone or anything can change for the better if it gains the necessary knowledge and applies it deliberately.

The article's practical sequence is:

1. Desire: know what you want.
2. Drive: commit to pursuing it.
3. Details: acquire the knowledge needed.
4. Deployment: apply that knowledge.

Esper is presented in this article as Blue's proof-of-concept world for perfection: a place where order has been pushed so far that weather, politics, and biology are described as mastered. This is design commentary, not in-world narration.

### White And Black As Allies

> **Promoted** - ESPER-005, ESPER-006

The Rosewater article frames White and Black as Blue's allies in Esper-adjacent terms:

- White aligns with Blue through societal improvement, long-term planning, strategy, and technology used for public benefit.
- Black aligns with Blue through control, information value, focus, and vision, while carrying an ethical risk because it can prioritize gain over society.

This is the safe VM-163 basis for later WU/UB/WB separator work. More detailed claims about Esper law, hierarchy, ruling class, or institutions require manual fill.

### Red And Green As Missing Pressures

> **Promoted with source note** - ESPER-007

The official article frames Red as impulsive and hostile to Blue's rational method. It frames Green as resistant to change and hostile to technology. The Alara RTF support material lists Esper as lacking Red and Green. Together, these sources support the VM-163 identity statement that Esper's design tension is built around WUB order/perfection in the absence of Red/Green impulse and organic acceptance.

Do not expand this into detailed weather, biology, geography, or society claims without manual-fill evidence.

### WUB Commander Patterns

> **Support-only** - ESPER-013, ESPER-014, ESPER-015

The internal Commander JSONL contains 10 WUB rows. These rows support operator and recommendation language only.

High-signal WUB patterns found:

- Control and card advantage.
- Top-of-library manipulation and miracles.
- Artifact creature/go-wide strategies.
- Life gain and passive inevitability.
- Reanimation and dungeon/value loops.
- Tribal overlays such as Zombies and Knights.
- Token card-draw engines.
- Evasive aggression and connive.

These rows must not support claims about Esper society, figures, geography, metallurgy, chronology, or in-world metaphysics.

## 7. Research Summary

### Strongly Supported

- Esper is the WUB shard in the local official Rosewater article.
- Blue is the center/design lens for Esper.
- Esper's design-philosophy frame is perfection through knowledge, potential, planning, and applied information.
- White and Black are Blue's allies in the article through planning/strategy/social improvement and control/information/focus respectively.
- Red and Green are the enemy-color pressures from Blue's perspective, and the local Alara support material records Esper as missing Red and Green.

### Support-Only

- Artifacts, colored artifacts, filigree, Ethersworn, etherium, and detailed Esper geography are strong discovery topics but need local official captures before canon promotion.
- WUB Commander rows support search/operator and recommendation language only.
- Cross-color dynamics supports Vox Mana separator language only.

### Manual Fill Required

- Any detailed lore about Esper society, named figures, exact locations, chronology, Conflux battles, etherium metallurgy, and contested canon rulings.
