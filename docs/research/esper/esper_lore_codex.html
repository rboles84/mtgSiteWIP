Section 01

Geographical & Structural Grids

Esper's topography has been stripped of organic randomness. Waterways are metered, clouds follow clinical coordinate pathways, and the landscape is organized into distinct zones of glass, metal, and polished spires.

The Absence of Red & Green

Without natural volcanic volatility (Red) or unchecked biological evolution (Green), Esper's climate remains perfectly static and predictable.

VECTIS CITY

High-spire urban center built of towering silver, glass, and polished steel. It stands as the seat of the Academy of Mages and coordinates the grid network.

TIDEHOLLOW

A damp, lightless subterranean network below Lower Vectis. Home to scullers, scrappers, and the discarded populations living off residual scrap metals.

THE GLASS DUNES

Vast deserts of shimmering white-glass sandstorms. Glaciers of glass compile over centuries under enormous pressure and predictable temporal shifts.

SANCTUM ARCANUM

A monolithic, heavily guarded citadel that houses the central headquarters of the Seekers of Carmot and the legendary Codex Etherium archive.

Reliability Audit

Resolving Narrative Contradictions

Issue: "Tezzeret is the Carmot"

Declared by Tezzeret in Test of Metal. Directly conflicts with core Wizards manuals describing carmot as an objective red mineral harvested in the volcanic zones of Jund.

Audit Verdict: Rejected as metaphorical/incorrect.
Issue: Sharuum's Throne Abdication

Stover's novel states Sharuum traded away her throne to Tezzeret. All subsequent card updates and official summaries depict Sharuum continuing to hold the title of active Hegemon of Esper.

Audit Verdict: Disregarded; Sharuum remains Hegemon.
