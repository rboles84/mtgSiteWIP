General Overview of Esper

Under the three-color mana alignment of Blue (primary), White, and Black, the world of Esper discarded natural entropic cycles and raw untethered instincts (lacking Green and Red mana). This exclusion meant that chaotic fire and primitive jungle growths disappeared, leaving a perfectly ordered landscape consisting of glass-dune deserts, metal coastlines, and brilliant, highly structured tides.

Planar Architecture & Philosophy

Nothing is accidental on Esper. The clouds run on schedules, the sea's tides follow a rigid mathematical pattern, and the winds are engineered for maximum efficiency. Every organism is considered a dynamic variable designed to be integrated into the grand sovereign grid of the plane.

The Noble Work & Carmot Crisis

The ultimate ambition of Esper's inhabitants is The Noble Work. Recognizing biological flesh as weak, decay-prone, and mathematically flawed, the citizens systematically infuse their skeletal structures, vital organs, and limbs with the glistening alloy of Etherium.

Crucius, an ancient sphinx, invented etherium by utilizing a rare compound known as Carmot. Carmot is the critical, non-regenerative catalytic element required to bind spellcraft to raw metal.

The Seekers of Carmot

Because Esper was isolated during the sundering, the supply of Carmot eventually depleted. The plane's society was thrust into a silent panic. A group known as the Seekers of Carmot arose—a highly secretive cabal dedicated to scouring the lands for any hidden deposits of Carmot, often resorting to dark or highly tyrannical means to secure it.

Esper Castes & Inhabitants

Esper society is structured strictly by biological and mechanical taxonomy. There are three primary thinking species: humans, vedalken, and sphinxes, with various artificial servant constructs.

1. The Sphinxes The supreme intellectual rulers and philosophers. Possessing massive mechanical, silver-filigreed wings, they govern with calculated decrees and demand rigorous adherence to logic.
2. The Vedalken Four-armed, blue-skinned scholars, surgeons, and etherium sculptors. They view flesh as an engineering puzzle waiting to be solved and represent the primary technicians of the Noble Work.
3. Human Filigree Mages Mortal casters who act as bureaucrats, soldiers, or agents. Many belong to the high-ranking elite, while the un-infused reside in the lower slummy ports of the world.
4. Homunculi & Gargoyles Artificial clockwork and filigree constructs programmed to run routine maintenance, carry items, or perform border defense patrols.

Sovereign Citadels & Geographies

Esper's geography is sleek, reflective, and dominated by architectural and geometric grids.

Vectis

The largest urban sprawl on Esper. Divided into **Upper Vectis**—a pristine city of gleaming towers, glass streets, and wealthy enhanced elites—and the **Tidehollow Sub-slums**, a damp, subterranean labyrinth where outcasts and un-infused scavengers scrap for fragments of etherium.

The Glass Dunes

An endless expanse of perfectly round, shimmering glass grains. These dunes are swept by mathematically precise wind currents, reflecting the blinding white sky and magnifying defensive magical arrays.

Palanth Citadel

The absolute center of academic and physical perfection. A massive floating citadel where the highest council of sphinxes gather to forecast global alignment changes and coordinate research.

The Otarian Sea

A pristine, metallic-sheen body of water. Its waves are regular and constant, devoid of chaotic natural storms due to weather-controlling arrays aligned in massive towers along the shores.

Key Figures of Esper

SH

Sharuum the Hegemon

A majestic, ancient female sphinx of massive intelligence. She is the current supreme sovereign ruler of Esper. Sharuum works tirelessly to resolve the Carmot scarcity to preserve the lifespan of her metallic subjects.

TZ

Tezzeret

Born in the damp dirt of the Tidehollow slums, Tezzeret's raw talent for sculpting metal led him to qualify as a Seeker of Carmot. After his Spark ignited, he expanded his control and returned during the Phyrexian Invasion to spread planar warnings.

CR

Crucius the Mad

The legendary sphinx genius who originally cracked the formula for manufacturing etherium. Crucius went missing centuries ago, leaving behind incomplete designs and notes that sparked the Seekers of Carmot factions.

⚔️ The Conflux Conflict

Exploring the historical overlap, ideological friction, and eventual resolution between Esper and Bant.

Esper Tactics ▶ Bant Tradition

The Betrayal of Single-Combat

When the Conflux began, the pristine kingdom of Bant expected combat to follow their historical, ritualized rules of single-combat dueling (the *Exalted* custom). Instead, Esper's pragmatic metal-mages bypassed honor entirely, projecting mental arrays directly onto the field.

In a catastrophic tactical encounter, Esperite mind-sculptors gained control of the rhox monk **Mubin** (Knight-General Rafiq's close confidant), forcing Rafiq to make the tragic decision to permanently paralyze his own friend to neutralize the mental threat.

Phyrexian Era ▶ Multiverse Alliance

The Messenger of Tidehollow

Following the complete reunification of Alara's borders, the geographic proximity between Valeron (Bant) and Upper Vectis (Esper) became a tense, highly fortified border.

During the subsequent New Phyrexian invasion, Tezzeret traveled to the dark metal underbelly of the Tidehollow slums. He selected a local youth named **Estel**, giving him a secure etherium-infused payload to bypass borders and deliver an critical warning directly to Rafiq to summon Bant's celestial angels to halt Elesh Norn's infection.
