# Prismari Narrative Taxonomy

**School:** Prismari  
**Color Pair:** Blue/Red  
**Core Axiom:** Prismari believes magic is most true when it becomes art in motion.

**Primary Question:** Is the self expressed through disciplined technique, emotional release, or the charged collision between them?

## Taxonomy Nodes

### Founder Axiom (`founder_axiom`)

Galazeth/Prismari-style identity: elemental force becomes art and art becomes operational power.

- **Signals:** founder dragon, elder inspiration, resource conversion, storm-like copying
- **Example Cards:** Galazeth Prismari, Prismari, the Inspiration
- **UI Panel Copy:** Your spellcraft wants to be seen, but not merely as display. It wants the display to do work.

### Technique vs. Emotion (`technique_vs_emotion`)

Blue frames the craft; red supplies the courage, heat, and immediacy.

- **Signals:** training, copying, rehearsal, breakthrough, risk
- **Example Cards:** Teach by Example, Practical Research, Prismari Apprentice, Expressive Iteration
- **UI Panel Copy:** You are not choosing between control and passion; you are looking for the moment where they intensify each other.

### Spectacle as Truth (`spectacle_as_truth`)

The huge spell is not excess; it is the honest scale of the feeling or idea.

- **Signals:** big spells, expensive spells matter, damage + draw, stage reveal
- **Example Cards:** Magma Opus, Creative Outburst, Spectacle Mage, Zaffai, Thunder Conductor
- **UI Panel Copy:** Some truths are too large for a quiet answer. Prismari lets the answer arrive as a performance.

### Elemental Medium (`elemental_medium`)

Fire, water, wind, ice, magma, lightning, and color are not effects; they are the materials of expression.

- **Signals:** Elemental tokens, elemental transformation, living artwork, weather and color
- **Example Cards:** Elemental Masterpiece, Resculpt, Colorstorm Stallion, Scorched Geyser
- **UI Panel Copy:** Your medium is not paint or ink alone. The weather, the floor, the heat in the room—everything can become part of the spell.

### Performed Research (`performed_research`)

Study is not hidden in a notebook. It happens through practice, revision, and public experiment.

- **Signals:** card selection, modal questions, study as motion, revision loops
- **Example Cards:** Multiple Choice, Practical Research, Expressive Iteration, Rootha, Mercurial Artist
- **UI Panel Copy:** You learn by staging the experiment, watching what changes, and then restaging it with more intention.

### Improvised Breakthrough (`improvised_breakthrough`)

The best moment may be the one the plan could not predict, but the craft can still carry it.

- **Signals:** reinterpretation, free casting, demonstrate, red rupture
- **Example Cards:** Reinterpret, Creative Technique, Muse Vortex, Splatter Technique
- **UI Panel Copy:** The plan is useful until it becomes a cage. Then Prismari breaks it with style.

### Rhythmic Study (`rhythmic_study`)

Some Prismari patterns arrive like music: suspend, refrain, countdown, repetition, and release.

- **Signals:** suspend, recurring cadence, music, countdown
- **Example Cards:** Inspiring Refrain, Rousing Refrain, Prismari Pianist
- **UI Panel Copy:** A delayed spell is not waiting; it is keeping time. The payoff lands on the downbeat.

### Duality Amplifier (`duality_amplifier`)

Prismari doubles the event: one spell becomes two triggers, one voice becomes an echo, one performance becomes a room-wide reaction.

- **Signals:** magecraft, copy, trigger doubling, echo
- **Example Cards:** Veyran, Voice of Duality, Rootha, Mercurial Artist, Teach by Example
- **UI Panel Copy:** The point is not more for its own sake. The echo reveals what the first cast could not say alone.

### Resource Residue (`resource_residue`)

Every spell leaves something behind: treasure, counters, graveyard material, or future charge.

- **Signals:** treasure, graveyard fuel, spell count, accretion
- **Example Cards:** Storm-Kiln Artist, Tome Shredder, Elementalist's Palette, Galazeth Prismari
- **UI Panel Copy:** Prismari does not waste expression. Even the sparks become fuel for the next impossible thing.

### Kinetic Technique (`kinetic_technique`)

Dance, attack, gesture, and bodily motion shape the spell as much as words or formulas.

- **Signals:** attack triggers, dance, choreography, combat copies
- **Example Cards:** Maelstrom Muse, Rionya, Fire Dancer, Visionary's Dance, Surge to Victory
- **UI Panel Copy:** The body is part of the spell diagram. Motion is the syntax.

## False-Positive Guardrails

- Do not classify generic blue-red artifact science as Prismari unless it has performance, elemental art, or big-spell expression.
- Do not over-index on chaos. Prismari expression is often rehearsed, choreographed, or framed.
- Do not reduce Prismari to 'spellslinger'. Its distinctive Strixhaven expression is big, elemental, artistic, and theatrical.
