# Silverquill Sources

This bundle is structured around official Wizards/Scryfall grounding plus card-specific Scryfall exact-title searches.

## Official Wizards / Magic Sources

- Planeswalker’s Guide to Secrets of Strixhaven — Silverquill magic as spoken, written, and signed word, shown as calligraphic ink and light; Silverquill as competitive, stylish, leadership-oriented, and commanding.
- Secrets of Strixhaven Vision Design Handoff, Part 1 — Silverquill as the college of eloquence; original aggressive small/evasive creature strategy; new Secrets framing around keyword counters, Inklings, removal, and combat tricks.
- Strixhaven product page — Silverquill as the College of Eloquence, wielding word-magic, style, intimidation, competition, leadership, wit, and charisma.
- Planeswalker’s Guide to Strixhaven — Inkling mascot details and black-aligned shadow/ink creature framing.
- The Legends of Strixhaven — Embrose/Shaile/Killian/Shadrix character grounding.
- Commander 2021 decklists and token articles — Silverquill Statement anchors including Breena, Felisa, Inkshield, Cunning Rhetoric, Inklings, and political Commander framing.

## Scryfall Search Patterns

- `wm:silverquill`
- `set:sos watermark:silverquill`
- exact-title searches in `source_url` for every matrix row

## Notes

- `Detain()`, `Override()`, `ConstraintField()`, and `AccretionEngine()` are shared Vox Mana primitive names, not claims that Silverquill literally has Azorius Detain.
- The current Secrets of Strixhaven anchors are kept in `current-*` tiers so repo consumers can distinguish them from original Strixhaven and Commander 2021 anchors.
- Broader white-black mechanics are included only when they reinforce Silverquill language/status/ink/politics readings.
