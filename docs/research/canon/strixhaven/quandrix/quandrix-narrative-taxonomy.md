# Quandrix Narrative Taxonomy

**Identity:** Quandrix College
**Core axiom:** Reality is an equation that can be grown, copied, doubled, and proven by scale.
**Primary tension:** abstract theory versus material substance; the answer matters only when it changes the world.
**Vox Mana placement mode:** you read the world as variable, pattern, threshold, and proof. The right action is to solve for scale, then let the result become real.

---

## 1. High-Level Read

Quandrix is not simply “Simic,” “ramp,” “counters,” or “smart people doing math.”

Quandrix is the green-blue worldview where:

- reality can be described as pattern
- patterns can be expressed as equations
- equations can become creatures, counters, copies, or lands
- one answer can imply another answer
- scale matters because small changes compound
- the line between abstract theory and physical substance is negotiable

The Quandrix player does not ask:

> What is the biggest thing I can ramp into?

They ask:

> What pattern is hidden here, and how large can the proof become?

## 3. Archetypal Roles

### 3.1 The Fractal Theorist

**Drive:** turn spells, cards, and ideas into visible Fractal bodies.
**Cards:** Deekah, Fractal Theorist; Body of Research; Serpentine Curve; Manifestation Sage.
**Narrative line:** “The theorem is complete when it can stand on the battlefield.”

Use for:

- Fractal token panels
- spells-matter recommendations that should not feel Prismari/Izzet
- body-from-knowledge copy
- placement cards about ideas becoming real

---

### 3.2 The Twin Proof

**Drive:** make creation recursive through copies, twins, and duplicate tokens.
**Cards:** Adrix and Nev, Twincasters; Double Major; Replication Technique; Rite of Replication.
**Narrative line:** “One answer is persuasive; two answers are structure.”

Use for:

- token-doubling sections
- copy/clone Maze links
- Adrix and Nev Commander paths
- UI states where one chip becomes two

---

### 3.3 The Growth Curve

**Drive:** make counters accumulate, double, and convert into cards or lethal scale.
**Cards:** Growth Curve; Tanazir Quandrix; Pensive Professor; Simic Ascendancy.
**Narrative line:** “A counter is not a marker; it is evidence that the proof is still growing.”

Use for:

- +1/+1 counter searches
- scaling and threshold panels
- counter-to-card-draw engines
- “growth as data” copy

---

### 3.4 The Land Equation

**Drive:** turn lands into thresholds, cards, creatures, and conclusions.
**Cards:** Zimone, Quandrix Prodigy; Eureka Moment; Emergent Sequence; Coiling Oracle.
**Narrative line:** “The next land is not just mana; it is the next line in the solution.”

Use for:

- eight-land threshold explanations
- ramp sections that need flavor beyond “get more mana”
- landfall-adjacent Commander links
- mana-base and campus panels

---

### 3.5 The X-Variable

**Drive:** convert available mana into scalable spells and visible payoff.
**Cards:** Wild Hypothesis; Mind into Matter; Exponential Growth; Biomass Mutation.
**Narrative line:** “Quandrix asks how much reality the variable can hold.”

Use for:

- X-spell deck hooks
- big-mana but not generic-ramp panels
- variable-size Fractal UI
- “solve for X” CTAs

---

### 3.6 The Simplifier

**Drive:** reduce complex boards into tokens, categories, and comparable variables.
**Cards:** Oversimplify; Perplexing Test; Square Up; Quandrix Charm.
**Narrative line:** “The board becomes easier to read when every piece is converted to the same language.”

Use for:

- interaction recommendations
- board reset copy
- token/non-token categorization
- base power/toughness setting

## 4. Voice Rules

Use words like:

- prove
- solve
- scale
- double
- replicate
- materialize
- theorem
- variable
- pattern
- curve
- threshold
- Fractal

Avoid defaulting to:

- “science deck”
- “goodstuff ramp”
- “generic Simic value”
- “math homework”
- “biology blob”

## 6. Anti-Drift Guardrails

- Do not reduce Quandrix to generic Simic counters; require math, scale, variable, proof, or Fractal language.
- Do not make it only ramp; ramp should unlock threshold, X, Fractal, or proof states.
- Do not make it Izzet spellslinger; spell casting must materialize into geometry, tokens, or measured scale.
- Do not make it Azorius control; structure is mathematical, not legalistic.
- Do not overuse dense equations as visual noise; use math as metaphor and interaction structure.
