\## MAJOR PLANES



\### Core Planes ✅

\- \*\*Dominaria\*\* - the "home" plane, most historical events

\- \*\*Ravnica\*\* - city-world, ten guilds

\- \*\*Zendikar\*\* - adventure world, Eldrazi prison

\- \*\*Innistrad\*\* - gothic horror plane

\- \*\*Theros\*\* - Greek mythology-inspired

\- \*\*Alara\*\* - split into five shards, later reunited

\- \*\*Mirrodin/New Phyrexia\*\* - artificial metal world

\- \*\*Kamigawa\*\* - Japanese-inspired, now cyberpunk

\- \*\*Tarkir\*\* - dragons and clans

\- \*\*Kaladesh/Avishkar\*\* - aether-powered invention plane

\- \*\*Ixalan\*\* - Age of Exploration, dinosaurs and pirates

\- \*\*Eldraine\*\* - Arthurian fantasy and fairy tales

\- \*\*Kaldheim\*\* - Norse mythology-inspired

\- \*\*New Capenna\*\* - art deco crime families

\- \*\*Ikoria\*\* - monster world

\- \*\*Strixhaven/Arcavios\*\* - magic university plane



\### Other Notable Planes

✅ \*\*Phyrexia\*\* - plane of body horror and corruption - is an artificial plane of mechanical and biomechanical "life", an ecosystem comprised of metal, death, and tissue.

✅ \*\*Lorwyn/Shadowmoor\*\* - day/night transforming plane

✅ \*\*Shandalar\*\* - is a rogue plane, drifting through the multiverse instead of retaining a steady position or course. It is a relatively small plane and incredibly rich in mana.

✅ \*\*Ulgrotha\*\* - which means "Garden" - a world very rich in mana and diverse life forms - was a small backwater plane, far from Dominaria, sometimes called the "Homelands" by its inhabitants.

✅ \*\*Rabiah\*\* - Arabian Nights setting - is a collection of one thousand and one originally identical planes, all refracted off of the original Rabiah during the Thousandfold Refraction

✅ \*\*Mercadia\*\* - a plane near Dominaria ruled from Mercadia City, sitting strategically atop the inverted mountain Mount Mercadia

✅ \*\*Rath\*\* - artificial plane that overlaid Dominaria

✅ \*\*Serra's Realm\*\* - artificial plane (destroyed)

✅ \*\*Regatha\*\* - known for a magical phenomenon called the Purifying Fire - Parts of Regatha are highly volcanic and wildlife have adapted to living there

✅ \*\*Vryn\*\* - Jace's home plane - The landscape of Vryn is littered with lines of gigantic inhabited mage-rings

✅ \*\*Fiora\*\* - A plane of vast natural beauty, its powerful city-states are divided by vast wilderness and treacherous seas

✅ \*\*Kylem\*\* - non-lethal showmanship combat sports plane

✅ \*\*Meditation Plane\*\* - Bolas's realm

✅ \*\*Equilor\*\* -  is one of the most ancient and distant planes in the Multiverse. Not much is known about this plane, as the only account of it was Urza's visit.

✅ \*\*Thunder Junction\*\* - wild west plane

✅ \*\*Bloomburrow\*\* - animal folk plane - a charming pastoral world full of anthropomorphic animals. No human has set foot on the plane; Planeswalkers who visit are animal-shifted to match its inhabitants.

✅ \*\*Duskmourn\*\* - horror house plane - a modern-looking world, including electronics like television sets, mechanical devices like chainsaws, and 80's style fashion like sneakers.

✅ \*\*Capenna\*\* - Elspeth's birth plane - is the plane on which the metropolis of New Capenna is located.\[3] New Capenna is the plane’s only major settlement, as the rest of the plane was devastated by invading Phyrexians and is largely uninhabited.

✅ \*\*Gobakhan\*\* - Teyo's home - a desert plane with twin suns, where civilization is surrounded by two massive sandstorms known as the Eastern Cloud and the Western Cloud.\[2]\[3] These contain deadly amounts of diamond micrograins.

✅ \*\*Ergamon\*\* - Still largely unexplored - described as having "colossal peaks and exotic fauna"

✅ \*\*Amonkhet\*\* - Egyptian-inspired, corrupted by Bolas

✅ \*\*Mongseng\*\* - Still largely unexplored

✅ \*\*Muraganda\*\* - a vibrant, mighty primordial plane of virulent life, hunted ruins, and raw, unforgiving landscapes

\## KEY CONCEPTS



\### Game Mechanics with Lore Flavor ✅

\- \*\*Banding\*\* - cooperation (old mechanic) - Mechanically, it's notorious for being one of the most confusing rules structures in MTG history, allowing an attacking or defending group of creatures to operate as a singular combat unit.

\- \*\*Phasing\*\* - Teferi's time magic - debuted in Mirage, it was initially used to represent general illusions, invisibility, and unstable, shifting reality, before becoming firmly locked down as Teferi's signature chronomancy.

\- \*\*Flashback\*\* - Tormented memories (Odyssey) - a mage recalling any spell they've already cast from memory.

\- \*\*Morph\*\* - hidden nature - In specific lore blocks, it represents distinct magic: on Tarkir, Morph represents dragon magic (specifically the masking mists generated by the spirit dragon Ugin to hide humanoids). On Dominaria (Onslaught block), it represented illusion magic practiced by the Illusionist Ixidor.

\- \*\*Level Up\*\* - growth over time

\- \*\*Annihilator\*\* - Eldrazi destruction
