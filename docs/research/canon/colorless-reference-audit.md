## Pre-flight context

- **Colorless is not a color and not a sixth color.** It is the condition *outside* the WUBRG wheel: objects with no color, mana represented by `{C}`, terrain stripped into Wastes, and beings/tools whose meaning is not generated by color allegiance. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`, `-007`)
- **It has no official color-pie philosophy of its own.** Rosewater/Wizards govern it "almost like a color" in design process while it remains not-a-color (`COLORLESS-EVID-019`). Any unifying "philosophy of colorless" is **Vox Mana synthesis**, not canon.
- **Colorless ≠ five-color.** Five-color (WUBRG) is *full inclusion*; colorless is *non-coloration*. They are **not opposites**, and colorless must never be framed as "above," "superior to," or "mastery of" WUBRG — the only acceptable frame is *outside / orthogonal* (`COLORLESS-EVID-016`).
- **It is a set of separate branches, not one thing.** The defining discipline is **separation**: artifacts ≠ Eldrazi ≠ Wastes ≠ Ugin/Karn ≠ generic mana ≠ `{C}` mana. Collapsing them into one "neutral everything-bucket" is the core failure mode (raw `great_tension.shadow`).
- **Product status (current):** VM-334 ratified `COLORLESS` as a **controlled placeable** Layer 1 identity — `colors: []`, `core_color: "C"`, `placement_eligible: true`, `preview_eligible: false`, directory links suppressed, no public route/alias. It is the **36th** live expression (35 colored + Colorless); **WUBRG remains absent**.

## The Colorless identity — branches

Colorless resolves into **branches that must be kept distinct**. The architecture (`metaphysics.md`) frames six "pillars"; the raw packet groups them into the branches below.

### Rules foundation: three meanings that are NOT interchangeable
Before any branch, the load-bearing distinction (`COLORLESS-EVID-007`, `-008`, `-009`; blocker `COLORLESS-MF-011`):
- **Colorless object** — an object with no color under the rules (Pillar A: "presence without WUBRG allegiance").
- **Colorless mana `{C}`** — a specific mana *type*; a `{C}` cost requires *specifically* colorless mana (Pillar B: "absence becomes infrastructure").
- **Generic mana** — a *cost shape* (`{1}`, `{2}`…) payable with any mana. Generic ≠ colorless. Treating a big-generic-cost deck as "colorless identity" is a primary false positive.

### Branch 1 — Artifact / Function ("being as tool")
Tools, engines, equipment, vehicles, constructs, mana rocks, modular systems — instrumental power with no inherent color philosophy. Power *and* danger are the same trait: **availability** (it serves any color because it chooses none). Boundary: colored artifacts and artifact civilizations stay colored; artifact presence alone is **not** Colorless identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-011`; blocker `COLORLESS-MF-007`)

### Branch 2 — Eldrazi / Void ("being as alien outside-color pressure")
Not toolhood — alienness and reality pressure. Devoid, exile patterns, cast-triggers, annihilator-era scale make the table "survive contact with something not built from the colors." Synthesis: *the Eldrazi don't take a side against the colors; they make the question of sides feel provincial.* Boundary: Eldrazi alone, **five-color** Eldrazi, and Devoid alone are each insufficient to prove Colorless identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-010`, `-012`; blocker `COLORLESS-MF-006`)

### Branch 3 — Wastes / Desolation ("place after color is stripped")
Colorless-mana terrain and the residue of color emptied into `{C}`. "The scar that proves absence can become a landform." Boundary: Wastes are *one specific* land/desolation branch, not a symbol for every colorless object. Card/rulings details are blockers. (`COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `-013`; blockers `COLORLESS-MF-002`, `-003`)

### Branch 4 — Ugin / Karn / Transcendence ("agency without color allegiance")
The rarest, softest branch: detached intervention, construction, spirit-dragon distance, perspective beyond color. **Support-bound** — detailed Ugin/Karn/ghostfire lore is **manual-fill required** and must not claim Colorless is "enlightened/higher/more complete." (`COLORLESS-EVID-014`; blocker `COLORLESS-MF-005`)

### Branch 5 — Player-fit / Strategy (Vox Mana synthesis only)
Attraction axes: restriction puzzles, artifact machinery, Eldrazi spectacle, austere/clean-mana systems, resource-denial / MUD-Stax-Tron engine play. Explicitly **synthesis**, not official psychographics. (`COLORLESS-EVID-022`, `-023`)

## False-positive guardrails (the discipline)

Colorless **is not** proven by any of:
- being a sixth color, hidden mono-color, or "completion/mastery of WUBRG";
- generic mana costs, or a colorless-*looking* cost;
- a `{C}` requirement alone (mechanics signal ≠ whole identity);
- artifacts, artifact-heavy colored decks, equipment packages, or colored artifacts;
- Eldrazi, five-color Eldrazi, or Devoid alone;
- Wastes as a stand-in for all colorless;
- Ugin/Karn used to claim colorless is "enlightened/higher";
- **Phyrexia** — it *infects/converts/subjugates* color; it does not exist outside color (keep as distinction-only; `COLORLESS-EVID-017`, blocker `MF-013`);
- gray/neutral visual tone without rules or branch support.
