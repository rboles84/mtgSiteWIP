# The Metaphysics of WUBRG: Definitive Scholarly & Design References

## 1. Primary Design & Canonical Texts
The philosophical structures established throughout these documents are directly anchored in the foundational design essays published by Mark Rosewater (Head Designer for *Magic: The Gathering*) across his multidecadal tenure at Wizards of the Coast. Specifically, this analysis synthesizes and expands upon the definitive "Color Pie Revisited" series:

* **Rosewater, M.** (2015). *The Great White Way Revisited*. Wizards of the Coast. 
    * URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/great-white-way-revisited-2015-07-13
    * *Core Contribution:* Defined the foundational White paradox of peace through structural restriction and the sacrifice of individuality.
* **Rosewater, M.** (2015). *True Blue Revisited*. Wizards of the Coast.
    * URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/true-blue-revisited-2015-07-20
    * *Core Contribution:* Established Blue's obsession with *Tabula Rasa* (the blank slate), omniscience, and structural optimization.
* **Rosewater, M.** (2015). *Black Revisited*. Wizards of the Coast.
    * URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/black-revisited-2015-07-27
    * *Core Contribution:* Clarified that Black is not inherently evil, but rather completely dedicated to individual omnipotence and self-sovereignty.
* **Rosewater, M.** (2015). *Seeing Red Revisited*. Wizards of the Coast.
    * URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/seeing-red-revisited-2015-08-03
    * *Core Contribution:* Outlined Red's core drive of absolute freedom through emotional expression and immediate kinetic action.
* **Rosewater, M.** (2015). *It's Not Easy Being Green Revisited*. Wizards of the Coast.
    * URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/its-not-easy-being-green-revisited-2015-08-10
    * *Core Contribution:* Articulated Green’s unique framework of natural acceptance, ecological destiny, and interconnected growth.
