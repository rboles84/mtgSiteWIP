## MAJOR PLANES

### Core Planes ✅
- **Dominaria** - the "home" plane, most historical events
- **Ravnica** - city-world, ten guilds
- **Zendikar** - adventure world, Eldrazi prison
- **Innistrad** - gothic horror plane
- **Theros** - Greek mythology-inspired
- **Alara** - split into five shards, later reunited
- **Mirrodin/New Phyrexia** - artificial metal world
- **Kamigawa** - Japanese-inspired, now cyberpunk
- **Tarkir** - dragons and clans
- **Kaladesh/Avishkar** - aether-powered invention plane
- **Ixalan** - Age of Exploration, dinosaurs and pirates
- **Eldraine** - Arthurian fantasy and fairy tales
- **Kaldheim** - Norse mythology-inspired
- **New Capenna** - art deco crime families
- **Ikoria** - monster world
- **Strixhaven/Arcavios** - magic university plane

### Other Notable Planes
✅ **Phyrexia** - plane of body horror and corruption - is an artificial plane of mechanical and biomechanical "life", an ecosystem comprised of metal, death, and tissue.
✅ **Lorwyn/Shadowmoor** - day/night transforming plane
✅ **Shandalar** - is a rogue plane, drifting through the multiverse instead of retaining a steady position or course. It is a relatively small plane and incredibly rich in mana.
✅ **Ulgrotha** - which means "Garden" - a world very rich in mana and diverse life forms - was a small backwater plane, far from Dominaria, sometimes called the "Homelands" by its inhabitants.
✅ **Rabiah** - Arabian Nights setting - is a collection of one thousand and one originally identical planes, all refracted off of the original Rabiah during the Thousandfold Refraction
✅ **Mercadia** - a plane near Dominaria ruled from Mercadia City, sitting strategically atop the inverted mountain Mount Mercadia
✅ **Rath** - artificial plane that overlaid Dominaria
✅ **Serra's Realm** - artificial plane (destroyed)
✅ **Regatha** - known for a magical phenomenon called the Purifying Fire - Parts of Regatha are highly volcanic and wildlife have adapted to living there
✅ **Vryn** - Jace's home plane - The landscape of Vryn is littered with lines of gigantic inhabited mage-rings
✅ **Fiora** - A plane of vast natural beauty, its powerful city-states are divided by vast wilderness and treacherous seas
✅ **Kylem** - non-lethal showmanship combat sports plane
✅ **Meditation Plane** - Bolas's realm
✅ **Equilor** -  is one of the most ancient and distant planes in the Multiverse. Not much is known about this plane, as the only account of it was Urza's visit.
✅ **Thunder Junction** - wild west plane
✅ **Bloomburrow** - animal folk plane
✅ **Duskmourn** - horror house plane
✅ **Capenna** - Elspeth's birth plane
✅ **Gobakhan** - Teyo's home
✅ **Ergamon** - Still largely unexplored - described as having "colossal peaks and exotic fauna"
✅ **Amonkhet** - Egyptian-inspired, corrupted by Bolas
✅ **Mongseng** - Still largely unexplored
✅ **Muraganda** - a vibrant, mighty primordial plane of virulent life, hunted ruins, and raw, unforgiving landscapes

## MAJOR HISTORICAL ERAS & EVENTS ✅

### Pre-History ✅
- Birth of the Elder Dragons (-20,000 AR)
- Dragon War (-19,000 AR)
- Ugin's spark ignites (first known planeswalker)
- Elder Dragon War
- Fall of the Primevals
- Shattering of Madara (Nicol Bolas vs Leviathan)

### Time of the Thran (-5000 AR) ✅
- Thran civilization peak
- Yawgmoth's rise
- Thran-Phyrexian War
- Glacian and Rebbec trap Yawgmoth in Phyrexia
- Creation of Phyrexia as plane of corruption

### Time of Legends ✅
- Sorin Markov born (-2440 AR)
- Edgar Markov creates vampirism on Innistrad
- Sorin's spark ignites
- Imprisonment of the Eldrazi on Zendikar by Sorin, Ugin, Nahiri (-1400 AR)
- Formation of the Shard of Twelve Worlds (400 AR)

### The Brothers' War (20-64 AR) ✅
- Urza and Mishra born (Year 0 AR)
- Discovery of Koilos
- Brotherhood's End - Tocasia dies
- 40+ year war between brothers
- Battle at Argoth
- Sylex Blast - Urza's spark ignites, Ice Age begins
- Mishra revealed as Phyrexian construct

### Dark Age & Ice Age (64-2934 AR) ✅
- Urza becomes planeswalker, begins anti-Phyrexian crusade
- Meets Xantcha (defective Phyrexian)
- Formation of the Shard of Twelve Worlds
- Kami War on Kamigawa (3207-3227 AR)
- Toshiro Umezawa to Dominaria, starts lineage
- Summit of the Null Moon
- Freyalise's World Spell ends Ice Age (2934 AR)
- Shard destroyed

### The Thaw (2934-3285 AR) ✅
- War for Shandalar
- Flooding of Terisiare
- New Argive formed
- Fall of Soldev

### War with Phyrexia (3285-4206 AR) ✅
- Founding of Tolarian Academy (3285 AR)
- Tolarian Temporal Disaster (3307 AR)
- Urza's Bloodline Project
- Weatherlight crew assembled
- Rath overlays Dominaria
- **Phyrexian Invasion of Dominaria (4205 AR)**
  - Nine Titans assault Phyrexia
  - Yawgmoth enters Dominaria as death cloud
  - Legacy weapon activated
  - Yawgmoth destroyed
  - Karn ascends

### The Rift Era (4206-4500 AR) ✅
- Time rifts threaten Dominaria
- Karona's War (4306 AR)
- Jeska ascends
- Memnarch's madness on Mirrodin
- Mirrodin/Mirari saga
- **The Mending (4500 AR)** - rifts heal, planeswalkers lose godlike powers

### Modern Era (4500 AR-present)

#### Pre-Gatewatch ✅
- Lorwyn/Shadowmoor events (c. 4520)
- Alara shards reunite (4556 AR)
- Eldrazi released on Zendikar (4557 AR)
- Avacyn trapped in Helvault, released (4558 AR)
- Implicit Maze, Jace becomes Living Guildpact (4558 AR)

#### Gatewatch Period (4559-4562 AR) ✅

- Battle for Zendikar - Ulamog and Kozilek destroyed
- Emrakul sealed in Innistrad's moon (4559 AR)
- Kaladesh Revolt, Liliana joins Gatewatch (4560 AR)
- **Hour of Devastation on Amonkhet** (4560 AR)
  - Gatewatch defeated by Nicol Bolas
  - Nissa leaves
- Return to Dominaria, defeat Belzenlok (4560 AR)
- **War of the Spark** (4560 AR)
  - Massive planeswalker battle on Ravnica
  - Gideon dies saving Liliana
  - Nicol Bolas defeated and imprisoned
  - Niv-Mizzet becomes Living Guildpact

#### Post-Gatewatch ⚠️
- Eldraine - Oko's plot, Will and Rowan's sparks ignite (4560 AR)
- Theros - Elspeth escapes Underworld (4561 AR)
- Ikoria events (4561 AR)

#### New Phyrexian Invasion (4562 AR) ✅

  - Elesh Norn defeated by Karn
