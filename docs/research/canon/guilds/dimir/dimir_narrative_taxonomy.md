# House Dimir Narrative Taxonomy

## Core Axiom

**Information is power only after it has been hidden, stolen, reshaped, and used before anyone knows who moved it.**

## Identity Frame

House Dimir is **not merely blue-black control**. It is blue-black as hidden information architecture: secrecy, surveillance, infiltration, memory erasure, identity theft, and counterintelligence.

- **Blue component:** perfection through knowledge, planning, and hidden access.
- **Black component:** survival and dominance through agency, ambition, and exploitation.
- **Dimir synthesis:** power through controlled information asymmetry: know more, reveal less, move unseen, and make opponents pay for incomplete reality.

## Narrative Axes

| Axis | Meaning | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Secrecy | The true structure is hidden from outsiders and often from insiders. | hidden information, covert agents, need-to-know networks, encoded action |
| Surveillance | The field is watched before it is acted upon. | surveil, top-card control, informants, spy agents, reconnaissance fliers |
| Infiltration | Agents bypass normal defenses and carry encoded value. | unblockable, evasion, cipher, saboteur damage triggers, assassins |
| Memory Erasure | Opponent options are stripped from library, hand, graveyard, or name. | mill, discard, name/exile, lobotomy effects, psychic drain |
| Identity Theft | Enemy resources, faces, and futures are copied or stolen. | copy, cast from exile, graveyard copy, Doppelganger/Lazav effects, Thief/Specter effects |
| Counterintelligence | Actions fail because Dimir knows the plan first. | counterspells, discard before casting, sabotage, Shadow of Doubt, Thought Erasure |
| Covert Accretion | Small secrets become engines, payoffs, and inevitability. | surveil payoffs, mill payoffs, recursive enchantments, graveyard setup, mana infrastructure |

## Translation Function Read

### `Detain()`
Disappear, freeze, assassinate, displace, or silence what should not remain visible.

### `Override()`
Rewrite a plan through counterintelligence, theft, discard, name erasure, copying, or exact-tool substitution.

### `ConstraintField()`
Turn the board into a watched environment where safe routes narrow and agent routes open.

### `AccretionEngine()`
Convert secrets, surveil choices, milled cards, encoded hits, and stolen resources into compounding advantage.

## Voice

Dimir copy should feel quiet, precise, paranoid, elegant, predatory, coded, surgical, and untraceable.

Sample lines:

> You do not need to be faster than the table if you know what it will do before it does it.

> Dimir power feels like the missing page, the locked door, the helpful stranger, and the plan that was never yours.

> Every secret is a resource. Every resource is a route. Every route belongs to the House once it has been mapped.

## Shadow Expression

Dimir can become paranoia, manipulation for its own sake, isolation, over-control, and winning by making trust impossible.

Failure mode:

> The network becomes so hidden and self-protective that it cannot distinguish strategy from fear.

## Healthy Expression

Healthy Dimir is strategic patience, privacy, careful reading, resourcefulness, and the ability to solve danger before it becomes public damage.

Integration:

> Healthy Dimir uses secrecy as stewardship of fragile information, not merely as domination.

## UX Copy Seeds

- **Placement title:** House Dimir — The Hidden Hand
- **Short description:** You read the room by watching what others miss, keeping your own motives private, and turning information into leverage.
- **Maze prompt:** Find commanders that reward surveil, mill, discard, theft, evasive agents, graveyard setup, or hidden-route control.
- **Deck prompt:** Start with a covert engine: cheap interaction, surveillance/filtering, evasive carriers, graveyard or exile payoffs, then a quiet finisher.
