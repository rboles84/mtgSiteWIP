# Orzhov Syndicate Narrative Taxonomy

## Core Thesis
Orzhov is white-black power expressed as obligation: social order, spiritual authority, and personal ambition fused into contracts that outlive the body.

## Primary Axes
### Debt as Social Gravity
Every action creates obligation; every obligation can become control.

- Signals: extort, tithe, tax, payment, treasure, life drain, contract
- Representative cards: Blind Obedience, Syndic of Tithes, Smothering Tithe, Debt to the Deathless

### Afterlife as Labor System
Death is not freedom; it is a second phase of service, haunting, or collection.

- Signals: haunt, afterlife, spirits, ghost council, death triggers
- Representative cards: Belfry Spirit, Blind Hunter, Ministrant of Obligation, Teysa Karlov

### Hierarchy as Identity
Rank, title, office, and wealth determine personhood and permission.

- Signals: pontiff, oligarch, advokist, knight, basilica, guildgate, church
- Representative cards: Teysa, Orzhov Scion, Ghost Council of Orzhova, Imperious Oligarch, Syndicate Guildmage

### Guilt as Weapon
Confession, sin, penance, and absolution are spiritual tools used to extract leverage.

- Signals: castigate, confessor, penance, absolution, sin collector, punishment
- Representative cards: Castigate, High Priest of Penance, Vizkopa Confessor, Ethereal Absolution

### Life Ledger Conversion
Life is an account balance; their loss and your gain are paired transactions.

- Signals: lifelink, drain, life gain, vampire, Vizkopa, extort
- Representative cards: Vizkopa Guildmage, Tithe Drinker, Blood Baron of Vizkopa, Grasping Thrull

### Aristocrats and Asset Sacrifice
Expendable bodies are converted into cards, mana, protection, spirit labor, or tablewide drain.

- Signals: sacrifice, thrulls, tokens, aristocrat, death triggers, Teysa
- Representative cards: Cartel Aristocrat, Priest of Forgotten Gods, Maw of the Obzedat, Vindictive Vampire

## Archetypal Roles
### The Advokist
Turns wording, procedure, and technicality into power.
- Cards: Teysa Karlov, Teysa, Orzhov Scion, Teysa, Envoy of Ghosts

### The Pontiff
Speaks sacred authority that blesses insiders and diminishes outsiders.
- Cards: Orzhov Pontiff, Pontiff of Blight, Pitiless Pontiff

### The Collector
Extracts repeated small payments until the table is exhausted.
- Cards: Syndic of Tithes, Basilica Screecher, Smothering Tithe

### The Deathless Executive
Uses wealth and undeath to preserve authority beyond accountability.
- Cards: Ghost Council of Orzhova, Obzedat, Ghost Council, Debtors' Knell

### The Enforcer
Makes the contract physical when soft power fails.
- Cards: Orzhov Enforcer, Syndicate Enforcer, Executioner's Swing

### The Spirit Laborer
Continues service after death as a token, haunting, or recurring asset.
- Cards: Ministrant of Obligation, Debtors' Transport, Belfry Spirit

## Mechanical Taxonomy
- **haunt**: Past action attaches to future death, making consequence linger beyond the first body.
- **extort**: Every spell becomes a tithe event: small payments compound into inevitability.
- **afterlife**: Death produces spirit labor; the board keeps an echo of the original asset.
- **lifegain_drain**: White preservation and black extraction become a single accounting loop.
- **aristocrats**: Sacrifice is not waste; it is conversion of low-status assets into high-status advantage.
- **tax_prison**: Players retain agency but must pay through tempo, life, mana, or opportunity cost.
- **recursion**: The graveyard is an estate registry where useful assets can be reclaimed.

## Voice Guidelines
- Use: contract, debt, tithe, ledger, obligation, absolution, penance, estate, inheritance, rank, church, deal, ghost, spirit labor
- Avoid: neutral justice, pure civic law, heroic charity, random death magic, generic vampires, public democracy, pure spycraft
- Sentence shape: Elegant sentence, then a knife of transaction: “The blessing is real; so is the invoice.”

## Placement Read Template
- **Opening**: You do not see power as chaos; you see it as obligation with architecture.
- **Middle**: Where others see death, guilt, status, and payment as separate forces, Orzhov turns them into one durable machine.
- **Closing**: Your magic does not merely win the moment. It makes the moment owe you interest.
