# Simic Combine Narrative Taxonomy

Generated: 2026-05-18

## Core Thesis
Life is not a fixed state. Knowledge becomes ethical when it helps the organism, the city, or the ecosystem adapt before collapse arrives.

## Primary Conflict
Simic wants harmony with life, but pursues it through experimental optimization that can become cold, invasive, or unbounded.

## Taxonomy

### SIMIC-TAX-001 — The Biomancer
**Role:** Researcher who treats biology as editable language.

**Signals:** experiments, mutation, laboratory ethics, creature tutoring, card selection

**Gameplay Mapping:** Master Biomancer, Prime Speaker Vannifar, Momir Vig, Simic Visionary

**UX Mapping:** Use for explanations of how a placement turns observation into practical deckbuilding choices.

**Gameplay Mapping:** Hydroid Krasis, Sharktocrab, Drakewing Krasis, Elusive Krasis

## Voice Rules
- Tone: curious, clinical, calm, slightly uncanny, optimistic but not naive
- Sentence shape: Prefer cause-effect language: observe → adapt → compound → emerge.
- Avoid: random slime jokes only, generic nature harmony without science, pure blue control framing, pure green instinct framing
