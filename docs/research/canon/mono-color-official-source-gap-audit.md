# Mono Color Official Source Gap Audit

Audit date: 2026-06-12 America/Denver

Related card: `docs/kanban/done/VM-361-mono-color-official-source-inventory.md`

## Scope

This audit starts from `docs/research/canon/mono-color-reference-audit.md`, but treats that file as a path guide, not as the final claim ledger.

The question answered here is narrower:

- What local repo material is official enough to support future W/U/B/R/G mono-color claim work?
- What local material is reference, synthesis, or discovery only?
- What official online source material still needs to be captured before mono-color raw packets or claim ledgers can be treated as source-bound?

This file does not promote any mono-color identity claim into final authority. It is an intake map for future source-packet and ledger work.

## Authority Boundary

Recent source-authority decisions still apply:

- `docs/kanban/done/VM-325-source-bound-gold-standard-rule.md` requires claims to stay bound to source evidence.
- `docs/kanban/done/VM-335-mono-source-authority-decision.md` says the active mono-color registry and architecture docs are not enough by themselves for claim evidence.
- `docs/reference/source-generated-guardrails.md` says generated/runtime files should not be edited or treated as source of truth when source docs exist.
- `docs/reference/identity-metaphysics-markdown-schema.md` permits mono architecture docs as active narrative interfaces, but they are not raw source packets unless a future card promotes them through evidence work.

## Short Verdict

The repo has useful official local material for the color pie, color relationships, mechanical color-pie history, current color rules, and Commander color-identity boundaries.

The repo does not yet have source-bound mono-color raw packets or claim ledgers for white, blue, black, red, and green. `data/raw-factions/` currently has a colorless raw packet set, but no mono W/U/B/R/G raw packet set.

The strongest local official captures are Mark Rosewater Making Magic articles and the colorless official rule/source captures. The weakest local mono-specific captures are the five Drive to Work transcript files: they contain official podcast content in intent, but the local text is noisy, interleaved with unrelated transcript content, and appears to be sourced from a third-party transcript archive rather than an official Wizards article page.

For future mono-color claim work, the repo should fetch clean official captures for the 2015 Revisited series, the 2025 My Words series, The Council of Colors Revisited, and Mechanical Color Pie 2021 / Mechanical Color Pie 2021 Changes before writing final mono claim ledgers.

## Recommended Intake Sequence

1. Create a mono source-intake ledger that labels each source as claim-bearing, support-only, discovery-only, shaping-only, or stale/historical.
2. Capture the five 2015 Revisited articles as the first claim-bearing mono philosophy layer.
3. Capture the five 2025 My Words articles as the current first-person mono philosophy layer.
4. Capture Mechanical Color Pie 2021 and Mechanical Color Pie 2021 Changes before making current mechanical claims.
5. Capture The Council of Colors, Revisited as the color-pie governance/evolution source.
6. Decide whether Drive to Work transcripts are worth retaining; if yes, recapture the relevant episodes with clean provenance and do not rely on the current noisy transcript files.
7. Build W/U/B/R/G mono raw packets and claim ledgers from those captured sources.
8. Only after ledgers exist, reconcile `docs/architecture/colors/*` and `data/identity-layers.json` against the ledgers.
