# Canon Inventory and Four-Color Reference Audit

Date: 2026-05-31

This audit inventories every file under `docs/research/canon` and flags exact references to these four-color targets:

- Names: `Yore`, `Glint`, `Dune`, `Ink`, `Witch`
- Codes: `WUBR`, `UBRG`, `BRGW`, `RGWU`, `GWUB`

## Pre-flight context

- Four-color identities originate from the Ravnica Nephilim cycle and were later formalized for Commander product in *Commander 2016*.
- Each four-color combination is defined by the one color it excludes, and that exclusion is the philosophically meaningful element—not the four colors present.
- The Nephilim names (Yore-Tiller, Glint-Eye, Dune-Brood, Ink-Treader, Witch-Maw) provide canonical flavor anchors; the Commander 2016 names (Breya, Yidris, Saskia, Kynaios & Tiro, Atraxa) provide mechanical archetypes.
- Four-color canon has no dedicated Rosewater article series equivalent to the three-color series. Research draws primarily from flavor text, commander product context, and color-philosophy inference.
- This report is intentionally stored outside `docs/research/canon` so the canon source tree remains untouched.

## Four-Color Combinations (5)

### {W}{U}{B}{R} Artifice (Yore)

Four-color combination excluding green representing civilization, technology, and artifice completely divorced from nature's influence. White-blue-black-red philosophy combining structure, knowledge, ambition, and passion while explicitly rejecting natural growth creates identity valuing progress through artificial means. Named "Yore" referencing ancient secrets, lost civilizations, and knowledge from forgotten eras—suggesting that greatest technological achievements belong to past waiting rediscovery.

**Nephilim anchor:** Yore-Tiller Nephilim — *"When it awoke, the worms of the earth hissed in a chorus of beckoning."* Suggests resurrection, disturbing buried secrets, and awakening things best left sleeping.

**Commander anchor:** Breya, Etherium Shaper — artifact animation and etherium manipulation; also Kykar and various artifact-focused commanders.

**Mechanical identity:** Artifact synergies representing technological advancement; reanimation from graveyard symbolizing recovery of lost knowledge; control strategies through superior artificial tools.

**Philosophy:** Values progress over tradition, intelligence applied to creation, ambition driving innovation, passionate pursuit of perfection, and structured organization of resources. Completely rejects natural limitations, organic growth, acceptance of death, and working within nature's bounds.

**Color exclusion significance:** Green's absence symbolizes rejecting nature's wisdom, acceptance of natural cycles, and working with ecosystem. Represents peak humanoid civilization achieving godlike power through technology—Esper's etherium augmentation, Thran powerstones, and Phyrexian biomechanical perfection all exemplify this philosophy taken to extreme.

**Commander format:** Enables artifact-heavy strategies with access to best artifact tutors (blue/black), removal (white/black/red), and card advantage (blue).

**Symbol:** Metal, circuits, and ancient machinery representing civilization's pinnacle divorced from natural world.

### {U}{B}{R}{G} Chaos (Glint)

Four-color combination excluding white representing disorder, selfishness, and complete rejection of law, morality, and community structure. Blue-black-red-green philosophy combining knowledge, ambition, emotion, and growth while explicitly rejecting order creates identity valuing individual freedom and self-interest absolutely. Named "Glint" suggesting greed, materialism, and momentary flashes of desire driving behavior—wealth's seductive shine, power's allure, and selfish acquisition's appeal.

**Nephilim anchor:** Glint-Eye Nephilim — *"When it awoke, it shook the plane with the thunder of its craving."* Suggests insatiable hunger, desire consuming everything, and need that cannot be satisfied.

**Commander anchor:** Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder — cascade chaos; also Omnath, Locus of Creation demonstrating multi-color ramping greed and various chaos-matters commanders.

**Mechanical identity:** Cascading effects representing plans spiraling out of control; greed-based card advantage through accumulation; chaotic value generation through unpredictable interactions.

**Philosophy:** Embraces knowledge for personal power, ambition without ethical limits, emotion unrestrained by community, and natural growth serving self. Completely rejects law's constraints, moral limitations, community obligations, and societal structure.

**Color exclusion significance:** White's absence symbolizes rejecting peace through order, community over self, and structured civilization. Represents ultimate individualism where every person pursues desires without regard for others—Jund's survival-of-fittest taken further by adding intelligence (blue) and removing any trace of community or law.

**Commander format:** Provides access to best ramp (green), card draw (blue/black), and chaotic effects (red). Enables strategies accumulating resources selfishly and creating chaos benefiting controller.

**Symbol:** Treasure, flames, and wild growth representing unrestrained desire consuming everything.

### {B}{R}{G}{W} Aggression (Dune)

Four-color combination excluding blue representing physical might, direct action, and anti-intellectualism through immediate force. Black-red-green-white philosophy combining ambition, passion, strength, and community while explicitly rejecting contemplation creates identity valuing action over thought, present over future, and doing over planning. Named "Dune" suggesting desert warfare, harsh environments demanding immediate response, and survival through direct confrontation rather than clever maneuvering.

**Nephilim anchor:** Dune-Brood Nephilim — *"When it awoke, it spawned nameless thousands to herald its arrival."* Suggests overwhelming armies, quantity over quality, and victory through sheer numbers.

**Commander anchor:** Saskia the Unyielding — aggressive multi-target combat; also Tana/Tymna partner pairing enabling token generation with value extraction and various go-wide commanders.

**Mechanical identity:** Token generation representing armies overwhelming through numbers; aggressive combat rewarding constant attacking; going-wide strategies multiplying modest threats into devastating force.

**Philosophy:** Values ambition manifesting through conquest, passion driving immediate action, natural strength deployed directly, and community bonds forged in battle. Completely rejects intellectual planning, patient calculation, long-term strategy, and clever maneuvering.

**Color exclusion significance:** Blue's absence symbolizes rejecting intellectualism, patience, planning ahead, and solving problems through superior information rather than superior force. Represents pure martial philosophy where problems are solved through overwhelming force—if violence doesn't solve the issue, insufficient violence was applied.

**Commander format:** Provides access to best aggressive creatures (red/green), efficient removal (white/black), and token generation (white/green). Enables strategies deploying threats faster than opponents can answer.

**Symbol:** Armies, weapons, and desert sands representing conquest through superior numbers and relentless assault.

### {R}{G}{W}{U} Altruism (Ink)

Four-color combination excluding black representing selflessness, community benefit, and complete rejection of individual ambition for greater good. Red-green-white-blue philosophy combining passion, growth, community, and knowledge while explicitly rejecting self-interest creates identity valuing collective prosperity and shared success. Named "Ink" suggesting knowledge recorded for others' benefit, information preserved for future generations, and wisdom shared rather than hoarded—libraries open to all, teachers dedicating lives to students' success, and discoveries made public immediately.

**Nephilim anchor:** Ink-Treader Nephilim — *"When it awoke, the mirrors of the world reflected only darkness."* Suggests sacrifice so complete that self disappears, individual identity subsumed into collective, and self-effacement as ultimate virtue.

**Commander anchor:** Kynaios and Tiro of Meletis — group-hug strategies where everyone benefits and opponents given resources freely; various symmetrical-effect commanders.

**Mechanical identity:** Symmetrical effects benefiting all players equally; group-benefit abilities providing resources to opponents; communal growth strategies where everyone prospers together.

**Philosophy:** Values passion channeled toward helping others, growth shared among community, community needs over individual wants, and knowledge distributed freely. Completely rejects personal ambition, hoarding resources, prioritizing self, and seeking individual advancement at others' expense.

**Color exclusion significance:** Black's absence symbolizes rejecting power-seeking, self-interest, ambition, and individual achievement over collective good. Represents ultimate altruism—Selesnya's community taken further by adding knowledge-sharing (blue) and removing any trace of self-interest. Demonstrates belief that shared prosperity creates more total happiness than wealth concentrated in few.

**Commander format:** Creates political dynamics where opponents are hesitant to attack the benefactor. Positions controller to capitalize on group growth while drawing more than opponents through asymmetric symmetrical effects.

**Symbol:** Shared meals, open libraries, and communal gardens representing prosperity through cooperation.

### {G}{W}{U}{B} Growth (Witch)

Four-color combination excluding red representing patient development, calculated expansion, and complete suppression of emotion and impulse. Green-white-blue-black philosophy combining growth, community, knowledge, and ambition while explicitly rejecting passion creates identity valuing methodical advancement and cold rationality. Named "Witch" suggesting mysterious knowledge applied to transformation, patient schemes spanning years, and power gained through calculated ritual rather than passionate conquest—witches stirring cauldrons awaiting perfect moment, nature magic channeled through precise methodology, and transformation achieved through patient work.

**Nephilim anchor:** Witch-Maw Nephilim — *"When it awoke, it shattered the hillsides to make way for its passage."* Suggests inevitable unstoppable growth, slow but inexorable expansion, and patient force that cannot be hurried or halted.

**Commander anchor:** Atraxa, Praetors' Voice — proliferate mechanics and +1/+1 counter strategies embodying slow accumulating power; various scaling growth commanders.

**Mechanical identity:** +1/+1 counters accumulating over time; incremental advantage building exponentially; scaling growth strategies where modest starts become overwhelming finishes.

**Philosophy:** Values growth through patient cultivation, community organized for efficient expansion, knowledge applied to optimization, and ambition pursued through methodical planning. Completely rejects impulsive action, emotional decision-making, hasty risks, and unplanned spontaneity.

**Color exclusion significance:** Red's absence symbolizes rejecting emotion, spontaneity, living in the moment, and action without planning. Represents calculated domination through superior planning and patience—Simic's evolution plus Orzhov's long-term schemes plus Azorius's structured growth minus any emotional impulse. Demonstrates belief that slow inevitability defeats quick passion and methodical expansion achieves more than explosive growth.

**Commander format:** Provides access to best ramp (green), card advantage (blue/black), removal (white/black), and scaling payoffs across all four colors. Enables strategies protecting investment until critical mass is achieved, then transitioning inevitable advantage into victory through proliferate and scaling.

**Symbol:** Patient gardens, calculated experiments, and inevitable growth representing methodical expansion crushing opposition through superior planning.
