# Glint Reliability Audit

Status: VM-276 reliability audit

## Reliability Summary

Glint now has a workable repo-local source base for a conservative live lane, but its claim-bearing floor remains thinner than mature guild or shard packets. VM-276 adds exact local card-data validation for `Glint-Eye Nephilim` and `Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder`, while preserving the existing boundaries: Glint is the UBRG, non-White four-color lane; Glint-Eye remains the main card anchor; Yidris and `Entropic Uprising` remain support-only Commander texture; and none of those anchors become institutions or proof of a universal Glint doctrine.

## Current Safe Floor

- Glint is Vox Mana's reserved UBRG/non-White public expression and research label, not a settled official MTG faction name.
- `Chaos` is a Commander 2016 theme alias for the same color quadruple and must not be presented as an official exclusive color-combo name.
- Glint is framed by absent White.
- Glint-Eye Nephilim can be used as an anchor card, not as a faction or institution.
- Exact local card-data validation is available for Glint-Eye Nephilim and Yidris, but it remains support-only.
- Yidris and `Entropic Uprising` can support cascade/chaos Commander texture, not prove Glint lore.
- Detailed worldview language may be written only as support-bound framing or clearly labeled Vox Mana synthesis.

## Reliability Risks

- Overclaiming Glint as an official MTG faction, plane, or institution.
- Overclaiming Glint or Chaos as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for UBRG.
- Treating Glint-Eye Nephilim as the founder of a coherent doctrine or civilization.
- Treating Yidris or `Entropic Uprising` as proof that all cascade or chaos decks are Glint.
- Treating the Commander JSONL row as lore, legality, or raw-placement proof.
- Treating support-only local card-data validation as raw-claim authority by itself.
- Treating `Magic Four-Color Identity Dossier.md` or `cross-color-dynamics.md` as direct claim authority without role labeling.
- Treating unmanaged Glint draft files as approved evidence because they already contain citations.
- Adding runtime aliases from UBRG permutations during source work.

## Downstream Readiness

VM-276 is ready for bounded raw/profile reconciliation and, if those raw/profile changes justify it, conservative generated/live-copy cleanup. It is not a license for route work, Home preview changes, `UBRG` alias promotion, Commander-directory unsuppression, or broader canon inflation.
