## Product Copy Seed

Glint is the four-color pressure of appetite without civic restraint. Blue studies the storm, Black takes what it can from it, Red rides the surge, and Green lets the living world grow wild around the blast zone. White is absent, so the pattern does not settle into law, duty, or shared peace; it becomes velocity, hunger, and improvisation.
