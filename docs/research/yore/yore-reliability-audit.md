Status: VM-240 reliability audit

## Reliability Summary

Yore has a useful local source base, but it is not yet ready for architecture, raw-packet, or runtime use. The source packet is strong enough to establish a guarded research frame: Yore is the WUBR, non-Green four-color lane; Yore-Tiller Nephilim and Breya are the main card/Commander anchors; and Nephilim must remain anchors rather than institutions.

## Current Safe Floor

- Yore is Vox Mana's reserved WUBR/non-Green public expression label, not a settled official MTG color-combo name.
- Yore is framed by absent Green.
- Yore/Artifice language may discuss civilization, technology, artificial progress, artifact texture, and graveyard/reanimation support.
- Yore-Tiller Nephilim can be used as an anchor card, not as a faction.
- Breya/Invent Superiority can support artifact Commander texture, not prove Yore lore.

## Reliability Risks

- Overclaiming Yore as an official MTG faction, plane, or institution.
- Overclaiming Yore or Artifice as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for WUBR.
- Treating Cult of Yore as the same thing as the future Vox Mana Yore identity.
- Treating the generated WUBR terminal as evidence.
- Treating Commander rows as lore, legality, or raw-placement proof.
- Treating all WUBR artifact/reanimation decks as Yore by default.
- Adding runtime aliases from WUBR permutations during source work.
