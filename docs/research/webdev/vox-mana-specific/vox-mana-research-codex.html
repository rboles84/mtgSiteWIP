Volume I — Edges of the Color Pie

Where five colors meet,
and where no color follows.

Vox Mana is a research console for color identity, faction philosophy, and mana topology across the Magic multiverse. This codex maps the two outermost territories of the color pie — full inclusion and non-coloration — and treats them not as opposites but as questions the pie answers in different keys.
