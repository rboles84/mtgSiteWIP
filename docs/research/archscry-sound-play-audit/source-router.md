# Archscry Sound/Play Canon Research Source Router

Status: preliminary authority gate complete; stop before the 119-row Sound/Play audit

Scope: all 37 Vox Mana identities

Prepared: 2026-08-16

Related card: [VM-560](../../kanban/done/VM-560-archscry-sound-play-source-router.md)

## Purpose and hard stop

This router assigns every identity a defensible path from the repository's reference audits to the current underlying Vox Mana and official/external sources. It exists only to govern the later 37 Identity Evidence Packets and 119-row Sound/Play review.

It is not a new semantic authority, claim ledger, prose approval, or replacement for the certified raw faction packets. It does not authorize rewriting Sound/Play text. The audits listed below are navigation and authority-classification aids; they do not prove a substantive claim when the underlying source is available.

The next review must open the cited underlying source and verify the exact claim at the correct evidence altitude. If the route below identifies a gap, the reviewer must use `SOURCE_INTAKE_REQUIRED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `INSUFFICIENT_VOX_MANA_AUTHORITY`, or `CONFLICT_REQUIRES_OWNER` rather than infer around it.

## Authority order for the later review

1. The current certified `data/raw-factions/<identity>/*.claims.json` packet records the approved Vox Mana semantic claim set. Its paired `*.sources.json` records the source roles and routes. The raw packet remains frozen unless separately reopened under the governing semantic workflow.
2. Identity source/evidence/manual-fill ledgers control how their underlying files may be used. A resolved path does not erase a role such as support-only, shaping-only, discovery-only, comparator-only, or lifecycle-only.
3. Actual Wizards/Mark Rosewater captures and pages control official color philosophy, setting, lore, rules, mechanics, product, and card facts within the source's stated scope.
4. Vox Mana taxonomies, dossiers, architecture prose, and synthesis may shape player-facing interpretation only when their role is explicit and their statements remain inside the approved semantic/evidence boundary.
5. Scryfall records may control canonical card facts under the repository's governed card-data policy. They do not prove card-to-identity resonance, faction voice, or an identity philosophy.
6. Generated/runtime artifacts, rendered dossiers, the review workbook, reference audits, handoffs, and Kanban records can prove state, provenance, or workflow. They cannot prove the underlying MTG or identity claim.

## Allowed route outcomes

- `STRONG_EVIDENCE`: the identity has a current certified Vox Mana claim route plus strong identity-specific official or governed evidence. Individual claims can still fail if they exceed that scope.
- `SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`: a defensible route exists, but specific lore, chronology, official capture, or interpretation boundaries remain active.
- `VOX_MANA_SYNTHESIS`: the usable identity philosophy is explicitly Vox Mana synthesis; official sources can support bounded facts or design context but do not establish one canonical ideology.
- `SOURCE_INTAKE_REQUIRED`: use at claim level when a required primary source is identified but not governed/captured strongly enough for the proposed statement.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: use at claim level when neither current official evidence nor approved Vox Mana evidence entails the statement.
- `CONFLICT_REQUIRES_OWNER`: use when current controlling sources or approved product decisions materially conflict and classification cannot resolve the conflict.

`INSUFFICIENT_VOX_MANA_AUTHORITY` is also retained as the required Selesnya-specific claim result when official facts exist but the proposed Vox Mana-specific interpretation has no approved owner.
