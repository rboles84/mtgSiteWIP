## Research Standard

This package applies a strict source hierarchy and separates information into these categories:

- **Confirmed Canon:** Directly stated in an official Wizards story, mechanics article, plane page, or official product source.
- **Card-Supported:** Directly supported by official card text through Gatherer or Scryfall.
- **Rules-Supported:** Directly supported by official release notes, mechanics articles, or the Comprehensive Rules.
- **Official Summary:** Stated in an official Wizards summary or design article.
- **Repository Archive Supported:** Supported by a local or archived copy of official story text.
- **Supported Interpretation:** Reasonable synthesis of cited evidence, labeled as interpretation and not presented as fact.
- **Alara Context:** Relevant to Alara broadly, not necessarily unique to Bant.
- **Bant-Adjacent Discovery:** Appears in secondary sources but is not promoted unless bounded by the approved packet.
- **Unverified / Not Found:** Could not be confirmed in reviewed materials.
