## Identity Floor

Jeskai's approved color identity begins with the MaRo three-color article. `JESKAI-SRC-001` establishes Jeskai as the Blue-centered White/Blue/Red wedge, with Blue focused on perfection through knowledge, Red on freedom/action, and White on peace/structure. The same source identifies Jeskai's core attribute as cunning and supports a mechanics vocabulary around knowledge, proactive control, trickery, spell interaction, card selection, tempo, protection, damage prevention/redirection, direct damage, copying/redirecting spells, and prowess.

Vox Mana synthesis: Jeskai can be described later as disciplined Blue-centered cunning coordinating Red action and White structure. That phrasing is approved only as synthesis from `JESKAI-EVID-001`, `JESKAI-EVID-002`, and `JESKAI-EVID-003`.

## Khans-Era Jeskai Way

`JESKAI-SRC-002` is the main Khans-era source. It supports Jeskai Way as a clan of martial artists, mystics, and wandering warriors seeking discovery and enlightenment. It also supports strongholds, elders, training, honor, respect, discipline, enlightenment, industriousness, traditionalism, the Annals, Rules of Reeds, Ways, fires, and dragon-cunning reverence.

Approved claims:

- Jeskai reveres dragon cunning.
- Jeskai has a monastery/stronghold culture in the eastern mountains and lake-top locations.
- Jeskai social practice includes discipline, enlightenment, artisanship/industriousness, and traditionalism.
- The three Ways exist as artisan, mystic, and wandering warrior paths.
- Fire-language and ghostfire material exist in the local source.
