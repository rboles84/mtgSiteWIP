## Safe Source Spine

Use this order when drafting future Jeskai docs:

1. `JESKAI-SRC-001` for Blue-centered Jeskai color identity, cunning, and broad mechanics vocabulary.
2. `JESKAI-SRC-002` for Khans-era Jeskai Way culture, strongholds, Ways, fires, Narset, and clan practice.
3. `JESKAI-SRC-003` for Fate Reforged/past Jeskai and Shu Yun/Ojutai context.
4. `JESKAI-SRC-004` for Khanfall story events and the Ojutai discontinuity.
5. `JESKAI-SRC-005` for Dragons-era Ojutai and Narset boundary.
6. `JESKAI-SRC-006` for Dragonstorm-era revived Jeskai.
7. `JESKAI-SRC-008` and `JESKAI-CMD-001` through `JESKAI-CMD-011` for support-only Commander discovery language.
8. `JESKAI-SRC-015` through `JESKAI-SRC-017` only as discovery-only seed material.
