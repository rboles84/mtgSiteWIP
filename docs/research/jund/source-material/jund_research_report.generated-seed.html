JUND

MTG Deep Research Report

1. Executive Summary & Definition

This section provides a high-level overview of Jund, distinguishing its canonical origins on the plane of Alara from its broader mechanical and player-driven identity in modern Magic: The Gathering.

The Best Short Definition

"Jund is the black-red-green shard of Alara, a harsh, volcanic world defined by the absence of white's order and blue's logic. It is a predatory ecology built entirely on impulse, instinct, and a ruthless 'eat-or-be-eaten' hierarchy where dragons rule as apex predators. In player culture, 'Jund' has transcended this lore to become shorthand for a resource-attrition, midrange gameplay strategy defined by efficient threats and universal answers."

Core Findings

  • ►
    Alara Canon: Jund is a volcanic, jungle-covered shard where survival of the fittest is the only law. It is dominated by dragons who sit at the top of an inescapable food chain.
  • ►
    Red-Centered Philosophy: According to Mark Rosewater, Jund is driven by Red's desire for action and impulse, utilizing Green's instinctual natural order and Black's amoral selfishness to survive.
  • ►
    The Void of Order: The complete absence of White and Blue means Jund has no civilization, no concept of law, no reflection, and no abstract intellectualism. Everything is visceral.
  • ►
    Mechanical Identity: Officially represented by the "Devour" mechanic (eating one's own to grow stronger). It heavily features sacrifice as a resource conversion tool.
  • ►
    Player Culture Overlap: In Constructed formats, "Jund" describes a midrange attrition archetype ("Jund 'em out") that focuses on individual card quality rather than synergy. Many popular "Jund" Commanders (like Prossh) are frequently misattributed to the plane of Alara despite originating elsewhere.

Research Gaps & Uncertainties

Post-Conflux integration details remain sparse. How the rigid predator/prey hierarchy of Jund fully adapted to encountering the structured military of Bant or the undead hordes of Grixis is largely told only in fragments. Furthermore, the daily existence of non-warrior classes on Jund is almost entirely undocumented, leaving it uncertain how basic societal reproduction occurs outside of combat.

2. Jund as a World (Alara Lore)

This section explores the physical and societal structure of the shard of Jund, built strictly upon official Shards of Alara lore, planeswalker guides, and set materials.

The Environment

Jund is a world of jagged peaks, active volcanoes, toxic tar pits, and dense, suffocating jungles. The air is thick with ash and the smell of blood. It is a primal, unshaped world where tectonic violence mirrors the violence of its inhabitants.

The Food Chain

Society does not exist; only ecology. The hierarchy is absolute and based purely on physical power. Dragons sit at the apex. Below them are Viashino, humans, and goblins. Everyone is a hunter, and everyone is prey. The "Life Hunt" is the central driving force of daily existence.

Absence of White & Blue

Without White, there is no empathy, no law, no community, and no peace. Without Blue, there is no forethought, no planning, no intellectual curiosity, and no illusion. Jund operates purely in the present moment, driven by hunger and immediate survival.

Named Locations

The Jund Panorama: The vast volcanic mountain ranges. Tar Pits: Natural traps where predators wait for struggling prey. Note: Kher Ridges is often associated with Jund colors due to Prossh, but is canonically on Dominaria.

The Denizens of Jund

Dragons (Apex): The undisputed rulers of Jund. They do not govern; they consume. They feed on everything below them. They are fueled by the intense volcanic mana of the plane.
Viashino (Predators): Lizard-folk who thrive in the volcanic heat. They are cunning pack-hunters, highly adapted to the harsh terrain, often preying on humans and goblins.
Humans (Survivors): Divided into warrior clans, they revere strength and view death in battle as the only worthy end. Shamans weave magic from death and blood to strengthen their warriors.
Goblins (Bottom Feeders): The bottom of the sentient food chain. They survive through sheer numbers, rapid reproduction, and scavenging, often serving as fodder for larger predators.

3. Red-Centered Color Philosophy

Drawing heavily from Mark Rosewater's "Making Magic" articles, this section breaks down how Jund functions conceptually as a Black-Red-Green identity anchored by Red.

The Core of Jund: Red's Impulse

Mark Rosewater explicitly designed the Shards around a central color flanked by its two allies. Jund is Red-centered. Red is the color of emotion, action, impulse, and freedom.

In Jund, Red's desire to act without restraint is taken to its absolute extreme. There is no waiting, no deliberating. You act, or you are eaten. Red provides the explosive, chaotic energy that drives the plane's endless cycle of violence and volcanic eruptions.

  • Black's Contribution: Adds pure selfishness, amorality, and a willingness to kill or sacrifice others for personal gain. It removes any moral hesitation from Red's impulses.
  • Green's Contribution: Provides the framework of the "natural order" (the food chain), instinct, and growth. It grounds Red's chaos into a brutal, inescapable ecological system.

What Jund Loses Without White and Blue

The defining feature of a shard is its missing enemy colors. Without White, Jund lacks structure, altruism, laws, and peace. Without Blue, it lacks reflection, study, artifice, and patience. The result is a world completely devoid of civilization, existing in a perpetual state of immediate, visceral reality.

4. Mechanics & Gameplay Identity

Jund's identity is split between its canonical mechanical debut in Shards of Alara and its evolved, player-driven identity in Constructed formats.

Alara Lore Mechanics

Devour

The signature mechanic of Alara Jund. As a creature enters the battlefield, you may sacrifice any number of creatures to put +1/+1 counters on it. This perfectly encapsulates the "eat-or-be-eaten" lore. You feed the weak to the strong to create an apex predator.

Sacrifice as Resource

Cards frequently require sacrificing creatures or lands to deal damage, draw cards, or generate mana. This reflects the expendable nature of life and resources in the Jund food chain.

Constructed / "Modern" Identity

Midrange Attrition

In competitive Magic (esp. Modern), "Jund" means a deck that seeks to trade resources 1-for-1 using efficient removal (Lightning Bolt, Fatal Push) and hand disruption (Thoughtseize), relying on standalone powerful threats (Tarmogoyf, Bloodbraid Elf) to win a topdeck war.

"Jund 'em Out"

A community phrase meaning to strip an opponent of all synergy and resources, dragging them into a topdeck battle where the sheer individual power of Jund cards ensures victory. This is a gameplay interpretation of "survival of the fittest," not Alara lore.

6. Themes & Community Interpretation

Analyzing the perceived themes of Jund and how strongly they are backed by official lore versus how they have been molded by the player community.

Scale 1-5: How strongly a theme is supported by the source type.

Theme: "Eat or Be Eaten" (Predation)

Support: Universal. Canon explicitly builds the world on this. Design embedded it in Devour. Players identify it instantly with BRG creature strategies.

Theme: "Jund Goodstuff / Value Engines"

Support: Player/Community Driven. Lore Jund is not about accumulating incremental "value" or card advantage; it is about explosive, high-risk violence. "Value Jund" is entirely a product of competitive Constructed formats.

Why Players Enjoy Jund

According to community hubs (r/EDH, Modern MTG content), players love the feeling of inevitability and interacting with every part of the board. It feels "fair" but powerful. It appeals to players who want to win through combat math, resource denial, and overwhelming physical board presence rather than infinite combos or stack interaction.

What NOT To Overclaim (Guidelines for Accuracy)

  • Do not claim Kresh the Bloodbraided is the "leader" or "king" of Jund. Jund has no central government.
  • Do not claim Prossh or Korvold are from Alara.
  • Do not equate Modern Jund (Thoughtseize/Goyf) to the lore of Alara Jund. One is about calculated resource trading; the other is explosive survivalism.
  • Do not treat Reddit/EDHREC discussions as story canon.
