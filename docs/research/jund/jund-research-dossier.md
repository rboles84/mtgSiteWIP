## Identity Floor

Jund is the BRG Alara shard with Red as its center/design lens. The approved identity floor comes from `Jund_Following Your Heart`, supported by the normalized canon inventory.

The safest Jund authoring frame is:

- Jund is BRG.
- Jund is Red-centered.
- Red's frame emphasizes being true to the self, following gut instinct, honoring emotion, and resisting imposed restraint.
- Black supports Red through self-interest, personal needs, contentment, and opposition to White's control.
- Green supports Red through unrestrained instinct, less overthinking, and feral force.
- White and Blue are absent enemy-color pressures in the design frame.

Evidence rows: `JUND-EVID-001` through `JUND-EVID-011`.
