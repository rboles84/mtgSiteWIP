FATE REFORGED ERA

Elite Dragon-Slayers of the Primeval Steppes

More than a thousand years in Tarkir's past, the plane was a volatile ecosystem continually producing dragonstorms. Under the leadership of the young human khan Alesha, the Mardu developed into high-mobility hunting units, utilizing coordinated cavalry charges, heavy ballistics, and tactical speed to ground and execute dragons.

// KEY HISTORICAL CHARACTERISTICS:

Meritocratic structure: any youth earns their "war name" through battlefield valor. Alesha claimed her identity at sixteen after slaying a dragon, accepted fully by her clan.

CODES OF THE HORDE: THE LEGAL EVOLUTION

COMPARING LAW CODES ACROSS TEMPORAL RECONSTRUCTIONS

ORIGINAL CODE

THE EDICTS OF ILAGRA

1. "TO CONQUER IS TO EAT"

Resources are scarce. Surviving the harsh elements requires constant raids against neighboring clans.

2. "TO RULE IS TO BLEED"

The leader must always be the strongest warrior, proving their right to govern through continuous acts of battlefield violence.

3. "VICTORY OR DEATH"

Mandates full, absolute commitment in battle. Demands suicidal self-sacrifice rather than a tactical retreat.

REFORMED CODE

THE DECREE OF THUNDER

1. "LOYALTY IS EARNED"

Commanders must continually demonstrate tactical competence and deep-seated commitment to their troops to maintain power.

2. "COMMUNITY IS STRENGTH"

The horde protects and preserves non-combatant labor classes. A single rider is nothing without the collective support.

3. "VICTORY IS SURVIVAL"

Rejects suicidal sacrifice. Victory is empty if it results in the total destruction of the clan's future population.
