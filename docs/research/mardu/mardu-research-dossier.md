# Mardu Research Dossier

Status: VM-223 conservative dossier. Mardu remains docs-only, non-live, and review-gated.

This dossier summarizes only the source-bound floor established by `mardu-source-ledger.md` and `mardu-evidence-ledger.md`. It is not an architecture document and must not be used to create raw-faction JSON, runtime keys, generated artifacts, routes, Maze behavior, Home previews, schemas, Supabase changes, builders, placement fixtures, or placement changes in VM-223.

## Identity Floor

Mardu is the red-white-black wedge in the approved MaRo source. Red is the design center, with White contributing structure and Black contributing opportunity. The source frames Mardu around speed: action, early pressure, coordinated attack, and the overlap of all three colors in aggressive tools.

VM-223 records `MARDU` as the docs key and future public key. `RWB` and `WBR` are metadata/query-only context, not runtime aliases or public keys.

## Khans-Era Floor

The Khans-era Mardu Horde emulates the dragon wing and centers speed, brutality, honor, war, raiding, archery, war magic, and mounted force. Its social floor includes the Edicts of Ilagra, war names, khan authority, dragon-slayer pride, seminomadic raiding economics, labor classes, dakla archery, concealment magic, war shrieks, wind magic, and battlefield roles such as archers, shamans, riders, hordechiefs, wranglers, and feudmasters.

Zurgo Helmsmasher is the Khans-era khan anchor. Safe Khans-era location anchors include Wingthrone, Goldengrave, The Scour, and Screamreach.

## Fate Reforged And Alesha Floor

Fate Reforged material supports ancient Mardu as feared battle-focused raiders with speed as their highest value and Kolaghan as the dragon they honor. Alesha is supported as the young Mardu khan, rider, archer, swordfighter, and story anchor.

`The Truth of Names` supports Alesha leading Mardu against Kolaghan dragons, war-name culture, and the source-bound identity/name scene. `Khanfall` supports Alesha attending the khan summit and choosing to listen despite Mardu rank-and-file pressure.

Detailed biographies for Alesha, Zurgo, Ankleshank/Vial Smasher, Zurgo Stormrender, Dalkovan generals, and modern Mardu figures remain `Manual fill required` unless later cards capture direct official sources.

## Kolaghan Boundary

Kolaghan clan is not the same as Khans-era Mardu Horde. It is a Dragons-era dragonlord clan marked by the crave, little internal structure, no official rank, lightning speed, and Dragonlord Kolaghan's destructive rule. Zurgo Bellstriker and Vial Smasher are Kolaghan-era contrast anchors, not proof that Khans-era Mardu works the same way.

Use Kolaghan material as contrast, suppression, and timeline boundary unless a row explicitly makes a narrower bridge.

## Modern Dragonstorm Floor

Modern Dragonstorm-era Mardu are restored after Kolaghan rule, Phyrexian-invasion losses, rebellion, and re-unification. This Mardu is not simply the old Horde restored. It is organized around the khan, Dalkovan Assembly, mobile camp-cities, intertwined military/civilian life, artisans, hunters, healers, revered blacksmiths, cavalry, infantry, siege engineers, hunting/trade, singing, sculpture, sports, the Decree of Thunder, Lightning Proving, stormsingers, clan dragons, dragonstorm mobility, and modern locations.

The Decree of Thunder and modern clan-dragon practices must be timeline-labeled. Do not backfill them into Khans-era Zurgo Mardu unless a later source explicitly supports that bridge.
