## Product Copy Seed

Vox Mana synthesis:

Dune is the four-color pressure of bodies already in motion. Black accepts the cost of conquest, Red strikes before doubt can settle in, Green trusts force and survival, and White turns many fighters into a line that can hold. Blue is absent, so the pattern does not pause to model every outcome; it drives forward, multiplies presence, and treats hesitation as danger.
