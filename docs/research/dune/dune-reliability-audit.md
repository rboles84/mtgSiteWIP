Dune has a workable repo-local source base for a guarded packet, but it is not yet ready for architecture, raw claims, or runtime use. ## Current Safe Floor

- Dune is Vox Mana's reserved BRGW/non-Blue public expression and research label, not a settled official MTG faction name.
- `Aggression` is a Commander 2016 theme alias for the same color quadruple and must not be presented as an official exclusive color-combo name.
- Dune is framed by absent Blue.
- Dune-Brood Nephilim can be used as an anchor card, not as a faction or institution.
- Saskia and `Open Hostility` can support combat-pressure Commander texture, not prove Dune lore.
- Tana/Tymna, Ravos, and other `Open Hostility` commanders can support decklist or play-pattern texture only.
- Detailed worldview language may be written only as support-bound framing or clearly labeled Vox Mana synthesis.

## Reliability Risks

- Overclaiming Dune as an official MTG faction, plane, or institution.
- Overclaiming Dune or Aggression as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for BRGW.
- Treating Dune-Brood Nephilim as the founder of a coherent doctrine or civilization.
- Treating Saskia, `Open Hostility`, partner commanders, or decklist texture as proof of Dune lore.
- Treating the Commander JSONL row or repo-local precon data as lore, legality, or raw-placement proof.
- Treating the discovery draft as approved evidence because it already contains polished prose and outbound references.
- Adding runtime aliases from BRGW, WBRG, or other permutations during source work.
