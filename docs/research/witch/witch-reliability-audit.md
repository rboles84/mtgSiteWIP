Status: VM-264 reliability audit

## Reliability Summary

Witch has a workable repo-local source base for a guarded packet, but it is not yet ready for architecture, raw claims, or runtime use. The source packet is strong enough to establish a careful research frame: `WITCH` is the `GWUB`, non-Red four-color lane; `Witch / Growth` is a bounded paired display frame rather than a settled official faction name; Witch-Maw Nephilim is the main card anchor; Atraxa plus `Breed Lethality` provide Commander texture around proliferate and counters; and those anchors must remain anchors rather than institutions, lore proof, or naming authority.

## Current Safe Floor

- `WITCH` is Vox Mana's reserved `GWUB` / non-Red public expression and research label, not a settled official MTG faction name.
- `Growth` is the Commander 2016 support/display alias for the same color quadruple and must not be presented as an official exclusive color-combo name.
- `GWUB` is the canonical metadata/query code for Witch, while `WUBG` may appear only when echoing Commander 2016 support-source order.
- Witch is framed by absent Red.
- Witch-Maw Nephilim can be used as an anchor card, not as a faction or institution.
- Atraxa and `Breed Lethality` can support proliferate, counters, scaling-growth, and patient-inevitability Commander texture, not prove Witch lore by themselves.
- Detailed worldview language may be written only as source-bound framing, support-bound framing, or clearly labeled Vox Mana synthesis.

## Reliability Risks

- Overclaiming Witch as an official MTG faction, plane, or institution.
- Overclaiming Witch or Growth as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for `GWUB`.
- Treating `WUBG` support-source order as a public alias or runtime key.
- Treating Witch-Maw Nephilim as the founder of a coherent doctrine or civilization.
- Treating Atraxa, `Breed Lethality`, or Commander/precon support rows as proof of Witch lore or naming authority.
- Treating the broad lore compendium as standalone Witch proof.
- Treating the discovery drafts as approved evidence because they already contain polished prose, rankings, live-stat language, and strategy ladders.
- Collapsing Witch into Atraxa alone, New Phyrexia alone, or generic proliferate/counters decks without separator work.
