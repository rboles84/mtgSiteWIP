## Identity Floor

Temur is the Green-centered Green-Blue-Red wedge in the approved MaRo source. Its design attribute is savagery, expressed through physical toughness, instinct, inner strength, mental fortitude, impulse, action, and the practical will to win a fight. Green supplies acceptance, natural connection, growth, and mana; Blue supplies knowledge, thought, and tactical understanding; Red supplies freedom, emotion, speed, and action.

## Khans-Era Floor

The Khans-era Temur Frontier occupies the Qal Sisma mountains. They are nomadic, self-sufficient, family-centered, and organized around survival in harsh terrain. They follow prey, raid for supplies when needed, show little expansionist or conquest desire, and defend the community from physical and cultural intrusion.

Their shamanic life centers on frozen ancestors, whispering, elemental affinity, ice and stone shaping, the thaw, ice doubles, the Wide Whisper, the Dragonclaw, the First Father or First Mother, the One Who Whispers Twice, and the Hunt Caller. Surrak Dragonclaw, Nitula, Chianul, Baihir Torn-of-Ear, Kharkhel, Karakyk Valley, Staircase of Bones, the Dragon's Throat, and Qal Sisma are safe Khans-era anchors when cited through TEMUR-SRC-002.

## Fate Reforged And Atarka Boundary

Fate Reforged material establishes ancient Temur values, Yasova Dragonclaw as khan, Qal Sisma clanholds, whisperers, and the pressure of Atarka and her brood. `Khanfall` story context supports Yasova's survival bargain with Atarka. These rows do not license a full Yasova biography or every story beat cited by seed artifacts.

Atarka Clan material is contrast evidence. Under Atarka, the descendants of the former Temur survive by feeding the dragonlord, hidden shamans preserve traditions in secret, and Surrak no longer holds the Dragonclaw title. Atarka Clan is timeline-bound and must not be treated as the same cultural condition as Khans-era Temur Frontier.

## Modern Dragonstorm Floor

The modern Dragonstorm-era Temur are reformed after Atarka's defeat. They are semi-nomadic, northern, isolationist, protective of land and resources, and guided by whisperers. Modern governance pairs the Dragonclaw and Twice Whisperer, with smaller family groups mirroring lay and spiritual leadership.

Modern Temur daily life includes hunting, gathering, herding, careful resource use, scouting, storytelling, wrestling, portable arts, metalwork, and ice or natural sculpture. Their Endless Song connects land, spirits, past, present, future, and possible nows. Whisperers channel spirit and elemental energies through guidance, healing, protection, ceremonies, divination, and defensive magic.

Modern Temur dragonstorm practice includes predicting storms, arriving early when possible, sheltering or vacating threatened regions, and forming strong bonds with clan dragons. Modern locations include Karakyk Glacial Settlement, Summer Landing/Ayagor, Mistrise Village, Eternal Ice, Qadat, the Dragon's Throat, Glintglaze Lake, and Rainveil Forest.
