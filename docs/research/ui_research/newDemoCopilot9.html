Signal Engine / Color Philosophy

The Semiotics of the Color Pie

The five colors of the WUBRG wheel represent incompatible belief systems held in constant tension. They are not cosmetic themes, but an ideological closed-loop: each color enforces its philosophy through distinct mechanics, constraints, and freedoms.

When these forces are condensed face-down, they represent raw potential — a blank slate — before resolving into defined, colored identity.

Selected Ideology: White (W)

White values organization, community, and structure, believing that individual desires must be sacrificed to eradicate suffering. It seeks peace through order, codifying behavior and building systems that minimize harm — even at the cost of personal freedom.
