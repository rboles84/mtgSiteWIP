How to read a force
  • Diamond — where you landed on this force, 0–100. The number on the right.
  • Colored dot — where one of your colors sits on this force alone. Hover a dot for which color.
  • Blend tick — where you'd land if your colors simply averaged.
  • Amber pull — how far the synthesis pushed you past that blend, and toward which color. A long bar means the engine did real work here; no bar means you're just the average.
  • ⚖Load-bearing — the force where your colors disagree most, so it defines your identity more than any other.
