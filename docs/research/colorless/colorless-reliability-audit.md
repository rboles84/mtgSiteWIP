Colorless now has gold-level Layer 2 source authority for its controlled, source-bound claim set. VM-339 captured official/current sources for the nine rows VM-338 marked `external-required`, and VM-340 resolved the canon relocation blocker with a file-by-file map.

## Gold-Safe Floor

- Colorless is not a color, not a mono-color, and not a sixth color.
- Colorless is a mana type, and `{C}` is specifically colorless mana or a colorless-only cost.
- Generic mana costs are not a mana type and are not the same as colorless mana.
- Wastes is a Basic Land and a clean `{C}` mana-base anchor, with no basic land type.
- Devoid makes an object colorless, but Commander color identity can still make a card non-strict-Colorless.
- Ulalek and Eldrazi Incursion are comparator/five-color Eldrazi material, not native strict-Colorless proof.
- Zhulodok, Omarthis, and Eldrazi Unbound are official strict-Colorless support at the product/card-boundary level.
- Artifacts and Colorless are not equivalent; colored artifacts exist and colorless non-artifacts exist.
- Phyrexian mana symbols are colored, so Phyrexia is safe only as a negative false-positive discriminator.
- Ugin, Karn, and the Eldrazi titan prison story are safe as summarized branch anchors only.

## Remaining Reliability Risks

- Treating generic mana, colorless mana, artifacts, and Colorless identity as interchangeable.
- Treating all artifact decks as Colorless identity.
- Treating Eldrazi, Devoid, or five-color Eldrazi as strict-Colorless proof.
- Treating Wastes as a symbol for every Colorless card instead of a specific land/mana branch.
- Treating Phyrexia as Colorless or making positive Phyrexia lore claims from the negative discriminator.
- Treating support-only Commander rows as product, legality, Oracle, or recommendation authority.
- Treating generated artifacts, runtime copy, or `colorless.webp` as source evidence.

## Downstream Readiness

Colorless Layer 2 is gold for controlled source authority. That does not unlock public product expansion. Home preview, public routes, public aliases, directory links, Commander Compass, broad Commander/deck/land-package advice, prices, metagame claims, schema expansion, Supabase manual-context expansion, and image changes still require separate approval.
