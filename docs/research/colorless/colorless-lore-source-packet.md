## Comparator Guardrails

- Five-color means full inclusion; Colorless means non-coloration.
- Generic mana costs are not colorless mana requirements.
- Phyrexia distorts or weaponizes color; Eldrazi stand outside or unmake colored structure.
- Artifact identity can belong to colored worlds and color philosophies; Colorless artifacts are one branch, not the whole identity.
