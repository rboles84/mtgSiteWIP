## Current Safe Floor

- Ink is Vox Mana's reserved RGWU/non-Black public expression and research label, not a settled official MTG faction name.
- `Altruism` is a Commander 2016 theme alias for the same color quadruple and must not be presented as an official exclusive color-combo name.
- Ink is framed by absent Black.
- Ink-Treader Nephilim can be used as an anchor card, not as a faction or institution.
- Kynaios and Tiro and `Stalwart Unity` can support group-benefit, shared-resource, and political Commander texture, not prove Ink lore.
- Detailed worldview language may be written only as support-bound framing or clearly labeled Vox Mana synthesis.

## Reliability Risks

- Overclaiming Ink as an official MTG faction, plane, or institution.
- Overclaiming Ink or Altruism as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for RGWU.
- Treating Ink-Treader Nephilim as the founder of a coherent doctrine or civilization.
- Treating Kynaios and Tiro, `Stalwart Unity`, or Commander/precon support rows as proof of Ink lore or naming authority.
- Treating the Commander JSONL row or repo-local precon data as lore, legality, or raw-placement proof.
- Treating the discovery drafts as approved evidence because they already contain polished prose, rankings, and external citations.
- Adding runtime aliases from RGWU, WURG, or other permutations during source work.
