### Terminology (bind as definitions)
- `MECH-DEF-primary` **Primary** — the color(s) an ability appears in most: highest volume, lowest rarity it's used at; almost always gets the effect each set if it's an every-set ability; usually the color that pushes power level. "Primary" is relative to how often an effect type is used at all.
- `MECH-DEF-secondary` **Secondary** — appears regularly but less often / not always at as low a rarity than primary; may carry restrictions (e.g., red is secondary in flying, but only on Dragons/Phoenixes).
- `MECH-DEF-tertiary` **Tertiary** — occasional, not every set; can go years between appearances; often narrow/rules-bound (e.g., black is tertiary in first strike, mostly on Knights).
- `MECH-DEF-notes` There is always a primary, but not always a secondary/tertiary; more than one color can share a level. Multicolor cards capturing a color's feel use primary/secondary, almost never tertiary. "N" = a natural number ≥ 1. "Spells" here = instants and sorceries.

### Key cross-color rationale (bind as mechanical-flavor claims)
- `MECH-removal` Black = king of single-target creature destruction, kills regardless of circumstance; White = clear #2 but usually higher cost / combat-bound / exile / conditional (big creatures, attackers/blockers, tapped). Green's only creature-destruction targets are fliers and artifact creatures, primarily via Fight. Red kills via damage (anything with low enough toughness). Blue does NOT destroy creatures (changes stats temporarily or via removable Aura instead).
- `MECH-carddraw` Blue best, unrestricted. Black draws by paying a cost (usually life/sacrifice). Green's draw is creature-tied (no longer land-tied). White is tertiary, drawing over time with a once-per-turn limit. Red, beyond cantrips, draws only via impulsive draw, punisher effects, and wheeling.
- `MECH-counters` Counterspells are almost mono-Blue; White dips in via taxing/delay counters.
- `MECH-flying` White + Blue primary (Blue most fliers, White most efficient small fliers); Black + Red secondary (Red restricted to Dragons/Phoenixes); Green tertiary/near-quaternary.
- `MECH-antiflying` Red and Green are the two anti-flying colors (deal damage to fliers); Green also can destroy fliers outright.
- `MECH-artifact-friendly` White + Blue (colors of civilization) are friendly to artifacts; Red associates with Equipment/Vehicles and using artifacts (Treasure) as a resource.
- `MECH-discard-effect` Discard-as-effect is essentially black-only.
