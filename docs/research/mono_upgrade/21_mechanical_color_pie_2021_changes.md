# MECHANICAL COLOR PIE 2021 — CHANGES — Source Digest

> Paraphrased change-log. Not verbatim. Pull quotes from the source URL under fair use.

## `MECH-CP-2021-CHG`
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (with the Council of Colors) · **Pub:** 2021-10-18 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/mechanical-color-pie-2021-changes
**Tier:** Gold (mechanical change-log). Companion to `MECH-CP-2021`; documents every delta from the 2017 version.

### Change types (definitions)
- `CHG-DEF` Five change types used throughout: **change text**, **change label** (rename, may reorder alphabetically), **change status** (which colors are primary/secondary/tertiary), **remove a section**, **add a section**. Every change was reviewed by the Council of Colors.

### Notable bindable shifts (2017 → 2021)
- `CHG-deslang` De-slanging pass: replaced slang labels that referenced long-unprinted cards (e.g., "The Abyss" → "Creature sacrifice, forced, repeatable"; "Bounce" → "Return to hand"; "Creatureball" → "Variable creature").
- `CHG-animate` Added "animating artifacts" (Blue primary / Green secondary) and "animating enchantments" (White primary / Blue secondary) — present since Alpha but previously omitted.
- `CHG-banisher` Banisher Priest exile effect consolidated to **White only** (removed proposed Green access and Blue's Colossal Whale style).
- `CHG-cantblock` "Can't block" moved to **primary in both Black and Red** (red's old "must attack" drawback plays worse; "can't block" is better gameplay).
- `CHG-carddraw` Commander's rise prompted rethinking card draw: Green draw pulled back to creature-tied only (no longer land-tied); Red upped slightly (impulsive draw higher as-fan, plus punisher + wheeling); White's draw defined as once-per-turn, long-game style.
- `CHG-cardfilter` Card filtering moved Green to **primary** alongside Blue (less tutoring, more top-of-library searching for lands/creatures).
- `CHG-removal-white` Single-target creature destruction: **White added as secondary** (clear #2 behind Black). "Destroy power-4+" floor lowered to **power 3+** (single and mass). "Destroy creature, controller compensated" consolidated to **White only** (removed from Blue — Blue isn't supposed to destroy creatures).
- `CHG-damage-pw` Direct damage updated to include **planeswalker** as a target; **White added as secondary** direct damage (damage = number of creatures you control) to support its small-army strength.
- `CHG-defender-blue` Defender: **Blue added as primary** alongside White (both most-defensive colors).
- `CHG-vigilance-blue` (per governance source) Blue made secondary in **vigilance** to give blue creatures non-evasion combat options.
- `CHG-enchant-removal-black` Enchantment destruction: **Black added as secondary/tertiary** (lower power level than White/Green, often forced-sac or extra cost, often opponent-only) — one of the bigger 2017→2021 shifts.
- `CHG-pump-red` +N/+N on spells: **Red added as secondary** (power > toughness). White allowed larger pumps under conditions (count creatures/Plains, or blockers-only).
- `CHG-variable-creature` "Variable creature" (X +1/+1 counters): **Black and Red added as tertiary** alongside Green primary.
- `CHG-counter-ability` "Counter activated/triggered ability": Green moved from secondary to **tertiary** (mostly vs. artifact activations now); Blue remains primary.
- `CHG-discard-cost` Added "discard as a cost" as its own section (Black primary, Red secondary, others tertiary) and relabeled the old "Discard" to "Discard as effect."
- `CHG-removed` Removed sections no longer used: "Blocking extra creatures," several "+0/+N / -0/-N to your/their team" subsections, "Return to library" in White, "Can't be attacked" secondary in Green, "+N/-N (Auras)" tertiary in Blue.
