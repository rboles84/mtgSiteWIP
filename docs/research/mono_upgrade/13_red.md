## `MONO-R-2015` — Seeing Red Revisited
**Author:** Mark Rosewater · **Pub:** 2015-08-03 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/seeing-red-revisited-2015-08-03
**Structure:** Six-question framework. **Tier:** Gold (philosophy backbone).

### Bindable claims
- `R2015-Q1` **End goal — freedom.** The meaning of life is known: listen to your heart/emotions and act accordingly. Live in the moment, be spontaneous, embrace every adventure, and reach the deathbed with no regrets. Red bonds strongly (passion, loyalty, camaraderie, lust).
- `R2015-Q2` **Means — action.** Constant steps toward what you want; attuned to feelings (direction can change quickly). Tools: destruction, speed/instant action, manipulating emotions, an impish/tricky nature, relentlessness.
- `R2015-Q3` **Cares about:** emotion (all of them, freely expressed), mood swings, impulse, might (brawn over brain), violence, brutality, destruction, "chaos" (red calls it living, not chaos), randomness, spontaneity, humor, mischievousness, fire/earth elements, fighting/brawling, romance, hedonism, barbarism.
- `R2015-Q4` **Despises:** frustration (being told it can't do what it wants) and boredom/inaction. Red would rather fail spectacularly than sit idle.
- `R2015-Q5` **Allies:** Black (internal voice, freedom, destruction; friction: black plans/plots more and is more selfish). Green (embraces its wild/instinctual side, has mana and big creatures, a destructive streak; friction: green is more orderly/serene than red likes).
- `R2015-Q5b` **Enemies:** White (rules/laws complicate everything and can imprison red → loss of freedom, red's worst outcome). Blue (lives to undercut feelings; wants emotionless automatons and to make nothing happen — bores red to death).
- `R2015-Q6` **Strength:** knows what it wants and acts; purpose, clarity, passion, speed that overwhelms. **Weakness:** unwillingness to think long-term; short-sighted; all eggs in one basket; bad at taking advice.

## `MONO-R-2025` — My Words: Red
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (in-character as Red) · **Pub:** 2025-10-20 · **Publisher:** WotC
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/my-words-red
**Lineage:** Article-form *Drive to Work* color voice. **Tier:** Gold (current voice).
**Voice caveat:** First-person as Red; casual register; explicitly addresses the "anger-only" stereotype.

### Bindable claims
- `R2025-a` Purpose of a finite life = live it to the fullest (love, friends, exciting experiences, things people remember).
- `R2025-b` Two-step method: (1) **listen to your body** — it tells you what you need (hunger, thirst, fatigue, and equally loneliness/anger/sadness); (2) **act on the message** — inaction is easy but fruitless (asking-someone-out analogy: either outcome leaves you better off).
- `R2025-c` What separates fulfilled from unfulfilled = willingness to act; ignore the voices telling you not to listen to your own desires.
- `R2025-d` **Enemy White:** makes rules forbidding what you want, labels Red the source of suffering; Red counters that oppressive rules cause suffering and that passion ≠ selfishness — a happy society lets everyone pursue passions. Accepts conflict/violence as people "being people," not anarchy. Desire is core to experience.
- `R2025-e` **Enemy Blue:** the master of *not acting*; over-thinks everything. Red defends mistakes as the best teacher and the iterative do→feedback→change loop; argues lived experience/emotion outranks book knowledge; Blue fears emotions.
- `R2025-f` **Ally Black:** agrees on prioritizing your needs, tapping the destructive side, taking risks, getting your hands dirty; Black is fun. Friction: Black's selfishness is extreme and untrustworthy; Red, by contrast, prizes **loyalty** to those who matter.
- `R2025-g` **Stereotype correction:** anger is just one facet; Red embraces *all* emotional impulses (love, happiness, sadness, fear) — they appear on fewer cards only because the game is combat-oriented.
- `R2025-h` **Ally Green:** also action- over thinking-driven (instinct vs. impulse), shares a destructive streak and a love of breaking things; shared dislikes (artifacts, fliers). Friction: Green's spiritual/"web of life" community side that Red doesn't get.
- `R2025-i` **Strength/weakness (self-described):** strength = speed/aggression + destruction (attacking before opponents are ready). Weakness = short-sightedness; acts first, deals with consequences later; "my own worst enemy."
