## `MONO-U-2015` — True Blue Revisited
**Author:** Mark Rosewater · **Pub:** 2015-07-20 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/true-blue-revisited-2015-07-20
**Structure:** Six-question framework. **Tier:** Gold (philosophy backbone).

### Bindable claims
- `U2015-Q1` **End goal — perfection.** Blue holds each person is born a blank slate with the potential to become anything; life is the ongoing, never-finished pursuit of self-improvement via education, experience, and tools.
- `U2015-Q1b` Requires a society that enables it (education, trial-and-error spaces, access to top tools) and the right attitude: deliberate, unhurried, careful decision-making. Blue also seeks to perfect the world it lives in; it is the color most interested in technology.
- `U2015-Q2` **Means — information/knowledge.** Knowledge is the ultimate tool; blue collects all of it. Information serves as improvement, control, key/access, protection, and weapon. Blue favors universities, libraries, laboratories, and structured systems it can master.
- `U2015-Q3` **Cares about:** academics, books/repositories, mind/thought, memory, telepathy, telekinesis, time manipulation, control, manipulation, trickery, subtlety, complexity, artifice, illusion, construction/technology, artifacts, man-made items, passivity, cold, water/air elements.
- `U2015-Q4` **Despises:** impulse and instinct. Impulse = emotion-driven short-sightedness; instinct = the shortcomings of (slow) biology. Also dislikes tradition and ceremony as impediments to progress/change.
- `U2015-Q5` **Allies:** White (shares planning/foresight/rules; friction: white's inflexibility and disregard for the individual, plus white's traditions/ceremony). Black (shares pragmatism, willingness to change, value of education/tools; friction: black's emotion in decisions and disregard for the group).
- `U2015-Q5b` **Enemies:** Red (gave in to emotion; reckless, unreliable, destructive, impulsive). Green (nature over nurture; denies self-shaped futures via destiny/predetermination — opposes blue's core).
- `U2015-Q6` **Strength:** knowledge as a tool — examines the big picture, foresees consequences, never careless; the master of control; uniquely counters spells. **Weakness:** passivity/reactivity — slow, can be overwhelmed by aggression or by indirect/non-proactive opponents.

## `MONO-U-2025` — My Words: Blue
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (in-character as Blue) · **Pub:** 2025-06-23 · **Publisher:** WotC
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/my-words-blue
**Lineage:** Article-form *Drive to Work* color voice. **Tier:** Gold (current voice).
**Voice caveat:** First-person as Blue.

### Bindable claims
- `U2025-a` Core belief: every person is a **blank slate** (cites Aristotle's *tabula rasa*); who you become is shaped by people, knowledge, experiences, tools — but above all by your own internal drive (who you *want* to be).
- `U2025-b` Knowledge is primary; education is key ("knowledge is power"). Knowledge takes many forms (formal, reading/listening/watching, other people) and is a lifelong, never-complete task.
- `U2025-c` Decisions should not be rushed; take time to weigh ramifications for the best long-term outcome (vacation-planning analogy: research/rank/select vs. drive-at-random).
- `U2025-d` Experience matters alongside knowledge; tools/technology extend capability (glasses analogy). Society must build tools to collect and share knowledge, and revere teachers as the central pillar.
- `U2025-e` Optimization scales beyond the individual to family/community/society; the desired mindset values efficiency over expediency and intellect over emotion.
- `U2025-f` **Ally White:** shares optimization (esp. societal), long-term thinking, willingness to do the work; divergence: White favors group over individual and one singular path; Blue wants each person's unique path. Frame: Blue wants individuals to **shine** even at unequal resource cost.
- `U2025-g` **Ally Black:** agrees on personal choice and rejecting destiny; both wield knowledge as a weapon (subterfuge/misdirection). Blue focuses on **identity (who you are)**, Black on **role (what you can do)**. Friction: Black's recklessness/risk-taking.
- `U2025-h` **Enemy Red:** emotion, snap decisions, short-term thinking; "embodiment of carelessness." Concedes Red's creativity helps surface non-obvious solutions.
- `U2025-i` **Enemy Green:** the nature-vs-nurture conflict; Green denies the blank slate and the concept of choice; disdains intellect/artifice; narrow view of education. Concedes evolution is a positive force worth studying — the one point of overlap.
- `U2025-j` **Strength/weakness (self-described):** strength = knowledge → sees systems, counters spells, builds resources to answer any threat given time; "master of control." Weakness = needs time to process; vulnerable to aggression and to indirect attacks; reactive.
