## `MONO-W-2015` — The Great White Way Revisited
**Author:** Mark Rosewater · **Pub:** 2015-07-13 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/great-white-way-revisited-2015-07-13
**Structure:** Six-question framework. **Tier:** Gold (philosophy backbone).

### Bindable claims
- `W2015-Q1` **End goal — peace.** White wants a world with no unnecessary suffering, where every individual's needs are met. It holds that resources already suffice and that suffering stems from individuals not prioritizing the good of the group.
- `W2015-Q1b` Success requires unified collective action; when individuals prioritize personal desire over the group, the plan fails. White accepts that some individuals must be *led* to the goal rather than choosing it themselves.
- `W2015-Q2` **Means — rigid structure.** White uses laws to bind individual choice toward the group, split into **moral law** (defining right/wrong, conscience, guilt, honor, chivalry) and **civil law** (rules with enforced consequences, usually loss of resources or freedom).
- `W2015-Q2b` Moral law governs how people think; civil law governs how they act. Enforcement runs through government, police, and military.
- `W2015-Q3` **Cares about:** religion, military, law, courts, politics, government, community, honor, chivalry, defense, self-sacrifice, cooperation, light (anti-secrecy), purity, charity, strategy, organization.
- `W2015-Q3b` White frames conflict as good vs. evil; notes black rejects the very concept of morality. White "sees in black and white" (low tolerance for gray areas).
- `W2015-Q4` **Despises:** selfishness (undermines moral law → conflict with black) and recklessness (undermines civil law → conflict with red). Can over-fixate on minor rule infractions; takes individual losses hard.
- `W2015-Q5` **Allies:** Blue (shares planning, detail-orientation, value of rules; both oppose red's impulsivity; friction: blue over-values the individual). Green (shares community/creature focus; both oppose black; friction: green's wild, uncontrolled side).
- `W2015-Q5b` **Enemies:** Black (group-vs-individual axis; white sees black as selfish/immoral/cruel). Red (order-vs-chaos axis; white fears red's carelessness/anarchy). White proactively restrains both to prevent harm.
- `W2015-Q6` **Strength:** organization — detail-oriented, deep infrastructure, distributed power (no single point of failure), making small pieces sum to more than their parts. **Weakness:** rigidity/inflexibility — slow to adapt to the unanticipated; treats everything as equally important. Summary: strength = effective use of the group; weakness = disregard for the individual.

## `MONO-W-2025` — My Words: White
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (written in-character as White) · **Pub:** 2025-05-19 · **Publisher:** WotC
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/my-words-white
**Lineage:** Article-form version of the *Drive to Work* color-voice series. **Tier:** Gold (current voice).
**Voice caveat:** First-person as White; rhetorical framing is White's, not neutral designer statement.

### Bindable claims
- `W2025-a` Restates end goal: a utopia free of hunger/crime/suffering is achievable; most suffering is a *choice* not to stop it.
- `W2025-b` The single required change: people must ask "what does the group need?" instead of "what do I want?"
- `W2025-c` People default to selfishness as the path of least resistance, but possess a learnable/nurturable natural goodness.
- `W2025-d` Method = **structure + incentives** (positive: belonging/reward; negative: loss of resources, freedom, or community standing). Peer pressure and belonging are explicit tools.
- `W2025-e` Two chief threats remain **selfishness** (tool to counter: morality, objective right/wrong, often via religion) and **recklessness** (tool to counter: law, public and clear, plus a judicial system for circumstance).
- `W2025-f` **Ally Blue:** shares love of civilization/systems; but Blue optimizes the individual and is willing to let people "fall through the cracks." Local frame: **White raises the floor; Blue raises the ceiling.**
- `W2025-g` **Ally Green:** shares community/"web of life"; Green leans spiritual over religious, acts on instinct rather than taught morality; divergence = restraint vs. Green's wild side.
- `W2025-h` **Enemy Red:** chaos/recklessness as a celebrated virtue; dangerous. Admits Red's capacity for self-sacrifice for loved ones.
- `W2025-i` **Enemy Black:** champion of selfishness; treats immorality as a trophy; aims at a dystopia where many suffer so a few thrive. Notes Black has co-opted White's systems (law, religion).
- `W2025-j` **Strength/weakness (self-described):** strength = order (many tailored answers, can solve most problems given time); weakness = prescriptive answers, poor at adapting to the unanticipated. Adds a moral strength: refuses to kill unless necessary (prefers imprisonment), accepting that this lets some threats escape.
