## `FMT-CMDR` - MTG Commander Format (official)

Publisher: Wizards of the Coast

Captured: 2026-06-13. Re-verify before high-stakes binding.

### Bindable overview claims

- `CMDR-deck` Deck overview: Commander uses one commander plus a 99-card singleton deck, with basic lands exempt from the singleton limit and legality still subject to Commander restrictions and banned-list handling.
- `CMDR-format-overview` The public Commander page is useful for explaining the format to players: commander-centric deckbuilding, multiplayer assumptions, command-zone play, commander tax, commander damage, life total, and optional bracket framing.
- `CMDR-command-zone` The commander starts in the command zone and can be cast from there with an added generic cost for each previous command-zone cast that game.
- `CMDR-cmd-damage` Commander damage is tracked as a separate loss condition when one commander deals enough combat damage to a player over the game.
- `CMDR-multiplayer` Commander is framed as a multiplayer format with a higher starting life total and social/matchmaking expectations.
- `CMDR-brackets` Commander Brackets and Game Changers are living, matchmaking-oriented overview concepts; re-check current official wording before using exact bracket claims.
- `CMDR-usage-note` Use this page as a user-facing format overview only. For exact commander eligibility and color-identity boundary claims, prefer Comprehensive Rules 903.3 and 903.4.

## `RULES-CR` - Comprehensive Rules (Commander eligibility and color identity)

Publisher: Wizards of the Coast
Landing URL: https://magic.wizards.com/en/rules
Type: Living rules document; landing page links to the downloadable Comprehensive Rules PDF
Tier: Gold official rules

### Bindable rules claims

- `CR-903.3` Commander eligibility boundary: in the Commander variant, a deck designates a legendary creature card as its commander unless another rule or card permission allows a different commander. Use the current Comprehensive Rules text for exact eligibility language.
- `CR-903.4` Color identity boundary: a Commander's deck construction is constrained by color identity, which is established before the game and includes the card's colors plus colors of mana symbols in its mana cost and rules text, with rule-defined exceptions handled by the Comprehensive Rules.
- `CR-usage-note` Use CR 903.3 and CR 903.4 only as Commander legality and identity-boundary sources. Do not use the rules document for color philosophy, mechanical color pie claims, profile-axis scoring, or candidate commander promotion.
