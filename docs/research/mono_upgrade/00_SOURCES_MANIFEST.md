## Purpose

This folder is the primary mono-color source-intake bundle for moving W/U/B/R/G from the VM-335 transitional Layer 1 exception toward gold-standard Layer 1 and Layer 2 readiness.

The markdown files are paraphrased claim digests and source-intake aids. They are not raw packets, exact-quote stores, runtime copy, or generated artifacts.

The PDF files are local official capture evidence for the corresponding canonical Wizards of the Coast pages. Exact quotes, if any, must be pulled directly from a canonical URL or the local PDF capture under fair use. The digests may supply paraphrased claims and stable anchor IDs.

Official color philosophy and mechanical sources can support philosophy and mechanical claims. They do not directly define Vox Mana profile-axis scores. Axis values remain Vox Mana interpretation until claim-backed raw packets are reviewed.

Drive to Work remains deferred unless clean episode metadata and transcript provenance are found.

## Source Roles

- Philosophy backbone: 2015 Revisited articles, used for each color's goal, means, cares, dislikes, allies/enemies, strengths, and weaknesses.
- Current voice/update: 2025 My Words articles, used for current first-person color framing and modern emphasis.
- Mechanical authority: Mechanical Color Pie 2021 and its 2021 changes article, used for color-pie mechanics and change history.
- Governance/process authority: The Council of Colors, Revisited, used for process, review, and color-pie evolution claims.
- Rules authority: Comprehensive Rules 903.3 and 903.4, used for exact Commander eligibility and color-identity boundary claims.
- Format overview: public Commander format page, used only as user-facing overview. Do not prefer it over CR 903.3/903.4 for exact boundary claims.

## Authority Layering For Claim Ledgers

1. Use `MECH-CP-2021` and `MECH-CP-2021-CHG` as the highest authority for mechanical claims, with date-stamped caution because the mechanical pie evolves.
2. Use `GOV-COC-2024` for how the color pie is maintained, reviewed, and evolved, including Commander-driven shifts.
3. Use `MONO-*-2015` as the neutral philosophy backbone for the six-question color framework.
4. Use `MONO-*-2025` as the current first-person voice layer. Treat its rhetorical framing as color-voice, not designer-neutral wording.
5. Use `RULES-CR` for exact Commander eligibility and color-identity boundaries, especially CR 903.3 and 903.4.
6. Use `FMT-CMDR` only for high-level user-facing format overview. Do not use it for exact rules claims where CR 903.3/903.4 is available.
