## `MONO-G-2015` — It's Not Easy Being Green Revisited
**Author:** Mark Rosewater · **Pub:** 2015-08-10 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/its-not-easy-being-green-revisited-2015-08-10
**Structure:** Six-question framework. **Tier:** Gold (philosophy backbone). Note: MaRo flags this as the biggest change from the 2002 original.

### Bindable claims
- `G2015-Q1` **End goal — acceptance.** Green alone doesn't want to change the world; the natural order is already perfect and holds the answers. Each individual is born with the potential they need; the path is to recognize the role you were born into and embrace it (destiny/predetermination).
- `G2015-Q1b` You must learn how you fit the larger picture — nature is an interconnected web of interdependencies; you are never alone.
- `G2015-Q2` **Means — nature itself.** If people won't come to nature, green brings nature to them. Tools: bond with wild creatures (the most creature-centric color, with larger/fiercer creatures), close tie to land (faster mana, larger/faster spells, color access), connection to life/rejuvenation/growth to overwhelm.
- `G2015-Q3` **Cares about:** life/birth, growth, nature/natural things (vs. artificial = abomination), reality/truth (vs. illusion), community, interdependence, spiritualism, the past, wisdom (vs. blue's future-knowledge), ancestry, tradition, instinct, animals, plants.
- `G2015-Q4` **Despises:** *unnatural* change — (1) creating things that were never meant to exist (hence hatred of artifacts, lesser of enchantments), and (2) removing things from nature inorganically (ecosystem disruption). Strong attachment to the status quo.
- `G2015-Q5` **Allies:** White (shares community; both creature-reliant; white's peaceful side; friction: white doesn't get green's wild side). Red (embraces its wild side; instinct vs. impulse; both have mana + big creatures + a destructive streak; friction: red ignores consequences to others).
- `G2015-Q5b` **Enemies:** Blue (nurture vs. green's nature; blue's artificiality and future-obsession; both educate but green looks to the past's wisdom). Black (power-hungry, no conscience, wrecks ecosystems; misuses death which green sees as part of the circle of life; black asserts free will vs. green's predetermination).
- `G2015-Q6` **Strength:** unwavering belief in the natural system; primal power; access to giant creatures. **Weakness:** over-reliance on that system (esp. creatures) — too many eggs in one basket; struggles when creatures aren't the answer.

## `MONO-G-2025` — My Words: Green
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (in-character as Green) · **Pub:** 2025-12-01 · **Publisher:** WotC
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/my-words-green
**Lineage:** Article-form *Drive to Work* color voice; Green goes last. **Tier:** Gold (current voice).
**Voice caveat:** First-person as Green; contemplative register.

### Bindable claims
- `G2025-a` Core difference: Green is the one color **not advocating change** — the world is perfect as is; the goal is to accept what *is* rather than focus on what *could be*. Nature is a perfect, elegant system.
- `G2025-b` Each of us has a role in the **web of life**; we're born to play a part, and the task is to understand and fulfill it.
- `G2025-c` Method (observe nature): four lessons from sitting in nature — (1) a sense of peace/belonging; (2) every object has a function; (3) everything is an interconnected system (creature ↔ environment); (4) everything happens for a reason — Green is *not* anti-change but holds that change is slow, purposeful evolution measured in lifetimes.
- `G2025-d` Four-step path to satisfaction: acknowledge nature's force → discover your innate skills → apply them (trial and error) to find your role → fulfill it and help others find theirs.
- `G2025-e` **Enemy Blue:** only accepts the provable; rejects gut/feeling/faith; "blank slate" denies that who you are comes from within/your origins. Green claims Blue lies to itself (the "color of deception" framing). Overlap: both enjoy exploration/categorizing; Green looks to the past, Blue the future.
- `G2025-f` **Enemy Black:** rejects **responsibility**; advocates selfishness dressed as ambition; misuses death as a tool. Green: if every part of the web acted only in self-interest, the ecosystem collapses. Frame: **Blue rejects identity; Black rejects purpose.** Concedes Black understands the life cycle, appreciates nature's dark side, and never gives up.
- `G2025-g` **Ally Red:** looks internally for motivation (impulse vs. Green's instinct), lives in the moment, carefree, taps the wild side. Friction: Red doesn't get Green's spiritual/introspective side and has limited community sense.
- `G2025-h` **Ally White:** understands community and the value of the group; both value faith/the nontangible (White → religion, Green → spirituality). Divergence: White treats all members the same and relies on established rules/morality; Green treats each individual uniquely and relies on natural systems/instinct.
- `G2025-i` **Strength/weakness (self-described):** strength = unwavering belief/certainty (already found its answers) → focus, access to more colors/mana/efficient creatures. Weakness = over-reliance on what it cares about (esp. its own creatures) — without them, often unable to answer threats.
