## `MONO-B-2015` — In the Black Revisited
**Author:** Mark Rosewater · **Pub:** 2015-07-27 · **Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/black-revisited-2015-07-27
**Structure:** Six-question framework. **Tier:** Gold (philosophy backbone).

### Bindable claims
- `B2015-Q1` **End goal — power.** Black is the realist that sees the world as it is; power is the resource that guarantees control over one's life and happiness. Black didn't make the world greedy — it thrives within it by accepting the system and self-imposing no restrictions.
- `B2015-Q1b` Philosophy: no one is better suited to look after your interests than you; if everyone does so, everyone has an advocate. The weak fail — that is what makes them weak; helping them is not black's concern.
- `B2015-Q2` **Means — seize every opportunity.** No taboos, restrictions, rules, morals, sympathy, hesitation, or regret. Costs are accepted as the price of success; death is a prime tool others wrongly avoid. Black puts itself first and will trade the sacrifice of others.
- `B2015-Q3` **Cares about:** death, undead, amorality (rejects morality as a human construct), fear, sadness/depression, pain, torture, darkness, disease, decay/entropy, parasitism, insects/spiders, vermin, corruption, impurity/contamination, reduction, deceit, manipulation, Machiavellian "ends justify means," individualism, calculated destruction, sacrifice of others, sacrifice of self (when efficient), execution, self-absorption.
- `B2015-Q4` **Despises:** those who act against their own self-interest for others' sake (black finds them baffling and dangerous — they can't be motivated by fear/pain/death). These self-made forces both intrigue and unsettle black.
- `B2015-Q5` **Allies:** Blue (manipulation, trickery, information-as-tool, emotional detachment, tech; friction: blue serves the group and learning over self). Red (embraces destruction, self-interest, ignores others' opinions; friction: red is short-sighted and will self-sacrifice for loved ones).
- `B2015-Q5b` **Enemies:** White (coddles the weak, invented morality to make people act against self-interest, runs propaganda casting black as "evil"). Green (deploys instinct-driven resources black can't control; opposes black with life/growth vs. death/decay; believes in destiny vs. black's free will).
- `B2015-Q6` **Strength:** willingness to do whatever is needed; nothing is off-limits. **Weakness:** that same willingness — calculated risks that can backfire; black's greatest enemy is often itself.

## `MONO-B-2025` — My Words: Black
**Author:** Mark Rosewater (in-character as Black) · **Pub:** 2025-09-15 · **Publisher:** WotC
**URL:** https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/my-words-black
**Lineage:** Article-form *Drive to Work* color voice. **Tier:** Gold (current voice).
**Voice caveat:** First-person as Black; explicitly argues Black is "the most misunderstood" color and reframes selfishness positively.

### Bindable claims
- `B2025-a` Reframed goal: Black wants every person to have the *opportunity* to thrive; it expects most won't, and that's acceptable. Most pragmatic color — sees the world as a harsh place.
- `B2025-b` Selfishness defended: only you can truly look out for you; the word's negative connotation is (per Black) White's doing. Endorses a merit-based, zero-sum framing — gain implies someone else's loss, and that's earned/okay.
- `B2025-c` Helping loved ones is allowed *only* when it doesn't conflict with your own needs (life-vest-first analogy).
- `B2025-d` Most people are governed by **fear** and decline opportunities; Black distinguishes calculated ruthlessness from recklessness — "careful and methodical," not reckless.
- `B2025-e` Goal stated as **power** ("be your own champion"); offers a 5-step method: (1) decide what you want, (2) list every step to get it, (3) identify obstacles, (4) inventory all resources, (5) use resources to clear obstacles — repeat.
- `B2025-f` **Enemy White:** master of convincing people to act against self-interest via the "good of the group" and morality (which Black calls a made-up story). Respects White's organizing ability; has co-opted law and religion.
- `B2025-g` **Enemy Green:** opposes via the concept of **destiny** ("not meant for you") and a self-serving reading of survival-of-the-fittest. Concedes Green truly appreciates death and has admirable ferocity, but acts on instinct (hard to motivate).
- `B2025-h` **Ally Red:** the color of **action** — does something rather than complaining; uses destruction; enjoys life. Friction: Red is reckless even for Black's taste and short-sighted.
- `B2025-i` **Ally Blue:** thinks everything through, plans, wields knowledge; doesn't fall for White's "morality." Friction: Blue chases the ideal/optimal over "good enough" and avoids necessary risk.
- `B2025-j` **Strength/weakness (self-described):** strength = sees opportunities; no taboos/qualms/hesitation/mercy; unattached to anything (will sacrifice) and willing to take calculated risks. Weakness = risks won't always pay off; meddling with forces others avoid can backfire.
