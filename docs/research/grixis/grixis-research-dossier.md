## Identity Floor

Grixis is the UBR shard of Alara in the local official Rosewater three-color article. Its design center is Black. That means VM-164 can safely treat Grixis through Black's survival, agency, self-interest, and "adapt to reality" lens, with Blue and Red as allied supports. Evidence: GRIXIS-001 through GRIXIS-003.

Blue contributes calculation, subtlety, problem-solving, and exploitation of weakness inside the Black-centered frame. Red contributes zeal, action, individual immediacy, and willingness to act, while retaining a volatility that Blue does not naturally share. Evidence: GRIXIS-004, GRIXIS-005.

The supported UBR synthesis is narrow: Black combines Blue's manipulation and Red's zeal into a more actively conniving survival world. Evidence: GRIXIS-006.

## What White And Green Absence Can Support

The official design evidence supports White and Green as Grixis's missing enemy-color pressures. VM-164 may say that this removes the design pressures associated with moral/order constraint and life/renewal pressure from the shard frame. Evidence: GRIXIS-007.

VM-164 may not expand that into unsupported claims that Grixis has no intelligence, no structure, no social logic, or no systems. Blue is part of Grixis, and the drafts themselves repeatedly reach for organized necromancy, liches, and power nodes. Those richer structures require source-bound local evidence before promotion.

---

## Not Simply Evil

The strongest safe boundary is that Grixis should not be reduced to "evil UBR." Rosewater's article is framed as Black explaining itself against that flattening. VM-164 can say the design frame is survival, self-advocacy, agency, exploitation, calculation, and force. Evidence: GRIXIS-003 through GRIXIS-008.

`Vox Mana synthesis`: Grixis can be product-described as "survival through exploitation, calculation, and volatility" only when the phrase is labeled as Vox Mana synthesis and supported by the evidence ledger. Evidence: GRIXIS-022.

## Mechanics And Card-Data Support

Support-only: Local Scryfall data supports unearth as a recurring card mechanic connected to Grixis-associated cards such as Sedris, Sedraxis Specter, Vithian Stinger, Kederekt Leviathan, and Dregscape Zombie. Evidence: GRIXIS-011.

Support-only: Sedris is locally supported as a UBR legendary Zombie Warrior card with unearth-granting rules text. This does not promote any claim that Sedris rules all of Grixis. Evidence: GRIXIS-012.

Support-only: Thraximundar is locally supported as a UBR legendary Zombie Assassin card with haste and sacrifice-pressure text. This does not promote a biography, mount, height, or political office. Evidence: GRIXIS-013.

Support-only: Prince of Thralls, Malfegor, and Cruel Ultimatum support UBR/Grixis-colored attrition and resource-loss patterns at the card-data level. They do not prove detailed story roles. Evidence: GRIXIS-014.

Boundary: `Absorb Vis` supports `vis` as a local card-data term, but the full vis economy remains `Manual fill required`. Evidence: GRIXIS-015, GRIXIS-MF-002, GRIXIS-MF-003.

## Safe Short Definition

Promoted/support-bound version:

Grixis is the UBR Alara shard with Black as its design center. The local official design source frames it through survival, self-advocacy, Blue calculation, Red zeal, and the absence of White/Green pressures. Local card data supports unearth, sacrifice, attrition, and resource-loss patterns as important support signals.

Vox Mana synthesis version:

`Vox Mana synthesis`: Grixis is survival through exploitation, calculation, and volatility: Black decides that survival comes first, Blue finds the weakness, and Red acts before the opening closes.
