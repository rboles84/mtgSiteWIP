## Cross-pair normalization

Each signal is classified `shared`, `mechanical_candidate`, `worldview_explanation`, `native_affiliation`, `flavor_only`, or `anti_signal`; only exact mechanical candidates may reorder, only inside a same-function tier. Native affiliation is disclosed but cannot pass a function gate. Worldview and flavor may explain, never lift. The corpus records counterexamples and “identity should not matter” controls for every pair.
