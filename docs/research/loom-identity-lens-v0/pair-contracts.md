# Five pair contracts — Loom Identity Lens v0

All contracts prohibit cross-tier promotion, query mutation, affiliation-only lift, and identity inference. They are design hypotheses, not calibrated models.

| Pair | Guild mechanical candidate / worldview | College mechanical candidate / worldview | Explanation-only / anti-signals / gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boros / Lorehold | multiple-attack/team/protection patterns; civic present-action | recovery/replay, cards leaving graveyard, history/Spirit/relic interaction; evidence from the past | Flavor and names only texture. Generic combat, artifacts, Spirits, or graveyards do not decide. Gap: no empirical pair distinction. |
| Izzet / Prismari | technical experimentation, prototype/iteration, volatile discovery | elemental performance, expression mediated by study and technique | “Instant/sorcery” and elemental alone are shared. Gap: native cards overrepresent their set language. |
| Golgari / Witherbloom | reclamation, discarded value, decay-to-renewal, undercity survival | essence exchange, remedies/toxins, ecology and embodied practice | Death/reanimation and pests alone are shared. Gap: card text rarely expresses care-versus-extraction intent. |
| Simic / Quandrix | biological adaptation, clades, public-health/living-system improvement | fractal/pattern/model/proof interactions | Counters, ramp, and growth alone are shared. Gap: formal-math expression is sparse off Strixhaven. |
| Orzhov / Silverquill | debts, bargains, institutional leverage, obligation | spoken/written influence, reputation, praise/critique, leadership | Lifegain, drain, sacrifice and removal alone are shared. Gap: rhetoric is often flavor rather than Oracle function. |

For each pair, the corpus uses: a shared-function witness, a guild candidate, a college candidate, overlap, a false friend, flavor/affiliation mismatch, and at least one control where neither identity should matter. Placement is a consumer cross-check only. Commander guidance can frame a deck-facing explanation but cannot create a signal.

## Corpus evaluation result

| Pair | Final recommendation use | Downgrade / no-effect rule |
|---|---|---|
| Boros / Lorehold | Constrained same-tier ranking is supportable for coordinated protection and recovery/history respectively. | Generic combat, artifacts, Spirits, and bare graveyard references tie or receive no lift. |
| Izzet / Prismari | Both remain explanation-first; native affiliation is disclosed separately. | Generic spells, copying, damage, and elementals do not distinguish technical iteration from artistic expression. |
| Golgari / Witherbloom | Golgari reclamation is constrained ranking-capable; Witherbloom remains unresolved. | Generic death, graveyard, lifegain, or pests do not prove embodied essence/ecology. |
| Simic / Quandrix | Simic biological adaptation is constrained ranking-capable; Quandrix is native-affiliation/explanation-only. | Counters, ramp, tokens, and large creatures receive no mathematical lift. |
| Orzhov / Silverquill | Narrow debt/obligation evidence is ranking-capable for Orzhov; Silverquill is explanation-only. | Lifegain, drain, sacrifice, removal, and ink/rhetoric flavor alone receive no lift. |

The 120-card / 60-assertion corpus froze its qualitative rules before holdout assessment; no holdout-triggered rule change was made. These are governed-evidence conclusions, not behavioral validation.
