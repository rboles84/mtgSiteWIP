# Owner Experience Checklist

Review the question route and result walkthroughs as an owner experience, not as proof of scoring or identity accuracy.

## Questions

- [ ] Does every prompt describe a decision or state recognizable in Commander?
- [ ] Can a newer player answer without already knowing archetype or color-philosophy terminology?
- [ ] Does an experienced player encounter a real tradeoff rather than a shallow preference label?
- [ ] Are answer titles distinct after the explanatory sentences are hidden, and still distinct when the sentences are read?
- [ ] Is any answer visibly coded to a named identity or faction?
- [ ] Is any option framed as more admirable, skilled, social, or correct?
- [ ] Does every conditional answer represent a genuinely deck-dependent distinction?
- [ ] Does each Crucible item stay within one approved construct?
- [ ] Do the three proposed prompt tunes improve clarity without changing answer meaning?
- [ ] Does the six-to-eight-question ceiling feel shorter and clearer than the EDHMatch benchmark?

## Results

- [ ] Does the result begin with what the answers showed?
- [ ] Does it explain why the current fit appeared without claiming calibrated confidence?
- [ ] When a nearby alternative appears, does it have direct support and a named separating observation?
- [ ] Are ties, close results, mixed readings, and insufficient evidence visibly different?
- [ ] Does every limitation state what the result does not claim?
- [ ] Does every named result provide a Commander pattern worth exploring?
- [ ] Does the next action point to a specific dossier, Matrix comparison, Maze path, or recommendation direction?
- [ ] Are `PARTIAL` and `GAP` identities visibly less complete than `READY` identities?
- [ ] Is every profile lens optional enrichment rather than placement rationale?
- [ ] Does the experience feel like Vox Mana—Commander-first, atmospheric but plain—rather than a generic personality quiz?
- [ ] Is every added sentence earning its place?
