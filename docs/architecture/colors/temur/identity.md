# Temur Frontier - Survival Through Attunement, Savagery, And Elemental Memory

> Source note: identity.md is evidence-backed from the VM-203 Temur packet. This file is architecture documentation only, not raw-faction JSON, generated placement data, route behavior, or runtime behavior. `TEMUR` is a planned expression name only in this phase. `GUR` is metadata/query-only color direction.

## Identity Overview

Temur Frontier is Vox Mana's Green-centered `GUR` Tarkir wedge expression for survival through attunement, savagery, self-sufficiency, shamanic listening, elemental memory, and strength earned in harsh terrain. The VM-203 source packet supports a strong but bounded floor: Temur is the Green-Blue-Red wedge with Green as center; its design attribute is savagery; and its Tarkir expression is grounded in Qal Sisma survival, nomadic family groups, whisperers, frozen ancestors, the Wide Whisper, elemental magic, and the hard practicality of living with the land rather than conquering it.

This file keeps Temur non-live. It does not create a runtime `TEMUR` key, add `GUR` as an alias, create raw-faction JSON, create generated data, alter placement, or authorize route, Home, Maze, schema, Supabase, fixture, or builder changes.

Evidence anchors: `docs/research/temur/temur-evidence-ledger.md` rows `TEMUR-EVID-001` through `TEMUR-EVID-031`; manual-fill boundaries in `TEMUR-MF-001` through `TEMUR-MF-008`.

## Core Drive

Temur wants to survive by hearing what the world already knows before the next storm, hunger, or enemy arrives.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Green supplies the center: acceptance, harmony, inner strength, natural connection, physical growth, and life lived in accord with the surrounding world.
- Blue supplies pattern-sense: knowledge, mental fortitude, problem solving, foresight, whisperer guidance, and the ability to read possible paths without replacing the wild with an institution.
- Red supplies the immediate spark: freedom, action, emotion, impulse, heat, and the willingness to act while survival is still possible.

The core drive is not generic ferocity or generic `GUR` value. It is an evidence-bound survival engine: Green roots the clan in the living world, Blue listens for patterns in spirits, memory, and terrain, and Red turns the heard signal into action before the ice closes.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Green-Centered Survival

Green is the center of the Temur evidence floor. That keeps Temur from flattening into generic `GUR`, generic dragon/ramp Commander play, or Izzet-style cleverness with Green mana attached. The supported lens is acceptance, harmony, inner strength, natural connection, physical growth, and surviving in accord with the world.

Evidence anchors: `TEMUR-EVID-001`, `TEMUR-EVID-003`, `TEMUR-EVID-006`, `TEMUR-EVID-007`, and `TEMUR-EVID-019`.

### Savagery As Readiness

Temur savagery is not random brutality. The design source supports inner strength, mental fortitude, instinct, impulse, action, and toughness when called upon. Architecture may write savagery as readiness under pressure, but it must not turn savagery into cruelty, spectacle, or generic Red-Green aggression.

Evidence anchors: `TEMUR-EVID-002`, `TEMUR-EVID-003`, `TEMUR-EVID-004`, `TEMUR-EVID-005`, `TEMUR-EVID-006`, and `TEMUR-EVID-012`.

### Whispering As Knowledge In Service Of Nature

Temur's Blue component is strongest when it serves the land rather than mastering it. Khans-era whispering, frozen memories, the Wide Whisper, ancestor spirits, elemental affinity, and modern whisperer guidance all support knowledge as listening, divination, warning, and survival coordination.

Evidence anchors: `TEMUR-EVID-004`, `TEMUR-EVID-008`, `TEMUR-EVID-017`, `TEMUR-EVID-020`, `TEMUR-EVID-023`, and `TEMUR-EVID-024`.

### Family Bands And Harsh Terrain

Temur society is supported as family-centered, nomadic or semi-nomadic, self-sufficient, and mountain-bound. The safe architecture floor includes wandering families, the Dragonclaw, the One Who Whispers Twice or Twice Whisperer, the Hunt Caller, Qal Sisma, Karakyk, and terrain-shaped survival. Detailed interspecies governance remains `Manual fill required`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These are Temur-side placement boundaries only. They do not define Gruul, Simic, Izzet, or any other expression; they only prevent Temur from absorbing adjacent G/U/R patterns without a positive Temur evidence cluster.

### Temur And Gruul

Temur can share Red-Green force, wilderness pressure, instinct, combat readiness, and embodied survival. The Temur difference is that force is steered by Blue-touched listening, whisperer knowledge, elemental memory, and pattern-sense in service of Green-centered survival. If a candidate only shows Red-Green aggression, anti-civilization energy, big creature pressure, or generic ferocity without Temur listening or shamanic evidence, keep it out of Temur.

The Temur difference is Red immediacy and harsh-terrain action: knowledge is not detached improvement, experiment, or optimization, but a survival signal heard through ancestors, whisperers, land, weather, and possible nows. If a candidate centers improvement systems more than immediate survival through attunement, keep it away from Temur.

Evidence anchors: `TEMUR-EVID-003`, `TEMUR-EVID-004`, `TEMUR-EVID-005`, `TEMUR-EVID-008`, `TEMUR-EVID-019`, `TEMUR-EVID-023`, and `TEMUR-EVID-030`.

If a candidate is mostly cleverness, spell throughput, invention, or copy value without Green-centered survival and natural attunement, keep it away from Temur.
