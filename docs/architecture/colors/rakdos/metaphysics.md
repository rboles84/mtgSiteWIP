## Philosophical Foundations

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

This section translates approved Rakdos evidence into project-facing metaphysical language.

Rakdos begins from the premise that restraint hides information. The faction profile states that performance can expose truths politeness hides, and its placement model requires performance, sensation, transgression, or theatrical truth-seeking (`data/raw-factions/cult_of_rakdos/cult_of_rakdos.profile.json:14`; `data/raw-factions/cult_of_rakdos/cult_of_rakdos.placement.json:156`).

The metaphysical foundations are:

- Spectacle: truth becomes actionable when it has an audience.
- Appetite: desire is treated as evidence of priority.
- Pain and risk: cost marks seriousness and turns action into commitment.
- Sacrifice: resources become meaningful when spent visibly.
- Discard and hellbent-adjacent pressure: plans lose privacy and players improvise in public.
- Patronage: power is theatrical, unstable, and shaped by the terms of the act.
- Anti-drift: chaos alone is not enough; the signal must preserve performance, sensation, transgression, and danger.

## Vox Mana Read

This is a compression-only Vox Mana read. It adds no new nouns, mechanics, or doctrine; it is architecture language for placement and discovery, not official faction doctrine.

Rakdos asks what desire looks like after comfort is gone. Its metaphysical unit is the public cost: the wound, payment, sacrifice, discarded plan, risky bargain, or life-loss gate that makes appetite impossible to pretend away.

## Ludological Matrix Mapping

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

These matrix axes are derived from approved evidence and should be treated as placement and discovery language.

| Axis | Rakdos value | Strong signal | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency | Public rupture | The player wants action seen, felt, and reacted to | Private leverage drifts Dimir |
| Ethic | Appetite with visible cost | Desire, risk, and consequence are named together | Appetite without cost becomes generic Black |
| Energy | Immediate release under pressure | Damage, haste, spectacle, and all-in payoff matter | Pure release without transgression becomes mono Red |
| Form | Performance, venue, audience | The table becomes part of the experience | Technique-first expression drifts Prismari |
| Cost | Life, cards, bodies, restraint | Sacrifice, discard, and life-loss gates unlock payoff | Cost framed as debt drifts Orzhov |
| Relation to order | Anti-authoritarian provocation | Authority is mocked or exposed through performance | Duty and justice drift Boros |
| Relation to wildness | Sensation and transgressive honesty | Disruption performs a truth | Dispossession and clan grievance drift Gruul |

Ludologically, Rakdos is strongest when game actions create a visible before-and-after: someone lost life, a resource burned, a body became damage, a hand disappeared, a demon arrived, or the table had to respond to the act. It is weakest when the deck only says "chaos" without performance, appetite, pain, risk, or consequence.
