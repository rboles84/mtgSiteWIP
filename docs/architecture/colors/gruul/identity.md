# Gruul Clans - Wild Strength Against the City

> Source note: identity.md is evidence-backed. metaphysics.md is project metaphysics, not canon doctrine. Gruul is treated here as an expression-level guild, not as a mono-color doctrine file.

## Identity Overview

Gruul is Vox Mana's model for wild life answering imposed civilization with direct force, local loyalty, and strength tested in the body.

The Gruul Clans are directly supported in repo sources as Ravnica's Red-Green guild of anti-civilization pressure, Rubblebelt survival, clan identity, instinct, raiding, Riot, Bloodrush, old ways, beasts, and wild reclamation. They are not simply Red plus Green smash. The strongest Gruul evidence cluster is the collision between erased wildness and totalizing city: former stewards of Ravnica's natural places became raiders after urban expansion, other guilds' disregard, marginalization, and the loss of wilderness.

At its best, Gruul turns anger into defense of land, memory, local autonomy, and living strength outside official permission. At its worst, the same force can collapse into indiscriminate destruction, contempt for planning, or domination by whoever looks strongest in the moment.

## Core Drive

Gruul wants the wild to breathe where civilization has crushed it.

Its core drive is not generic rage, generic freedom, or violence for attention. Gruul is driven by the belief that imposed civic systems can erase living places, old ways, rough local bonds, and embodied survival. When that happens, reform from inside the system is not enough.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Red supplies immediacy, emotion, momentum, and refusal to wait for permission.
- Green supplies instinct, nature, old ways, beasts, land memory, and the right of living systems to persist.
- Gruul fuses them into wild reclamation through direct action: lived strength over title, clan force over institution, and momentum over slow procedure.

## Vox Mana Read (Core Axiom)

This section is strongly supported as Vox Mana compression derived from the already-strong `Core Drive`, `Philosophical Foundations`, `Mechanical Identity`, and `Gameplay Philosophy` sections.

Authoring rule: the read may add no new nouns, mechanics, or doctrine. It only compresses the already-supported Gruul evidence for instinct, anti-civilization pressure, lived strength, clan force, wild reclamation, direct action, Riot, Bloodrush, Bloodthirst, haste, trample, fight, land force, and momentum.

Gruul answers imposed civilization with lived strength, clan force, wild reclamation, and direct action. It trusts instinct over bureaucracy, body over title, and momentum over permission.

When land, memory, old ways, or local survival are being crushed, Gruul does not ask the system for a place in line. It moves through Riot, Bloodrush, Bloodthirst, haste, trample, fight, smash-through pressure, land force, and creature-first momentum until the wild can breathe again.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Wildness Against Total City

Direct faction data frames Gruul around lost stewardship and anti-civilization rage. The Clans were connected to wild places before the city's expansion erased or contained those places, and the placement profile treats the Gruul signal as strongest when anger is tied to dispossession, wilderness, old ways, land, clan survival, or something living being stripped away.

This makes Gruul different from broad Red rebellion. Gruul is not only "I want freedom." It is "the city took something living, called the theft progress, and now the living world must answer."

### Instinct Over Bureaucracy

The Red-Green philosophy evidence supports acting in the moment, following emotion and instinct, and opposing Blue-style overthinking. Gruul placement sharpens that into a Ravnican pattern: direct, embodied action over procedural control, official titles, careful paperwork, and systems that protect themselves while land or local life is harmed.

### Strength As Lived Proof

Gruul sources repeatedly support strength-tested authority. Clan leadership is loose, contested, and tied to proven might, survival, challenge, and the ability to lead under pressure. This is not Boros discipline or civic rank. Gruul legitimacy is earned in the field, not granted by office.

### Clan Survival And Local Belonging

Gruul is clan-based rather than centrally unified. Clan belonging matters because the Rubblebelt is dangerous and institutional legitimacy is not trusted. This keeps Gruul distinct from Selesnya: Gruul belonging is rough, local, contested, and survival-minded rather than harmonious consensus or spiritual unity.

### Protective Rage

The strongest newer placement evidence warns against reducing Gruul Rage to random aggression. Story-supported placement claims tie anger to the dying Rubblebelt, land-draining magic, bloodcrime, territorial defilement, erased stories, and systems that make wild life impossible. That evidence is interpretive and should stay bounded, but it is strong enough for Vox Mana placement language.

## Mechanical Identity

### Riot

Raw Gruul claims identify Riot as the Ravnica Allegiance Gruul keyword. Riot lets a creature enter with either a +1/+1 counter or haste, making Gruul immediacy into a gameplay fork: become bigger now or move faster now. In identity terms, Riot is wild adaptation under pressure.

### Bloodrush

Raw Gruul claims identify Bloodrush as the Gruul Gatecrash mechanic. Bloodrush turns a creature card in hand into an immediate attacking-creature bonus, which supports a combat-first pattern: force is useful before it becomes formal board presence.

### Bloodthirst

Gruul profile metadata and Gruul card research include Bloodthirst as a Gruul signal through cards such as Skarrgan Pit-Skulk and Scab-Clan Mauler. In Vox Mana terms, Bloodthirst is escalation under conflict: damage becomes memory, and memory becomes greater force.

### Haste And Trample

Mechanical color-pie research supports Red as primary in haste and Green as the main trample color, with Gruul card research repeatedly using haste, trample, and power boosts. Haste carries anti-restraint momentum. Trample carries smash-through pressure: the obstacle does not end the force.

### Fight And Direct Creature Conflict

Gruul card research and mechanical color-pie research support fight as creature-centered conflict resolution, especially through Green's creature destruction pattern. For Gruul, fight is not courtroom adjudication or hidden leverage. It is dominance, survival, and direct testing.

### Land And Nature Force

Gruul sources tie the faction to the Rubblebelt, wild reclamation, old ways, beasts, and land-based force. Gruul card research includes land damage, land animation, power from lands, and commanders that weaponize lands or creature-only mana. Mechanical color-pie research supports Green as the color most closely tied to lands and mana, with Red also supporting land destruction pressure.

### Creature-First Momentum

Commander-facing data and Gruul card research support stompy, lands, enrage, Riot, Bloodthirst, creature-first pressure, punisher lines against noncreature spell plans, and direct combat. Gruul should feel like the board becoming harder to restrain, not like an abstract combo engine or civic plan.

## Gameplay Philosophy

Gruul gameplay should feel like force becoming impossible to keep in a cage.

The player is rewarded for committing bodies, pressuring quickly, choosing bigger or faster force, turning land and creatures into damage, and resolving conflict through the battlefield. Gruul wins when the table can feel the difference between a plan that asks permission and a board state that has already broken through.

In Commander-facing terms, Gruul safely supports:

- Riot, Bloodrush, Bloodthirst, haste, trample, fight, and power boosts.
- Stompy, large creatures, creature-first pressure, and smash-through combat.
- Land-matter force, land animation, land damage, and nature reclaiming infrastructure.
- Creature-based ramp and mana bursts when they feel like wild acceleration.
- Anti-control or anti-noncreature pressure when it reads as rejection of overbuilt systems.
- Enrage-like patterns, damage memory, and escalation under conflict.
- Clan, beast, old-ways, Rubblebelt, and strength-tested leadership signals.

Gruul should not be treated as every Red-Green deck. Red-Green artifact engines, generic haste aggro, purely optimized landfall, or splashy chaos can stay adjacent unless the evidence centers wild reclamation, anti-civilization pressure, clan survival, direct embodied action, or land/nature force.

## Philosophical Weaknesses

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

This section is strongly supported by raw placement/shadow evidence in `data/raw-factions/gruul_clans/gruul_clans.placement.json`.

For this pass, "shadow evidence" refers to these raw placement fields: `moral_and_psychological_profile.possible_shadow_expression`, `poor_fit_indicators`, `placement_summary.calibrated_false_positive_guardrail`, `chatbot_guidance.do_not_overweight`, and `calibration_tuning.false_positive_guardrail`. These fields are placement heuristics, not MTG canon, so the weaknesses below record Vox Mana interpretation derived from approved evidence.

### Indiscriminate Destruction

Gruul can stop distinguishing harmful civilization from necessary cooperation. When the only tool is breaking, it may destroy shelter, memory, or trust along with the cage.

Evidence anchor: `data/raw-factions/gruul_clans/gruul_clans.placement.json` / `moral_and_psychological_profile.possible_shadow_expression.summary`, which names collapse from liberation into indiscriminate destruction and refusal to distinguish harmful civilization from necessary cooperation.

### Anti-Planning Reflex

The Red-Green strength of acting in the moment can become refusal to think beyond the next impact. Gruul is vulnerable when patience, coordination, or information would protect the same living things it wants to defend.

Evidence anchor: `data/raw-factions/gruul_clans/gruul_clans.placement.json` / `moral_and_psychological_profile.possible_shadow_expression.summary`, which names contempt for planning, plus `poor_fit_indicators`, which weakens Gruul when direct action is avoided even as something living is harmed.

### Strength As Domination

Strength-tested leadership can become rule by whoever can intimidate everyone else. The project should preserve this risk rather than romanticizing every challenge as pure authenticity.

Evidence anchor: `data/raw-factions/gruul_clans/gruul_clans.placement.json` / `moral_and_psychological_profile.possible_shadow_expression.summary`, which names domination by whoever looks strongest, plus the placement note that this is a heuristic and not an endorsement of domination.

### Narrow Rage

Gruul Rage is strongest when tied to land, memory, old ways, local autonomy, or survival. When anger loses that grounding, it risks sliding toward shallow aggression, Rakdos spectacle, or mere appetite for damage.

Evidence anchor: `data/raw-factions/gruul_clans/gruul_clans.placement.json` / `placement_summary.calibrated_false_positive_guardrail`, `chatbot_guidance.do_not_overweight`, and `calibration_tuning.false_positive_guardrail`, which all warn against generic anger or freedom without concrete loss or wildness.

### False Positive Risk

Vox Mana should resist awarding Gruul affinity for:

- Generic anger without land, loss, wildness, clan, or survival evidence.
- Generic Red-Green color identity without Gruul-specific pressure.
- Boros-style discipline, civic justice, hierarchy, or protective public duty.
- Rakdos-style indulgence, spectacle, pain-as-performance, or theatrical chaos.
- Selesnya-style harmony, consensus, or belonging through shared spiritual unity.
- Izzet experimentation for its own sake.
- Simic managed biological optimization, clades, or lab-based improvement.
- Azorius law, procedure, documentation, and restraint as primary goods.

## Operator Translation Signals (Maze / Scryfall)

### Strong Gruul Signals

- The text names Gruul, Rubblebelt, Skarrg, Burning Tree, Ghor, Zhur-Taa, Ruric Thar, Borborygmos, Domri, Nikya, Riot, Bloodrush, Bloodthirst, or old ways.
- The card uses or evokes haste, trample, fight, power boosts, creature-first ramp, land force, land animation, land damage, or anti-noncreature pressure.
- The flavor centers wild reclamation, anti-civilization pressure, rubble, ruins, clan survival, beasts, local autonomy, or direct embodied action.
- The action answers land being drained, memory being erased, old ways being mocked, or a living place being replaced by a self-protective system.
- The gameplay asks whether force should be bigger now, faster now, or impossible to contain.

### Inhibitors

- The text centers disciplined public duty, martial hierarchy, and civic protection: lean Boros.
- The text centers spectacle, indulgence, pain-as-performance, or audience: lean Rakdos.
- The text centers harmony, consensus, communal voice, and belonging: lean Selesnya.
- The text centers invention, experiments, prototypes, spell-tech, or discovery pressure: lean Izzet.
- The text centers biomancy, clades, managed adaptation, or biological optimization: lean Simic.
- The text centers law, precedent, documentation, enforcement, and procedure: lean Azorius.
- The text is only generic Red-Green aggro, generic lands, or generic monsters without wildness, loss, clan, or anti-civilization evidence: keep Gruul weak.

### Placement Guidance

For placement, Gruul should require a specific positive evidence cluster: wild, wilderness, loss, dispossession, civilization, institution, clan, survival, ruin, direct action, land, old ways, or protective rage. A single Red, Green, haste, large-creature, or anger marker is not enough.

## Summary

Gruul is wild strength against the city. It believes living places, rough local bonds, and old ways have a right to answer systems that erase them.

For Vox Mana, Gruul should be read as the faction of instinct over bureaucracy, lived strength over title, clan force over institutional legitimacy, wild adaptation over managed optimization, and momentum over slow permission. Its signature question is not "who authorized this?" but "what living thing is being crushed while authorization waits?"
