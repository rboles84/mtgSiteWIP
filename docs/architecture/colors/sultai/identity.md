## Identity Overview

Sultai Brood is Vox Mana's Black-centered `BGU` Tarkir wedge expression for ruthlessness, opportunity, graveyard/resource conversion, necromantic power, and calculated advantage. The VM-209 packet supports a bounded identity floor: Sultai is the Black-Green-Blue wedge; its design attribute is ruthlessness; Khans-era Sultai gained wealth and power through resource exploitation, necromancy, sibsig labor, naga hierarchy, rakshasa pacts, poison, mind control, and Sidisi's rule; Silumgar clan is a separate Dragons-era boundary; and modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai must remain timeline-labeled. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001` through `SULTAI-EVID-010`, `SULTAI-EVID-014` through `SULTAI-EVID-030`, and synthesis permission in `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

This file keeps Sultai non-live. It does not create a runtime `SULTAI` key, add `BGU` or any permutation as an alias, create raw-faction JSON, create generated data, alter placement, or authorize route, Home, Maze, schema, Supabase, fixture, or builder changes. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001`, `SULTAI-EVID-036`.

## Core Drive

Sultai wants to turn every available advantage into power before anyone else can claim it. In Vox Mana terms, Black supplies the center: ambition, permission, cost-paying, theft, resource denial, and the will to do what must be done. Green supplies life, death, bodies, land, growth, decay, and the reality that nothing living stays outside the cycle. Blue supplies calculation: planning, hidden information, redirection, mind pressure, and the patience to choose which resource matters now. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001`, `SULTAI-EVID-002`, `SULTAI-EVID-003`, `SULTAI-EVID-004`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-008`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

The core drive is not generic `BGU` goodstuff or a color-code shortcut. It is an evidence-bound advantage engine: Black decides that desire is permission, Green makes the living and dead world usable, and Blue makes the conversion selective rather than merely hungry. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001`, `SULTAI-EVID-003`, `SULTAI-EVID-033`, `SULTAI-EVID-035`, and `SULTAI-EVID-036`.

Black is the center of the Sultai architecture layer.
