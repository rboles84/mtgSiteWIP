> Project metaphysics notice: this file is Vox Mana architecture built from the accepted VM-209 Sultai packet. ## Metaphysical Thesis

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Sultai metaphysics treats ruthlessness as resource conversion: Black turns opportunity into permission, Green supplies life/death/resource cycles, and Blue makes the conversion planned, selective, and strategic. The canon-backed floor is narrower than the image may feel: VM-209 supports Sultai as the Black-Green-Blue wedge, ruthlessness as its design attribute, Khans-era necromancy and resource exploitation, Sidisi as the safe Khans-era ruler anchor, Silumgar clan as a separate Dragons-era boundary, and modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai as a timeline-labeled modern expression. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001` through `SULTAI-EVID-030`, `SULTAI-EVID-035`, and `SULTAI-EVID-036`.

Everything beyond that floor must cite VM-209 rows or remain `Manual fill required`: exact delve text, exact exploit keyword/mechanic text, full figure biographies, exact Dragonstorm story chronology, Commander legality, and card-level product facts. VM-211 parity material belongs to `identity.md` as docs-only boundary guidance, not metaphysical canon or runtime behavior. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-MF-001` through `SULTAI-MF-010`.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Opportunity Is Permission

In this architecture layer, Sultai begins from Black's ruthlessness: desire can justify action, cost can be paid, and advantage is worth taking. This is `Vox Mana synthesis` from the design floor and must stay tied to the approved rows for ruthlessness, theft, resource denial, additional costs, and the "whatever it takes" posture. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-002`, `SULTAI-EVID-003`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

### Life And Death Are Convertible

Green enters Sultai not as innocence or harmony, but as the material reality of life, death, bodies, land, growth, poison, and decay. VM-209 supports Khans-era Sultai power through people, land resources, necromancy, sibsig labor, undead army resupply, body-part ritual practice, and poison or warping magic. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-004`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-008`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

### Calculation Chooses The Pressure Point

Blue makes Sultai resource use selective. The approved design and lore rows support theft/redirect, hand/library pressure, mind control or lotus perfume, planning, spies, scouts, diplomacy, and terrain knowledge, but VM-210 must not convert that into uncited generic espionage doctrine or full placement guidance. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-003`, `SULTAI-EVID-008`, `SULTAI-EVID-021`, `SULTAI-EVID-024`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

### Luxury, Death, And Rule

Khans-era Sultai metaphysics can read wealth as captured advantage because VM-209 supports the Brood's wealth through exploitation of people and land, necromantic power, naga rulership claims, rakshasa pacts, and Sidisi's inherited wealth and imperial ambition. This is not permission to add uncaptured court politics, biographies, or card-story details. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-004`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-006`, `SULTAI-EVID-009`, `SULTAI-MF-003`, and `SULTAI-MF-004`.

### The Dead Split By Timeline

The dead are not a single Sultai doctrine across eras. Khans-era Sultai use sibsig labor and necromancy; Silumgar clan repurposes corpses and harvests life force in a dragonlord hierarchy; modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai honor the dead through Abiding Harvest and Rite of Renewal, with unwilling raising outlawed. VM-210 may compare those boundaries only when timeline-labeled. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-017`, `SULTAI-EVID-026`, and `SULTAI-EVID-030`.

### Silumgar As Domination Boundary

Silumgar is the shadow boundary around Sultai metaphysics. Silumgar clan supports a version of ruthlessness where power and knowledge serve the dragonlord, living followers are few, undead numbers dominate, and worth depends on Silumgar's caprice. That contrast can sharpen Sultai architecture, but it cannot become Khans-era Sultai Brood identity. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-014`, `SULTAI-EVID-015`, `SULTAI-EVID-016`, `SULTAI-EVID-017`, `SULTAI-EVID-018`, and `SULTAI-EVID-019`.

### Dragonstorm As Reformed Counter-Image

Modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai shows that Sultai metaphysics can be reformed, but only inside its own timeline. The modern clan rejects Silumgar and old Sultai oppression, rejects rakshasa pacts, decentralizes power, protects honored dead, and uses trade, diplomacy, spies, scouts, life-essence magic, and dragonstorm adaptation. VM-210 may use this as contrast and modern boundary, not as a retcon of Khans-era Brood doctrine. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-020` through `SULTAI-EVID-030`, `SULTAI-MF-006`, and `SULTAI-MF-007`.

## Vox Mana Read

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Sultai is the wedge that asks what power can be made from the thing everyone else calls spent. A body can become labor. A death can become leverage. A secret can become a route. A cost can become proof that the goal mattered enough. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-002`, `SULTAI-EVID-003`, `SULTAI-EVID-004`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-008`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

Its sacred pattern is not simple rot, simple cunning, or simple greed. It is conversion under pressure: Black names the desire, Green supplies the material cycle, and Blue chooses the efficient transformation. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-001`, `SULTAI-EVID-003`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-021`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

## Primary Tension

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Sultai's primary tension is that resource use can become domination when ambition and calculation overrule restraint. The same life/death awareness that can make nothing wasted can also make people, land, corpses, secrets, and even ancestors feel like inventory. The same Blue planning that keeps a clan alive can refine cruelty into system. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-002`, `SULTAI-EVID-004`, `SULTAI-EVID-005`, `SULTAI-EVID-008`, `SULTAI-EVID-017`, `SULTAI-EVID-026`, and `SULTAI-EVID-035`.

This tension is not a claim about every Sultai individual, every era, every card, or every Commander expression. Khans-era Sultai, Fate Reforged Sultai, Silumgar clan, and modern Dragonstorm-era Sultai must remain distinct. Evidence anchors: `SULTAI-EVID-011` through `SULTAI-EVID-030`, `SULTAI-MF-003` through `SULTAI-MF-007`.
