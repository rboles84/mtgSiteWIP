# Quandrix College - The Mathematics of Reality

## Identity Overview

Quandrix College is a Strixhaven expression-level faction centered on mathematics as magic. Its identity is not generic Blue-Green overlap and not a Simic variant: Quandrix treats reality as pattern, proof, equation, model, symmetry, proportion, recursion, and scale. It is the school that looks at the natural world and asks what hidden structure makes it possible.

The approved repo evidence supports Quandrix through direct raw claims for numeromancy, mathematical structure, nature and magic as patterns, and the question of whether mathematics is found or made. `data/factions.json` also preserves Quandrix's Strixhaven expression as abstract pattern, fractal growth, land and mana scaling, counters, copying, doubling, and the "math of more" gameplay profile.

## Core Drive

Quandrix's core drive is to reveal, model, and reshape reality through mathematical insight. It seeks the truth beneath appearances: the equation behind the tree, the recursion inside growth, the symmetry inside magic, the proportion that lets abstraction become physical.

Its growth is not biomantic improvement. Its experimentation is not invention-first prototyping. Quandrix advances by discovering or constructing the correct model, proving the pattern, and letting the pattern change the world.

## Vox Mana Read (Core Axiom)

This Vox Mana read is compression-only: no new doctrine. It compresses approved repo evidence into a usable project axiom without presenting the sentence as MTG canon.

> Reality has a mathematical skeleton; magic is the act of perceiving, proving, and scaling that hidden structure until abstraction becomes embodied fact.

## Philosophical Foundations

Quandrix rests on numeromancy: mathematics as an operative magical language. Its direct evidence supports mathematical structure, hidden natural and magical patterns, fractals, symmetries, and scale as its distinctive field of meaning.

The college's central philosophical tension is the relation between natural reality and theoretical model. Repo claims and raw profile evidence support both wonder at the structure beneath the world and the found-vs-made question of mathematics: is Quandrix discovering the world's real pattern, or imposing a model powerful enough to become real?

That tension is the heart of the school. Quandrix does not simply admire nature, engineer life, or archive evidence. It abstracts, models, proves, iterates, and then watches the model acquire physical force.

## Mechanical Identity

Quandrix's mechanical identity is strongly supported by raw profile and faction data: Fractals, counters, scaling, doubling, copying, land and mana growth, token multiplication, and pattern-based escalation.

Fractals are the clearest bridge between metaphysics and game object. They are mathematical forms made creaturely, a visible expression of abstraction becoming physical.

Counters and scalable bodies express measured growth: increase as quantity, proof as accumulation, and development as mathematical progression.

Land and mana scaling express reality as a manipulable resource field. Quandrix often turns more lands, more mana, and more variables into larger outputs.

Doubling, copying, and token scaling are the gameplay face of proportion, recursion, and the "math of more." The faction's board states should feel like a proof expanding across the table.

## Gameplay Philosophy

In Commander terms, Quandrix wants the pilot to feel that a hidden formula is becoming visible. The deck should reward planning, scaling, proportional growth, and engines that turn small mathematical advantages into overwhelming board presence.

Quandrix gameplay should prioritize pattern recognition over raw mutation, spectacle, bureaucracy, sacrifice exchange, or invention chaos. The best play experience feels like solving for the board: lands and mana become variables, tokens become repeated terms, counters become measured increments, and Fractals become equations with power and toughness.
