## Structural & Mechanical Architecture

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

Fractals are the most explicit metaphysical object: mathematical abstraction embodied as creature form. They make the hidden pattern visible.

Counters express quantified change. Growth becomes measured, stackable, and trackable rather than purely organic or emotional.

Scaling bodies and tokens express proportion. They make power and toughness feel like variables in an expanding formula.

Mana and land growth express the world as an adjustable resource field. More lands and more mana become more terms available to the proof.

Doubling and copying express recursion and multiplication. They turn a single pattern into repeated structure, then let repeated structure become overwhelming presence.

Pattern-based growth is the bridge between philosophy and play: the board state should feel as if a mathematical model has crossed from notation into matter.
