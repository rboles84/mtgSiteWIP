## Core Identity

Witch is the four-color discipline of cultivated inevitability. It believes power is not proven by acting first, burning brightest, or following the immediate emotional spark. Power is proven by building the structure that keeps growing after the table has noticed too late.

The missing Red center matters. Witch does not naturally begin from passion, spontaneity, or the desire to live inside the present moment. It begins from patient development, calculated expansion, systematic accumulation, and the refusal to mistake impulse for wisdom. (`WITCH-EVID-003`, `WITCH-EVID-007`)

Witch's central question is not "What do I feel now?" but "What will still be growing when the moment has passed?" It treats time as a resource, silence as a workshop, and every small investment as material for a larger shape.

## Present Colors

Green gives Witch cultivation. Growth is not ornamental here; it is the living base of the lane, the seed that can become a forest when protected long enough.

White gives Witch architecture. The lane does not merely grow outward; it organizes growth through systems, boundaries, order, and durable community structure.

Blue gives Witch calculation. It studies sequence, information, and optimization so that expansion is not left to accident.

Black gives Witch ambition. It gives the lane the willingness to pursue power, preserve advantage, and keep the long plan from dissolving into passive stewardship.

Without Red, Witch distrusts the unscheduled blaze. It can still be beautiful, severe, and alive, but its aliveness is pruned, measured, and aimed at permanence rather than release. (`WITCH-EVID-003`, `WITCH-EVID-007`)

## Red Absence

Red is the missing color and the defining wound.

Its absence removes the native pull toward emotional immediacy, expressive release, haste, spectacle, and action for the sake of feeling alive. Witch can therefore become unusually patient and difficult to disrupt, but it can also become sterile when planning crowds out joy.

This should stay precise. The Red absence does not prove that every slow deck, counter deck, or `GWUB` shell is Witch. It identifies the pressure that makes the lane distinct: growth through deliberate cultivation rather than spontaneous combustion. (`WITCH-EVID-003`, `WITCH-EVID-007`, `WITCH-MF-012`)

Red is absent, so the pattern distrusts impulse, spectacle, and sudden combustion. This is synthesis, not direct canon. Keep it bounded to the approved Witch frame rather than treating it as MTG source text. (`WITCH-EVID-003`, `WITCH-EVID-004`, `WITCH-EVID-005`, `WITCH-EVID-007`, `WITCH-EVID-009`)

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These are present-color pair boundaries only. They explain why a two-color overlap can feel near Witch without becoming Witch by itself. Shard, wedge, and four-color comparisons belong in the separator section below. (`WITCH-EVID-003`, `WITCH-EVID-007`, `WITCH-MF-012`)

Bant can look close because it already carries Green, White, and Blue cultivation, order, and knowledge. It is still missing Black. Without Black ambition, self-interested leverage, and willingness to preserve advantage, Bant remains too serene and civic to stand in for Witch's cold expansion.
