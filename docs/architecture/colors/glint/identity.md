## Identity Overview

Glint is Vox Mana's UBRG, non-White four-color expression label. Chaos is the Commander 2016 support/theme alias for the same lane. Neither should be written as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for UBRG, and Chaos must not be presented as Wizards' official name for all UBRG identity.

Glint is defined by the missing color. Blue, Black, Red, and Green remain present, but White is absent. The resulting pressure is not simply "four good colors together." It is a field of appetite, adaptation, improvisation, and self-directed power that refuses civic restraint, moral symmetry, and imposed social order.

Glint is not an official MTG faction. It is not a plane, cult, school, or institution. It is Vox Mana's guarded identity architecture for the non-White lane.

## Core Drive

Glint wants motion without civic restraint.

It does not begin by asking how a community should stabilize itself. It asks what can be learned, taken, risked, and unleashed before the moment closes. Blue studies the storm, Black claims advantage inside it, Red rides its force, and Green lets power remain wild instead of bureaucratic.

White is absent, so the identity does not seek peace through law, duty, or common order. It is driven by appetite, improvisation, escalation, and the refusal to let collective structure decide what power is allowed to become.

Evidence floor: `GLINT-EVID-003`, `GLINT-EVID-006`, and `GLINT-EVID-009`.

This is synthesis, not direct canon. Keep it bounded to the approved Glint frame rather than treating it as MTG source text.

## Blue Role

Blue gives Glint perception, adaptive intelligence, and the urge to understand unstable systems instead of avoiding them. In this lane, knowledge does not exist to create safe institutions. It exists to stay ahead of the surge and turn unpredictability into advantage.

This is Blue under pressure, not Azorius civic order and not a purely detached laboratory stance.

## Black Role

Black gives Glint appetite, self-prioritization, and the willingness to benefit from volatility rather than mourn it. It refuses the idea that communal obligation should outrank survival, ambition, or desire.

This is Black as predatory agency, not proof that every selfish or ruthless deck belongs here.

## Red Role

Red gives Glint ignition. It supplies immediacy, risk, velocity, and the willingness to let events cascade beyond careful containment. Without Red, the identity would still be dangerous, but it would lose the felt shock that makes the lane kinetic rather than merely opportunistic.

This is Red as momentum and volatility, not Rakdos spectacle by default.

## Green Role

Green gives Glint living force, instinct, and growth that is not ashamed of being wild. It keeps the lane from becoming only cerebral or only exploitative. Even at its most cunning, Glint still trusts that life can push outward through pressure instead of waiting for official permission.

This is Green as feral vitality, not Sultai ecology and not Temur's communal natural wisdom.

## White Absence

White is the missing color and the defining wound.

Its absence removes the drive toward shared peace, civic symmetry, moral obligation, and stable public order. Glint does not naturally ask what structure protects everyone. It asks what structure gets in the way of appetite, adaptation, and self-directed force.

That absence does not prove that every UBRG deck is Glint. It identifies the philosophical pressure that makes the lane distinct: power without White-style civic restraint.

Evidence floor: `GLINT-EVID-003`, `GLINT-EVID-006`, and `GLINT-MF-005`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These boundaries are present-color pair boundaries only. They explain why a two-color overlap is real but insufficient by itself, not why the overlap proves Glint. Shard, wedge, and four-color comparisons belong in the near-match separator section below.

### Glint And Dimir (UB)

Dimir overlap can supply secrecy, adaptation, opportunism, and intelligence used for self-advantage. It is insufficient when the center is only covert control or information asymmetry. Glint also needs Red ignition, Green living force, and the missing-White refusal of civic restraint.

### Glint And Izzet (UR)

Izzet overlap can supply experimentation, volatility, curiosity, and spell momentum. It is insufficient when the center is only discovery or spectacle. Glint also needs Black appetite and Green instinct so experimentation becomes predatory and alive rather than merely clever.

### Glint And Simic (UG)

Simic overlap can supply adaptation, growth, and living intelligence. It is insufficient when the center is improvement through managed systems or ecological design. Glint also needs Black self-prioritization and Red ignition so adaptation becomes hungry, volatile, and unlicensed.

### Glint And Rakdos (BR)

Rakdos overlap can supply appetite, risk, immediate force, and delighted volatility. It is insufficient when the center is spectacle, pain, or hedonism alone. Glint also needs Blue's adaptive reading of unstable systems and Green's living push beyond mere performance.

### Glint And Golgari (BG)

Golgari overlap can supply hunger, survival pressure, and life/death materialism. It is insufficient when the center is cycle, rot, or ecological reclamation. Glint also needs Blue's quick intelligence and Red's ignition so predation becomes storm-like rather than patient or compostive.

### Glint And Gruul (RG)

Gruul overlap can supply feral force, wild momentum, and refusal of imposed order. It is insufficient when the center is instinct without analysis or rebellion without leverage. Glint also needs Blue's pressured knowledge and Black's self-directed appetite so wildness becomes calculating as well as explosive.

## Missing-Color And Near-Match Separators

Each separator explains why an adjacent identity is insufficient or only partially overlapping. Do not use these comparisons to claim that the adjacent identity is secretly Glint, proof of Glint, or a hidden official Glint faction.

### Grixis-Facing Boundary

Grixis can look close because it already carries Blue, Black, and Red ambition, volatility, and predatory intelligence. It is still missing Green. Without living force, instinct, and unlicensed growth, Grixis material should not be treated as Glint unless the source also supports the UBRG/non-White frame.

### Jund-Facing Boundary

Jund can look close because it already carries Black, Red, and Green appetite, force, and survival pressure. It is still missing Blue. Without adaptive intelligence, analytical self-revision, and pressured knowledge, Jund material should not be treated as Glint unless the source also supports the UBRG/non-White frame.

### Temur-Facing Boundary

Temur can overlap through Blue, Red, and Green motion, experimentation, and living force. It is still missing Black. Without self-prioritizing appetite and predatory willingness to benefit from volatility, Temur material remains too communal, exploratory, or open-ended to stand in for Glint.

### Sultai-Facing Boundary

Sultai can overlap through Blue, Black, and Green resource conversion, ambition, and adaptive growth. It is still missing Red. Without ignition, immediacy, and willingness to let the system cascade beyond containment, Sultai material remains too slow, cold, or controlled to stand in for Glint.

### Omnath / Non-Black Four-Color Value Boundary

Omnath and other non-Black four-color value shells can look superficially similar because they are explosive, resource-rich, and capable of dramatic turns. They are still missing Black and often include White. Without Black appetite and with White-style stability or abundance in the mix, they should not be used as Glint proof.

### Yidris-Only Boundary

Yidris is a support-only Commander anchor, not a universal definition of the lane. A Yidris shell can illuminate volatility, cascade, and four-color-without-White texture without making every Yidris list, cascade list, or combat-trigger shell identical with Glint.

### Generic Chaos And Cascade Boundary

Chaos and cascade language can help explain how the lane feels, but variance alone is insufficient. A deck or character can be wild, high-variance, or disruption-oriented without expressing the full Glint frame of appetite, adaptive intelligence, living force, and missing-White pressure.

### Generic UBRG Goodstuff Boundary

Color identity alone is insufficient. `UBRG`, plus all 23 same-color permutations, remains metadata/query-only until later review and promotion work. A deck being UBRG does not make it Glint unless the architecture signals and separator boundaries also align.

## Non-Runtime Identity Signals

### Strong Glint Signals

- The user wants power that stays adaptive, hungry, volatile, and alive rather than socially stabilized.
- The language favors storm, maelstrom, appetite, leverage, improvisation, predation, volatility, and living force used for self-directed advantage.
- Blue knowledge is present, but it serves speed, leverage, or survival rather than civic clarity.
- Green growth is present, but it feels wild, opportunistic, and unlicensed rather than harmonious or protected.
- Commander support leans toward Yidris-style spell momentum, cascade texture, and non-White four-color greed/value pressure.

### Inhibitors

- The center is stable public order, duty, fairness, or peace-through-law.
- The center is only Grixis cruelty, only Jund predation, only Temur exploration, or only Sultai exploitation without the full missing-White frame.
- The supposed Glint case depends only on generic cascade, generic chaos, generic high-variance play, or color identity alone.
- The claim depends on exact card facts, legality, Commander recommendations, or unmanaged-draft phrasing not yet audited into later stages.
