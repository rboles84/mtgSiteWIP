## Metaphysical Thesis

Project framing boundary: this thesis is Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; it is not MTG canon.

Selesnya metaphysics treats selfhood as relation. A person becomes more peaceful, more useful, and more real by joining a living community whose shared life is greater than any isolated member.

The canon-backed floor is narrow but strong: Selesnya is associated with collectivism, the natural world, unity, togetherness, preservation, communal voice over individual voice, Mat'Selesnya, Trostani, Vitu-Ghazi, and Convoke as a mechanic of communal spellcasting (`data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.claims.json`; `data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.profile.json`). The metaphysical layer translates that evidence into Vox Mana project architecture.

## Philosophical Foundations

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

This section translates approved Selesnya evidence into project-facing metaphysical language.

### The Self As Membership

The raw profile states that the self finds peace by becoming part of a greater living community. Vox Mana reads that as a relational metaphysics: the individual is not erased by default, but is asked to locate meaning in belonging rather than private distinction.

### Harmony As A Maintained Field

Selesnya harmony is not decorative calm. Placement evidence requires belonging, harmony, community, consensus, peace, collective life, nurture, and selflessness, and it suppresses answers that center conflict for its own sake or individual distinction above belonging (`data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.placement.json`).

### Growth As Shared Body

Selesnya growth is not isolated optimization. Raw and Commander sources describe many creatures mattering together: tokens, populate, Convoke, go-wide boards, lifegain through community, and board-wide growth (`data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.profile.json`; `docs/reference/commander-faction-guidance.md`).

### Peace As Interdependence

Rosewater Green-White philosophy material supports the broader backdrop: Green-White puts group welfare first, recognizes responsibility, interdependence, symbiosis, and the greater good, and turns a horde of creatures into something greater than the sum of its parts (`docs/research/canon/mark_rosewater_official_two_color/selesnya_Group Think _ MAGIC_ THE GATHERING.md`; `docs/research/canon/mark_rosewater_official_misc/Allied_Color_Pairings_Explained.md`). This is color-pie support, not Selesnya-specific doctrine.

### The Shadow Of Unity

The same structure creates Selesnya's shadow. Direct evidence says Selesnya asks the individual voice to yield to the communal one, and placement asks when belonging makes someone more themselves versus when it erases them (`data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.profile.json`; `data/raw-factions/selesnya_conclave/selesnya_conclave.placement.json`).

## Vox Mana Read

This is a compression-only Vox Mana read. It adds no new nouns, mechanics, or doctrine; it is architecture language for placement and discovery, not official faction doctrine.

Selesnya asks what a self becomes when peace is not solitude but participation. Its metaphysical unit is the shared life: the chorus, community, board, grove, conclave, token mass, or convoked spell where each member's value increases because it belongs to the whole.

The metaphysical shape is shared life -> collective body -> preservation through unity. Tokens, lifegain, enchantress engines, nature language, or order/community language are Selesnya only when they preserve that shape.
