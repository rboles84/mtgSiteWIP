# Grixis Shard - Survival Through Exploitation, Calculation, And Volatility

> Source note: identity.md is evidence-backed from the VM-164 Grixis packet. It is architecture documentation only, not raw-faction JSON, generated placement data, or runtime behavior. `GRIXIS` is the expression name; `UBR` is color direction metadata only.

## Identity Overview

Grixis is Vox Mana's Black-centered `UBR` expression for the Alara shard `GRIXIS`. The VM-164 source packet supports a narrow but strong identity floor: Grixis is the blue-black-red shard discussed in the local official Rosewater Grixis article, Black is the center/design lens, and the shard's safe architecture foundation is survival, self-advocacy, adaptation to reality, Blue calculation, Red immediacy, Red zeal, volatility, and the absence of White/Green pressures.

This file does not make Grixis live. It does not add `GRIXIS` to placement, add `UBR` as an alias, create raw-faction JSON, or authorize generated/runtime changes.

Evidence anchors: `docs/research/grixis/grixis-evidence-ledger.md` rows `GRIXIS-001` through `GRIXIS-008`, plus the non-live boundary in `GRIXIS-024`.

## Core Drive

Grixis wants to survive the world as it is, then turn the opening into advantage before the opening closes.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Black supplies the center: survival, agency, self-advocacy, self-interest, and adaptation to reality.
- Blue supplies calculation: subtle problem-solving, study, planning, weakness analysis, and information leverage.
- Red supplies immediacy: action, individual priority, zeal, willingness to act, and volatility.

The core drive is not generic cruelty. It is an evidence-bound survival engine: Black decides that surviving matters first, Blue finds the weakness, and Red acts with urgency once the weakness is visible.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-002`, `GRIXIS-003`, `GRIXIS-004`, `GRIXIS-005`, and `GRIXIS-006`.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Black-Centered Survival

Black is the center of Grixis's VM-164 evidence floor. That keeps the expression from flattening into generic `UBR` spells, generic villain coding, or a pile of Blue-Red tactics with Black decoration. The supported lens is survival, self-advocacy, agency, self-interest, and adapting to the world as it is.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-002`, `GRIXIS-003`.

### Calculation As Leverage

Blue contributes subtle problem-solving, study, planning, weakness analysis, and exploitation of information inside the Black-centered frame. This means Grixis can be intelligent, tactical, and structured at the design level. VM-165 must not claim Grixis lacks intelligence or structure because it lacks White/Green.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-004`; boundary support in `docs/research/grixis/grixis-manual-fill.md` rows `GRIXIS-MF-013` and `GRIXIS-MF-014`.

### Immediacy And Volatility

Red contributes action, individual priority, zeal, willingness to act, and volatility. The architecture read should preserve both halves: Red gives Grixis urgency, but that urgency does not erase Blue's calculation or Black's center.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-005`, `GRIXIS-006`.

### Missing White And Green Pressures

VM-164 supports White and Green as the missing enemy-color pressures in the Grixis shard frame. Architecture may say this removes moral/order constraint and life/renewal pressure at the design level. It must not expand that into unsupported claims that Grixis has no systems, no intelligence, no social logic, or no structure.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-007`; manual-fill boundaries `GRIXIS-MF-013`, `GRIXIS-MF-014`.

### Not Simply Evil

Grixis should not be reduced to "evil UBR." The source frame is Black explaining its own philosophy and complicating the flattening of Black or Grixis into simple evil. Vox Mana may describe danger, exploitation, pressure, and ruthless survival, but it must keep those terms tied to the source-bound survival frame.

Evidence anchors: `GRIXIS-008`.

- generic villain coding without Black-centered survival, Blue calculation, and Red immediacy
- generic "evil UBR" language
- Maestros/New Capenna aesthetics or social logic

- any claim that Bolas rules Grixis
- any claim that Sedris rules all of Grixis
- unearth as the whole identity
- claims that Black fully resolves all Blue/Red tension
- claims that Grixis lacks intelligence, structure, or social logic because it lacks White/Green
