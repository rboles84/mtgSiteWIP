## Identity Overview

Silverquill College is Strixhaven's white-black College of Eloquence. Direct raw claims identify Silverquill as a school of word magic, spoken-word battle poetry, magical written manifestations, and spoken, written, and signed word appearing as calligraphic strokes of ink and light.

Silverquill should read as language made operational: voice, inscription, critique, praise, insult, presence, reputation, and public influence becoming real pressure. It is not Orzhov debt logic, Dimir secrecy, Azorius procedure, Boros martial duty, Prismari elemental spectacle, Rakdos provocation, or Selesnya belonging.

Silverquill's core drive is to make words change power relationships in public.

The raw profile states the philosophy directly: language can inspire, expose, command, wound, defend, and transform identity. The school tension asks whether eloquence should uplift others or advance personal dominance.

## Philosophical Foundations

Silverquill is founded on the claim that language is action. Speech, writing, and signing are not ornamental. They are magical media that can inspire, command, wound, defend, expose, and reshape identity.

Direct claims support three school-specific foundations:

- Silverquill is Strixhaven's white-black College of Eloquence.
- Silverquill mages practice word magic through battle poetry and written magical manifestation.
- Silverquill magic can appear as calligraphic strokes of ink and light.

### Radiance And Shadow

Silverquill's central tension is uplift versus dominance. Radiance language supports inspiration, defense, leadership, and public courage. Shadow language supports critique, humiliation, intimidation, and pressure. Vox Mana should keep both as uses of verbal art rather than flattening one into pure virtue or the other into generic villainy.

### Precision And Audience

Silverquill cares not only what is said, but how, where, and before whom it is said. The placement file requires language as power, reputation, public influence, precision of speech, or verbal pressure.

### Academic Frame

The 2026 guide claim lists linguistics, political science, sociology, education, law, espionage, and journalism as Silverquill study areas. These support the school as a public-language institution without converting it into Azorius law or Dimir secrecy.

Evidence anchor:

- `data/raw-factions/silverquill/silverquill.claims.json -> silverquill_claim_005`

### Inklings

Inklings are Silverquill when they read as materialized words, ink, shadows, argument, or social pressure. Token play should preserve the school signal rather than becoming generic go-wide math.

### Counters And Status

Counters become Silverquill when they mark confidence, reputation, rank, public praise, humiliation, discipline, or status pressure.

### Politics And Combat Negotiation

Silverquill can redirect combat through incentives, demagoguery, goad-like table pressure, or public leverage. Commander guidance names political pressure, words as power, go-wide rhetoric, and combat negotiation as owned themes.

### Targeting, Removal, And Verdicts

Targeted spells, removal, and modal commands fit when they feel like precise language: naming the problem, issuing the verdict, elevating one voice, or cutting another from the conversation.

## Gameplay Philosophy

Silverquill gameplay should feel like rhetoric made tactical. The deck, recommendation, or placement answer should show how words alter the board, table, or social field.

Strong Silverquill gameplay signals include:

- political pressure and combat negotiation;
- visible status through counters, reputation, or public incentives;
- Inklings as word/ink/shadow manifestations;
- targeted spells, combat tricks, and removal as precise rhetorical acts;
- cards or play patterns that inspire allies, humiliate enemies, or redirect the audience.

Weak or ambiguous Silverquill signals include:

- generic black-white aristocrats with no language, status, reputation, or public influence;
- pure Orzhov debt, contracts, tithe, afterlife, or ledgers;
- pure Dimir secrecy, anonymity, or hidden information;
- pure Boros combat buffs without rhetoric;
- pure Selesnya go-wide belonging without verbal force.
