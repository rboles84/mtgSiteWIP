# Naya Shard - Abundance Through Scale, Belonging, And Instinct

> Source note: identity.md is evidence-backed from the VM-181 Naya packet. It is architecture documentation only, not raw data, generated placement data, or runtime behavior. `NAYA` is the expression name; `RGW` is color direction metadata only in this phase.

## Identity Overview

Naya is Vox Mana's Green-centered `RGW` expression for the Alara shard `NAYA`. The VM-181 source packet supports a narrow but strong identity floor: Naya is the red-green-white shard discussed in the local official Naya design source, Green is the center/design lens, and the shard's safe architecture foundation is life, growth, nature, belonging in a larger natural whole, White-supported larger-picture care, Red-supported instinct and loyalty, and the absence of Blue/Black pressures.

This file keeps Naya authored/review-gated. It does not make Naya live, placement-eligible, or runtime-visible. It does not add `NAYA` to placement, promote `RGW`, create raw data, or authorize generated/runtime changes.

Evidence anchors: `docs/research/naya/naya-evidence-ledger.md` rows `NAYA-EVID-001` through `NAYA-EVID-013`, plus non-live boundary `NAYA-EVID-022`.

## Core Drive

Naya wants life to become fully itself at the scale nature already promises.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Green supplies the center: life, growth, nature, belonging, role, ecosystem, and the living whole.
- White supplies care for the larger picture: life, creatures, greater-good framing, and protection of the whole without becoming institutional doctrine.
- Red supplies instinct: feral immediacy, loyalty, bond, and action before overthinking.

The core drive is not generic big creatures or generic tokens. It is an evidence-bound abundance engine: Green lets life grow toward its fullest expression, White keeps that growth oriented toward the larger whole, and Red keeps it alive, immediate, and instinctive.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Green-Centered Abundance

Green is the center of Naya's VM-181 evidence floor. That keeps the expression from flattening into generic `RGW`, generic creature combat, or White/Red spectacle with Green scenery. The supported lens is life, nature, growth, ecosystem belonging, role, and the living whole.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-002`, `NAYA-EVID-003`, `NAYA-EVID-004`, and `NAYA-EVID-005`.

### Belonging In The Living Whole

VM-181 supports Naya as a place where life is read as part of a larger natural whole. Vox Mana may describe belonging, role, and ecosystem as identity-level language, but it must not turn that into detailed social structure, law, tribe hierarchy, named-location politics, or religion.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-004`, `NAYA-EVID-006`, `NAYA-EVID-024`, `NAYA-MF-001`, `NAYA-MF-002`, `NAYA-MF-003`, and `NAYA-MF-007`.

### Instinct As Living Action

Red contributes feral instinct, immediacy, loyalty, and bond. In the Naya frame, Red prevents abundance from becoming managed stillness. It lets life answer quickly and bodily, but it does not certify specific hunting customs, clans, or creature-culture hierarchy.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-007`, `NAYA-EVID-008`, `NAYA-EVID-021`, and `NAYA-MF-007`.

### Care Without Over-Structure

White contributes the larger-picture and greater-good frame. VM-182 may use care, protection, and communal concern as architecture language only when tied to the evidence floor. It must not infer laws, councils, offices, priesthoods, social ranks, or named institutions.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-006`, `NAYA-EVID-008`, `NAYA-EVID-024`, `NAYA-MF-001`, `NAYA-MF-002`, and `NAYA-MF-007`.

### Missing Blue And Black Pressures

VM-181 supports Blue and Black as missing enemy-color pressures at the design level. Architecture may describe this as absence of controlling technology/intellectual mastery and self-interested ecological extraction. It must not claim Naya lacks thought, strategy, conflict, ambition in every person, or danger.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-009`, `NAYA-EVID-011`, and `NAYA-MF-007`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These sections explain how Naya expresses Selesnya, Gruul, and Boros overlaps from the Naya side only. They do not expand or redefine guild doctrine, and they do not make two-color articles into Naya story, place, people, creature-culture, or doctrine evidence.

**Separator:** Selesnya-like GW community becomes Naya only when communal care is centered in Green abundance and given Red instinct, immediacy, and living motion.

Naya can share creature-centered care, life, and larger-picture language with GW texture. It should not become pure harmony, consensus, or go-wide community as the whole identity. If the reading centers social unity without feral immediacy or living scale, keep it away from Naya unless Green-centered abundance and Red instinct are also doing the work.

This is Naya-side placement guidance, not Selesnya doctrine.

## Shard Separators

These are lightweight contrast notes only. They contrast Naya against Bant, Esper, Grixis, Jund, Abzan, and Temur from Naya's VM-181/VM-182 evidence floor and broad color-structure differences; they do not author positive doctrine for other shards.

### Naya And Bant

**Separator:** Bant-facing order regulates life through public honor, hierarchy, and sanctioned excellence. Naya centers Green abundance first, then lets White care and Red instinct support the living whole.

Naya should not win when the strongest signals are rank, public duty, formal honor, or a champion acting as the endorsed face of an ordered society. Naya should strengthen when life, growth, belonging, scale, instinct, and protection are doing the identity work.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-003` through `NAYA-EVID-011`, and comparator row `NAYA-EVID-019`.

### Naya And Esper

**Separator:** Esper-facing control seeks improvement through structure, information, and design. Naya treats controlling mastery and self-interested extraction as missing pressures and trusts life, instinct, and belonging as first signals.

Naya should not win when the strongest signals are artifact perfection, planned alteration, procedural optimization, or information control. Naya should strengthen when the reading wants natural growth, embodied immediacy, and living-world belonging rather than redesign.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-003`, `NAYA-EVID-004`, `NAYA-EVID-005`, `NAYA-EVID-009`, `NAYA-EVID-011`, and comparator row `NAYA-EVID-019`.

### Naya And Grixis

**Separator:** Grixis-facing pressure centers survival, calculation, and exploitation. Naya shares Red urgency with Grixis only at the surface; Naya's center is Green abundance rather than survival through leverage.

Naya should not import death-world, necromancy, exploitation, or calculated weakness-map framing. If the reading centers ruthless survival, hidden leverage, or death as the main pressure, keep it away from Naya. Naya should strengthen when the reading is warmer, life-forward, and more rooted in belonging than advantage.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-003`, `NAYA-EVID-004`, `NAYA-EVID-007`, `NAYA-EVID-009`, `NAYA-EVID-011`, and comparator row `NAYA-EVID-019`.

### Naya And Jund

**Separator:** Jund is the closest shard-level false positive because it shares Red and Green. From Naya's side, the split is Green-centered abundance and belonging against Jund-facing appetite, consumption, and survival pressure.

Naya should not win for appetite, overconsumption, self-advocacy under pressure, or Red-centered instinct alone. Naya should strengthen when instinct serves the larger living whole and when scale reads as abundance rather than consumption.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-003`, `NAYA-EVID-004`, `NAYA-EVID-005`, `NAYA-EVID-007`, `NAYA-EVID-011`, `NAYA-EVID-023`, and comparator row `NAYA-EVID-019`.

### Naya And Abzan

**Separator:** Abzan-like WBG durability becomes Naya-like only when the center is Green abundance in immediate living motion, not endurance, lineage, obligation, or ancestor-weighted continuity.

Naya can share life, protection, and belonging signals with WBG textures. It should not become survival through tradition, clan endurance, or graveyard-rooted memory as the main identity. This is a Naya-side boundary only; Abzan requires its own packet before positive doctrine is authored.

Evidence anchors: `NAYA-EVID-003`, `NAYA-EVID-004`, `NAYA-EVID-006`, `NAYA-EVID-011`, `NAYA-EVID-018`, and `NAYA-MF-007`.

Naya can share feral motion, creature scale, and instinct with URG textures. It should not become clever adaptation, wilderness technique, or raw ferocity without White's larger-picture support. This is a Naya-side boundary only; Temur requires its own packet before positive doctrine is authored.
