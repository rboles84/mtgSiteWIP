## Identity Overview

Dune is Vox Mana's public identity label for the BRGW, non-Blue four-color lane. `BRGW` is the canonical Vox Mana ordering for this lane. `Aggression` is a support-source Commander 2016 theme reference used for paired framing only; it is not a Vox Mana public alias, route key, dossier key, placement key, expression key, or exclusive name for the color combination. `WBRG` may appear only when echoing support-source ordering and must not replace `BRGW` in Vox Mana prose.

Dune is defined by the color it excludes. White, Black, Red, and Green remain present, but Blue is absent. The safe Dune floor therefore begins with pressure rather than detached contemplation: a line that rallies, a force that spends what it must, a strike that does not wait for perfect modeling, and a survival logic that multiplies presence on the ground.

## Core Drive

Dune pushes toward embodied advantage. It wants more bodies in motion, more ground taken before hesitation can freeze the moment, and more pressure applied while the opening is still open. White gives that pressure a line, Black gives it permission to spend and conquer, Red gives it immediacy, and Green gives it survival-minded multiplication. Blue is missing, so the identity does not naturally stop to cool itself through distance, theory, or exhaustive scenario mapping.

This is why Dune reads as territorial rather than contemplative. It trusts force that can organize itself quickly, hold the field through numbers and nerve, and make presence itself a claim.

Evidence floor: `DUNE-EVID-003`, `DUNE-EVID-007`, and `DUNE-EVID-009`.

## Philosophical Foundations

### White As Rally And Line

White gives Dune cohesion under pressure. It does not soften the identity into peace-first restraint; it turns scattered force into a line that can rally, hold, and advance together. In Dune, White reads as formation, common front, and force-backed solidarity rather than detached civic procedure.

### Black As Cost-Bearing Conquest

Black gives Dune permission to spend what must be spent. It accepts that territory, survival, and advantage often carry a price, and it refuses to let moral discomfort alone dissolve forward motion. In this lane, Black is not generic cruelty. It is the willingness to bear cost so pressure does not collapse at the first resistance.

### Red As Immediate Strike

Red gives Dune ignition. It sees the opening, acts before the moment cools, and treats hesitation as a risk in itself. Without Red, Dune would become only a hard formation; with Red, it becomes a force that surges, commits, and keeps the pressure hot.

### Green As Survival And Multiplication

Green gives Dune embodied survival. It trusts force that can root, spread, endure, and become more present rather than more abstract. In Dune, Green supports multiplication, staying power, and the sense that living pressure becomes harder to dislodge once it has taken ground.

### The Blue Absence

Blue is the central wound in the lane. Dune does not naturally pause for detached contemplation, total information, or exhaustive modeling before it acts. That absence does not mean the lane lacks awareness or tactics. It means cool distance does not sit in the driver's seat. The identity would rather move, rally, spend, and survive than remain suspended inside interpretation.

Evidence floor: `DUNE-EVID-003` and `DUNE-EVID-007`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These are present-color pair boundaries only. They explain why a two-color overlap can feel near Dune without becoming Dune by itself. Shard, wedge, and four-color comparisons belong in the separator section below.

### Dune And Rakdos (BR)

Rakdos overlap can supply appetite, attack, risk, and immediate pressure. It is insufficient when the center is spectacle, hedonism, or chaos for its own sake. Dune also needs White line discipline and Green survival-minded multiplication so attack becomes a territorial front rather than performance alone.

### Dune And Golgari (BG)

Golgari overlap can supply survival pressure, persistence, and grounded resource conversion. It is insufficient when the center is rot, cycle, or reclamation. Dune also needs White rally and Red ignition so persistence becomes advancing pressure rather than slow ecological inevitability.

### Dune And Orzhov (BW)

Orzhov overlap can supply cost, hierarchy, duty, and pressure through obligation. It is insufficient when the center is ledger, debt, or institutional control. Dune also needs Red immediacy and Green embodied spread so cost-bearing becomes movement across the field, not only a static order.

### Dune And Gruul (RG)

Gruul overlap can supply force, momentum, and living impact. It is insufficient when the center is rebellion, feral rejection, or unstructured smash. Dune also needs White organization and Black cost-bearing conquest so force can become a coordinated territorial claim.

### Dune And Boros (RW)

Boros overlap can supply discipline, strike timing, and an advancing line. It is insufficient when the center is duty, righteous order, or martial ideal alone. Dune also needs Black spending and Green persistence so the line can conquer and keep pressure alive after the first charge.

### Dune And Selesnya (GW)

Selesnya overlap can supply solidarity, numbers, and shared presence. It is insufficient when the center is belonging, harmony, or protected communal life. Dune also needs Black conquest pressure and Red strike timing so shared force becomes assertive and territorial rather than harmonious.

## Missing-Color And Near-Match Separators

Each separator explains why an adjacent identity is insufficient or only partially overlapping. Do not use these comparisons to claim that the adjacent identity is secretly Dune, proof of Dune, or a hidden official Dune faction.

### Jund-Facing Boundary

Jund can look close because it already carries Black, Red, and Green appetite, force, and survival pressure. It is still missing White. Without White line, common-front discipline, and organized solidarity, Jund remains more self-truth and instinct pressure than Dune's territorial formation.

### Naya-Facing Boundary

Naya can look close because it already carries Red, Green, and White bodies, scale, and creature-forward presence. It is still missing Black. Without Black cost-bearing conquest and willingness to spend for advantage, Naya remains more abundance and belonging than Dune's harder territorial pressure.

### Mardu-Facing Boundary

Mardu can look close because it already carries White, Black, and Red line pressure, speed, and conquest. It is still missing Green. Without Green survival-minded multiplication and embodied staying power, Mardu remains more raid-speed and warband immediacy than Dune's rooted, multiplying front.

### Abzan-Facing Boundary

Abzan can overlap through White, Black, and Green endurance, solidarity, and continuity. It is still missing Red. Without Red ignition and willingness to strike before the moment cools, Abzan remains too patient, ancestral, and continuity-driven to stand in for Dune.

### Glint-Facing Boundary

Glint is the closest four-color false positive because it also reads through a missing-color wound and support-only Commander texture. The split is decisive: Dune excludes Blue and organizes force into a territorial line, while Glint excludes White and favors appetite, adaptation, and unlicensed motion without civic restraint.

### Saskia-Only Boundary

Saskia is a support-only Commander anchor, not a universal definition of the lane. A Saskia shell can illuminate multi-front pressure and four-color-without-Blue combat texture without making every Saskia list or attack trigger shell identical with Dune.

### Generic Go-Wide And Combat Shells

Tokens, swarm pressure, tempo, and combat-focused plans can help explain how the lane feels, but they are insufficient on their own. A deck or character can go wide, attack quickly, or create battlefield pressure without expressing the full Dune frame of organized line, cost-bearing conquest, Red ignition, Green survival, and the Blue absence.

### Generic BRGW Goodstuff Boundary

Color identity alone is insufficient. `BRGW`, plus all same-color permutations, remains metadata/query-only. A deck being BRGW does not make it Dune unless the architecture signals and separator boundaries also align.

## Non-Runtime Identity Signals

### Strong Dune Signals

- The center is an organized front rather than an individual outburst.
- Bodies in motion matter more than detached contemplation.
- Territorial pressure, coordinated attack, and survival-minded spread appear together.
- White line, Black cost-bearing, Red strike timing, and Green persistence all matter at once.
- Commander support texture around multi-front pressure or go-wide attack stays clearly marked support-only.

### Inhibitors

- The center is spectacle, appetite, or rebellion without White organization.
- The center is abundance, belonging, or living harmony without Black conquest pressure.
- The center is patient endurance, ancestral continuity, or defense-first solidity without Red ignition.
- The center is appetite/adaptation without White, which is Glint-facing rather than Dune-facing.
- The supposed Dune case depends only on combat, tokens, swarm, or BRGW color identity alone.

### Commander And Archetype Anchors

- Saskia can support Dune only as support-only multi-front combat texture.
- `Open Hostility` can support Dune only as support-only Commander 2016 combat-pressure texture.
- Partner/decklist material can support Dune only as support-only go-wide or board-pressure texture.
- Generic token/combat archetypes require the full missing-Blue territorial frame before they should feel Dune-shaped.

### Placement Guidance

Use these signals as authoring guidance only. Do not wire them into placement, scoring, JSON, runtime, search aliases, generated artifacts, or model directives during VM-254.

It should weaken when the case is mostly appetite and instinct without White line, mostly abundance without Black conquest, mostly endurance without Red ignition, or mostly adaptation without White structure.
