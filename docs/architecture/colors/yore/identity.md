## Identity Overview

Yore is Vox Mana's WUBR, non-Green four-color expression label. Artifice is the Commander 2016 mechanical/theme alias that supports the same four-color lane. Neither term should be written as the official, exclusive, or universally accepted MTG name for WUBR. This is the first rule of the identity: the label is useful, resonant, and repo-approved for Vox Mana work, but it is not a canon faction badge.

Yore is defined by the missing color. White, Blue, Black, and Red remain present, but Green is absent. The resulting pressure is a philosophy of engineered agency: structure without surrender, knowledge without patience, ambition without mortality as a final answer, and passion converted into forge heat.

Evidence floor: `YORE-EVID-002`, `YORE-EVID-003`, `YORE-EVID-004`, and `YORE-EVID-010`.

## Core Drive

Yore seeks progress through constructed intervention.

It does not ask whether the natural order is wise. It asks where the blueprint failed, which tools can correct it, and what the table must pay to keep the engine moving.

White gives Yore architecture.

Blue gives it optimization.

Black gives it the refusal to be erased.

Red gives it heat, velocity, and the dangerous joy of creation.

Green is absent, so Yore does not trust the slow mercy of cycles. Death, decay, instinct, inheritance, and untouched wilderness are treated as constraints to study, break, reuse, or rebuild.

Evidence floor: `YORE-EVID-003`, `YORE-EVID-004`, `YORE-EVID-006`, and `YORE-EVID-009`.

## Vox Mana Read

Vox Mana synthesis:

> You see the world as an unfinished machine. The old laws are not sacred to you; they are inherited defaults. You build order, sharpen knowledge, spend what must be spent, and give the whole system enough fire to move.

This read is synthesis, not direct canon. It is traceable to the VM-240 missing-Green frame, artifice theme, mechanical support texture, and product-copy seed. Keep it source-bound whenever it becomes user-facing copy.

## Philosophical Foundations

### White Blueprint

White supplies the scaffolding: law, repeatable procedure, protected infrastructure, and the belief that a dangerous world must be shaped into something safer. In Yore, that structure does not remain civic alone. It becomes laboratory protocol, industrial standard, graveyard taxonomy, and the social machinery that lets invention scale.

This is White as architecture, not Selesnya-style harmony and not Azorius-only bureaucracy.

### Blue Optimization

Blue supplies the insistence that the given world is only a draft. Its relationship to Yore is direct: study the system, isolate the failure, improve the design, and do not mistake nature for final truth. Blue makes Yore's artifice intelligent instead of merely ornate.

This is Blue as refinement and possibility, not pure Izzet experimentation and not Esper without Red.

### Black Refusal

Black supplies agency under pressure. Yore inherits Black's willingness to pay costs, break taboos, and convert forbidden material into advantage. The grave can become archive, debt can become fuel, and survival can become an engineered right rather than a natural accident.

This is Black as refusal to be powerless, not Phyrexian inevitability and not generic villainy.

### Red Heat

Red supplies ignition. Without Red, Yore risks becoming a cold museum of perfect systems. Red turns the machinery on: forge-fire, risk, creative violence, and immediate intervention. It makes the identity kinetic rather than merely preserved.

This is Red as motion and creative force, not Mardu speed and not Rakdos spectacle.

### The Green Absence

Green is the missing color and the main philosophical pressure. Green accepts natural limits, inherited roles, slow growth, death, decay, and the web of life. Yore does not. Yore treats those things as design constraints or raw material.

That absence is not proof that every WUBR deck, artifact deck, or graveyard deck is Yore. It only defines the central interpretive wound: no natural surrender.

Evidence floor: `YORE-EVID-003`, `YORE-EVID-004`, `YORE-MF-004`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These boundaries are docs-only placement language. They describe why a two-color overlap is insufficient by itself, not why the overlap proves Yore.

### Yore And Azorius (WU)

Azorius overlap can supply law, procedure, infrastructure, and controlled knowledge, but WU alone is too civic and procedural to describe Yore. Yore also needs Black's refusal to accept costless limits, Red's ignition, and the missing-Green pressure that treats nature as editable rather than sovereign.

### Yore And Dimir (UB)

Dimir overlap can supply secrecy, memory work, ambition, and forbidden knowledge. It is insufficient when the center is only covert advantage. Yore needs White's architecture, Red's forge heat, and an artifice frame that turns information into constructed intervention.

### Yore And Rakdos (BR)

Rakdos overlap can supply appetite, cost payment, sacrifice, and immediacy. It is insufficient when the center is spectacle, impulse, or pain as performance. Yore needs White/Blue structure and optimization so sacrifice becomes machinery rather than theater.

### Yore And Izzet (UR)

Izzet overlap can supply invention, experiment, velocity, and dangerous creativity. It is insufficient when invention stays episodic or curiosity-driven. Yore needs White's durable systems, Black's refusal to let death or taboo close the ledger, and the missing-Green rejection of natural surrender.

### Yore And Orzhov (WB)

Orzhov overlap can supply contract, debt, hierarchy, and afterlife pressure. It is insufficient when obligation remains religious, financial, or static. Yore needs Blue optimization and Red ignition so the ledger becomes an engine, not only an institution.

### Yore And Boros (WR)

Boros overlap can supply public order, action, discipline, and righteous heat. It is insufficient when the center is duty in motion. Yore needs Blue's technical revision, Black's cost-bearing agency, and an artifice frame that turns action into engineered continuity.

## Missing-Color And Near-Match Separators

Each separator explains why an adjacent concept is insufficient or only partially overlapping. Do not use these comparisons to claim that the adjacent concept is part of Yore, proof of Yore, or a hidden official Yore faction.

### Esper-Facing Boundary

Esper can look close because it carries White/Blue/Black artifact perfection, hierarchy, and body-editing themes. It is still missing Red. Without Red heat, improvisation, and ignition, Esper-facing artifact material should not be treated as Yore unless the source also supports the WUBR/non-Green frame.

### Grixis-Facing Boundary

Grixis can overlap through Blue/Black/Red ambition, recursion, and refusal. It is missing White's civic architecture and durable system-building. Grixis recursion can be hunger, necromancy, or ruin; Yore recursion should read as archive, engine restart, or constructed continuity.

### Jeskai-Facing Boundary

Jeskai can overlap through discipline, study, action, and refined technique. It is missing Black. Without Black's willingness to pay forbidden costs and refuse erasure, Jeskai-facing invention remains technique or enlightenment rather than Yore's engineered defiance.

### Mardu-Facing Boundary

Mardu can overlap through White/Black/Red sacrifice, urgency, memory, and battlefield pressure. It is missing Blue. Without Blue optimization and technical revision, Mardu-facing sacrifice remains raid, honor, blood-price, or tactical expenditure rather than a self-improving machine.

### Sultai-Facing Boundary

Sultai can overlap through resource conversion, graveyard use, and long ambition. It includes Green, which changes the metaphysical center. Sultai recursion can live in organic exploitation, ecology, and rot; Yore recursion should avoid Green-coded renewal unless the source makes the artificial, non-Green frame explicit.

### Thran-Facing Boundary

Thran material can be useful as ancient-technology or lost-civilization texture only when separately supported. VM-242 should not imply that Yore is Thran, descended from Thran, or authorized by Thran lore.

### Phyrexia-Facing Boundary

Phyrexia overlaps through artifice, body alteration, and refusal of natural limits, but it has its own canon identity. Do not make Yore a Phyrexia bucket. Compleation, oil, orthodoxy, or Phyrexian factions require their own sources and should not be imported into Yore as proof.

### Breya-Only Boundary

Breya and Invent Superiority are Commander support anchors, not proof of Yore lore. A Breya deck can support artifact, sacrifice, combo, and four-color-without-Green texture without making Breya an official Yore representative or making every Breya shell a Yore identity statement.

### Generic Artifacts And Recursion

Artifact decks, graveyard decks, recursion loops, and value engines are insufficient by themselves. Yore needs the missing-Green worldview: constructed agency against natural surrender, with WUBR pressure converting structure, knowledge, cost, and heat into a machine.

### Cult Of Yore Boundary

Do not treat "Cult of Yore," "Yore-Tiller," or any Yore-adjacent phrase as evidence for a WUBR faction unless directly supported by the approved claim-bearing source. Similar words are not enough; the claim must bind to the approved evidence floor.

### Generic WUBR Goodstuff

Color identity alone is insufficient. WUBR, plus all 23 same-color permutations, remains metadata/query-only until VM-245 or later approval. A deck being WUBR does not make it Yore unless the architecture signals also align.

## Non-Runtime Identity Signals

Strong Yore signals in architecture prose:

- The user wants to build, optimize, refine, or mechanize instead of accepting the given state.
- The language favors machines, blueprints, laboratories, foundries, archives, contracts, engines, recursion, and controlled risk.
- The emotional center is agency: death is not accepted, limits are not sacred, and resources can be redesigned.
- Commander support leans toward artifacts, sacrifice loops, value engines, and careful combo assembly.

Inhibitors:

- The center is organic belonging, land, ancestral place, patient growth, pack instinct, or ecological harmony.
- The artifact language is only Esper perfection without Red heat, or only generic artifact deck preference without a missing-Green worldview.
- The graveyard language is purely Golgari renewal, Sultai exploitation, or Mardu memory without the artifice frame.
- The claim depends on Cult of Yore, Yore-Tiller lore, Breya lore, cEDH data, or exact card facts not yet audited into a raw packet.

### Placement Guidance

Use these signals as authoring guidance only. Do not wire them into placement, scoring, JSON, runtime, search aliases, or generated artifacts during VM-242.

For future human review, Yore should outrank adjacent identities only when the text centers engineered agency against natural surrender, not merely artifacts, graveyards, sacrifice, optimization, or WUBR color identity. If the text centers law without heat, secrecy without civic system, spectacle without optimization, invention without cost, debt without revision, or action without archive, keep it in the nearer two-color lane until stronger evidence appears.
