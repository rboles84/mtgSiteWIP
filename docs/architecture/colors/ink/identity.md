# Ink / Altruism - Identity

Status: VM-259 / VM-260 architecture
Live state: `INK` remains non-live and architecture-only
Source floor: VM-258 Ink packet

Ink is Vox Mana's reserved expression label for the non-Black four-color identity of Red, Green, White, and Blue. `Altruism` is Commander 2016 support framing for the same color set; it is not treated here as a co-equal canonical name. `RGWU`, `WURG`, and every same-color permutation remain metadata/query-only and are not public identity names. (`INK-EVID-002`, `INK-EVID-010`)

## Core Identity

Ink is the four-color commons: the belief that life improves when resources, knowledge, protection, and joy circulate outward rather than being locked behind private advantage. It is not self-erasure. It is the discipline of making abundance durable enough that generosity does not become prey.

The missing Black center matters. Ink does not begin from personal sovereignty, private ambition, or the right to possess more simply because one can. It begins from shared prosperity, community benefit, protected generosity, open knowledge, public abundance, and resistance to private hoarding. (`INK-EVID-003`, `INK-EVID-007`, `INK-MF-010`)

Ink's highest question is not "How do I win alone?" but "What kind of world lets everyone keep becoming?" It treats the self as real, but nested inside table, archive, ecology, promise, and memory.

## Present Colors

Red gives Ink the immediacy of care. Generosity is not only law or theory; it is the sudden impulse to feed, protect, celebrate, and answer suffering with presence.

Green gives Ink reciprocity. A healthy world is not a pile of isolated owners, but a living web where abundance returns through relationship, season, inheritance, and stewardship.

White gives Ink civic promise. Shared prosperity needs agreements, repair, and protection from those who would turn the commons into a private throne.

Blue gives Ink open knowledge. Learning, records, craft, and memory become public goods when they are held for communal growth rather than gatekept as leverage.

Without Black, Ink distrusts extraction as identity. It can still recognize need, boundaries, and self-preservation, but it does not make private gain the measure of a life.

## Vox Mana Read

Ink is the identity of circulated flourishing. It favors the feast over the vault, the library over the patent-lock, the public square over the private court, and the protected commons over naive openness. Its generosity has teeth because an unguarded gift can be captured.

That makes Ink warmer than pure civic order and less hierarchical than simple White control. It is more intentional than Red impulse alone, more social than Blue abstraction alone, and more protective than Green acceptance alone. Its synthesis is an architecture of shared plenty.

`Ink` can carry public-memory and archive resonance as Vox Mana interpretation, but that reading remains synthesis-bound unless stronger source capture later supports it directly. (`INK-EVID-009`, `INK-MF-007`)

## Altruism Boundary

Use `Altruism` as Commander 2016 support framing only. It can explain why Kynaios and Tiro / `Stalwart Unity` feel adjacent to Ink's table texture: shared resources, political reciprocity, group benefit, and public prosperity. It does not name Ink's philosophy, prove Ink's lore identity, or outrank `INK` as the Vox Mana expression label. (`INK-EVID-005`, `INK-EVID-006`, `INK-EVID-010`, `INK-MF-002`, `INK-MF-003`)

In body prose, default to Ink or `INK`. Use paired Ink / Altruism display language only when the document is deliberately calling out the difference between Vox Mana label and Commander 2016 support theme.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These are present-color pair boundaries only. They explain why a two-color overlap can feel near Ink without becoming Ink by itself. Shard, wedge, and four-color comparisons belong in the separator section below. (`INK-EVID-003`, `INK-EVID-007`, `INK-MF-011`)

### Ink And Azorius (WU)

Azorius overlap can supply civic promise, public order, and knowledge held in common. It is insufficient when the center is law, procedure, or authorized knowledge alone. Ink also needs Red immediacy and Green reciprocity so the commons stays warm, living, and responsive rather than only administered.

### Ink And Izzet (UR)

Izzet overlap can supply curiosity, invention, and sudden inspiration. It is insufficient when the center is experiment, spectacle, or cleverness for its own sake. Ink also needs White protection and Green relationship so knowledge circulates as public care rather than volatile novelty.

### Ink And Simic (UG)

Simic overlap can supply growth through learning, adaptive stewardship, and living systems. It is insufficient when the center is optimization, biomantic improvement, or natural study alone. Ink also needs White civic promise and Red felt generosity so adaptation serves communal abundance rather than pure refinement.

### Ink And Selesnya (GW)

Selesnya overlap can supply community, belonging, and protected life. It is insufficient when the center is harmony, congregation, or unity alone. Ink also needs Blue open knowledge and Red immediacy so the community becomes a learning commons rather than only a shared garden.

### Ink And Boros (RW)

Boros overlap can supply public defense, urgent aid, and a willingness to act for others. It is insufficient when the center is martial duty, righteous strike, or command discipline alone. Ink also needs Green reciprocity and Blue shared memory so protection becomes durable public abundance rather than only intervention.

### Ink And Gruul (RG)

Gruul overlap can supply vitality, instinctive generosity, and embodied resistance to enclosure. It is insufficient when the center is rupture, feral rejection, or anti-civic force. Ink also needs White promise and Blue legibility so living abundance can be protected, taught, and shared.

## Missing-Color And Near-Match Separators

Each separator explains why an adjacent identity is insufficient or only partially overlapping. Dune-facing and Witch-facing notes are architecture comparisons only; they do not change the status of either lane. (`INK-EVID-003`, `INK-EVID-007`, `INK-MF-011`)

### Bant-Facing Boundary

Bant can look close because it already carries Green, White, and Blue community, order, and learning. It is still missing Red. Without Red immediacy, emotional presence, and urgent care, Bant remains too formal and serene to stand in for Ink's warmer commons.

### Jeskai-Facing Boundary

Jeskai can look close because it already carries Blue, Red, and White discipline, expression, and public practice. It is still missing Green. Without Green reciprocity, rootedness, and living return, Jeskai remains too technique-centered to become Ink's ecological abundance.

### Naya-Facing Boundary

Naya can look close because it already carries Red, Green, and White embodied community and shared life. It is still missing Blue. Without Blue open knowledge, archive, and deliberate learning, Naya remains more immediate belonging than Ink's public-memory commons.

### Temur-Facing Boundary

Temur can look close because it already carries Green, Blue, and Red vitality, study, and improvisational life. It is still missing White. Without White civic promise and protective structure, Temur remains too wild and local to become Ink's defended commons.

### Dune-Facing Boundary

Dune can look close because both four-color lanes use a missing-color wound and can discuss public action, communal pressure, and support-only Commander texture. The split is decisive: Ink excludes Black and resists private hoarding, while Dune excludes Blue and centers territorial pressure without detached contemplation. Dune comparison here is architecture-only.

### Witch-Facing Boundary

Witch can look close because both four-color lanes can speak about community, knowledge, and guarded social power. The split is decisive: Ink excludes Black and resists enclosure, while Witch excludes Red and should be read through a different missing-color wound. Witch comparison here is architecture-only.

### Kynaios-Only Boundary

Kynaios and Tiro / `Stalwart Unity` can illustrate shared resources and table reciprocity, but a Kynaios shell alone is not Ink. The support texture becomes Ink-shaped only when it remains subordinate to the full non-Black frame of protected generosity, open knowledge, community benefit, and resistance to private hoarding. (`INK-EVID-005`, `INK-EVID-006`)

### Ink-Treader-Only Boundary

Ink-Treader Nephilim can anchor the RGWU lane historically, but Ink-Treader alone is not an Ink society, institution, or philosophy. The anchor helps locate the color set and label lineage without authorizing an invented faction. (`INK-EVID-004`, `INK-MF-001`)

### Generic Group-Hug Boundary

Group-hug, shared draws, extra resources, and table-friendly politics can resemble Ink's generosity. They are insufficient when the center is novelty, appeasement, or everyone-getting-more without a theory of protected abundance. Ink needs a commons that resists capture, not only gifts handed around the table.

### Generic Public-Archive Boundary

Libraries, archives, scripts, records, and memory imagery can resemble Ink's open-knowledge side. They are insufficient when the center is secrecy, prestige, or knowledge as leverage. Ink's archive must be public-minded, protective, and connected to living reciprocity. (`INK-EVID-009`, `INK-MF-007`)

### Generic RGWU Goodstuff Boundary

Color identity alone is insufficient. `RGWU`, `WURG`, and every same-color permutation remain metadata/query-only. A deck, character, or concept being RGWU does not make it Ink unless the architecture signals and separator boundaries also align. (`INK-EVID-002`, `INK-EVID-010`)

## Editorial Identity Signals

### Strong Ink Signals

- The center is a protected commons rather than private achievement.
- Shared prosperity, public benefit, open knowledge, and living reciprocity appear together.
- Generosity has boundaries that prevent capture by the strongest claimant.
- The text resists Black-style private hoarding without erasing personal need.
- Commander support texture around shared resources remains clearly support-only.

### Inhibitors

- The center is only law, archive, or formal procedure without Red care and Green reciprocity.
- The center is only group-hug friendliness without protection from capture.
- The center is only public memory without living communal benefit.
- The center is only RGWU color identity, Kynaios-only texture, or Ink-Treader-only anchoring.
- The case depends on discovery-draft claims that have not been re-sourced into approved rows.

### Commander And Archetype Anchors

- Kynaios and Tiro can support Ink only as shared-resource and political-reciprocity texture.
- `Stalwart Unity` can support Ink only as Commander 2016 table-politics texture.
- Generic group-hug shells require the full non-Black commons frame before they should feel Ink-shaped.
- Public-archive imagery requires open knowledge plus protected generosity before it should feel Ink-shaped.

### Placement Guidance

Use these signals as descriptive authoring guidance only. Treat them as prose cues, not as operational criteria or field definitions during VM-260.

For human editorial sorting, Ink should strengthen when the text centers shared prosperity, community benefit, open knowledge, protected generosity, and resistance to private hoarding. It should weaken when the case is mostly color identity, mostly group-hug friendliness, mostly archive imagery, mostly Kynaios texture, or mostly Ink-Treader anchoring.

### Editorial Search-Planning Shapes

- Positive editorial shape: language of commons, public abundance, shared learning, civic memory, and protected generosity.
- Positive editorial shape: descriptions where resources circulate outward while the gift remains guarded from capture.
- Boundary editorial shape: Bant, Jeskai, Naya, Temur, Dune, Witch, Kynaios-only shells, and generic group-hug lists should prompt human attention rather than automatic Ink assignment.
- Stop editorial shape: any attempt to turn `RGWU`, `WURG`, a permutation, or `Altruism` into a public alias belongs outside VM-260.

## Identity Statement
