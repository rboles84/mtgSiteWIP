# Witherbloom College - Essence Exchange

Source boundary: this file drafts Witherbloom College identity for Vox Mana architecture from approved repo evidence. Witherbloom is treated as an expression-level Strixhaven school pilot, not as mono Black plus mono Green and not as generic Golgari with school language. Metaphysical, placement, system-mapping, and operator language is Vox Mana internal architecture where noted; it is not MTG canon.

## Identity Overview

Witherbloom College is Strixhaven's black-green College of Essence Studies. The direct raw claims identify Witherbloom as a school whose mages draw power from opposing forces of life and death, brew spells from natural components and living essence, and use that craft to heal, harm, raise, or entreat the dead (`data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.claims.json`; `data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.profile.json`).

Witherbloom should read as practical, embodied, biological magic. Its center is not the abstract color pair and not Ravnican rot-farm identity. It is schoolcraft in the bog: life force, death magic, medicine, toxins, pests, fieldwork, essence, sacrifice, lifegain, drain, and resource conversion.

The official-source floor is strong enough to draft. The raw evidence names Witherbloom as Essence Studies, describes the black-aligned side as exploiting life energy and the green-aligned side as encouraging life energy to blossom and grow, and lists areas of study including alchemy, medicine, biomancy, pathology, toxicology, mortuary science, and necromancy (`data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.claims.json`).

## Core Drive

Witherbloom wants to understand what vitality costs and how living systems convert that cost into power, medicine, pressure, and survival.

The direct evidence supports this drive through life/death essence, healing and harm, natural components, living essence, raising or entreating the dead, and the school tension between nurturing ecosystems and exploiting life essence for practical or personal power (`data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.profile.json`; `data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.claims.json`).

This is exchange, not sentiment. Witherbloom does not treat life as pure innocence or death as pure failure. It studies the living boundary where cure and toxin, meal and specimen, sacrifice and growth, pest and resource, body and lesson all become difficult to separate.

## Vox Mana Read (Core Axiom)

This is a compression-only Vox Mana read. It adds no new nouns, mechanics, or doctrine; it compresses supported Witherbloom evidence into a usable operator axiom. It is Vox Mana internal synthesis, not MTG canon.

Witherbloom says: life and death are not opposites to escape; they are materials in one embodied exchange, and the mage who understands the exchange can heal, harm, feed, and endure.

## Philosophical Foundations

Witherbloom's philosophical floor is direct: life and death are studyable forces, living essence can be worked through practical magic, and the same craft can restore, wound, raise, or speak across death (`data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.claims.json`).

The Mark Rosewater black-green philosophy article is not Witherbloom-specific authority, and it explicitly discusses the color-pie intersection rather than Strixhaven's school. Used with that boundary, it supports the broader backdrop: black-green centers the conflict of life and death, manipulates graveyard resources, uses growth and death together, and can strengthen through adversity (`docs/research/canon/mark_rosewater_official_two_color/golgari_Life and Death _ MAGIC_ THE GATHERING.md`).

For Vox Mana, those sources support four bounded Witherbloom foundations:

- Life is a practical force, not merely a symbol.
- Death is a conversion event, not merely an ending.
- Care and harm can share the same craft.
- Knowledge is embodied: wounds, swamps, specimens, medicine, toxins, pests, and appetite all matter.

### Essence Studies

Witherbloom is a school before it is a deck archetype. Its official identity is Essence Studies, and its coursework anchors the expression in life sciences, fieldwork, medicine, toxins, mortuary study, and necromancy rather than generic black-green value.

### Care And Exploitation

The school tension is not "good nature versus bad death." The raw profile states that Witherbloom splits between nurturing ecosystems and exploiting life essence for personal or practical power. That means its identity must keep both Lisette-style growth and Valentin-style extraction legible without collapsing either into a single moral answer.

### Embodied Knowledge

Witherbloom rejects clean abstraction. Its placement profile strengthens when answers center fieldwork, remedies, ecology, the body, practical magic, life/death exchange, and comfort with messy material processes (`data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.placement.json`).

## Mechanical Identity

Witherbloom mechanical identity is strongly supported by lifegain, drain, pests, sacrifice, healing, harm, death-to-resource patterns, and resource conversion. Commander guidance names life exchange, pests, sacrifice, and drain as owned themes, and the raw profile describes Witherbloom identity around lifegain, pests, sacrifice, healing, harm, and biological exchange (`docs/reference/commander-faction-guidance.md`; `data/raw-factions/witherbloom/witherbloom.profile.json`).

### Lifegain And Drain

Lifegain and drain are Witherbloom when they express life movement as pressure, medicine, resource flow, or survival craft. The enhanced lore summary names lifegain/lifedrain as core Witherbloom mechanics, and raw Commander guidance supports drain for power while keeping the identity distinct from Orzhov debt and Rakdos spectacle (`docs/research/MTG_Lore_Research_Enhanced.md`; `docs/reference/commander-faction-guidance.md`).

### Pests And Sacrifice

Pests are one of the clearest Witherbloom signals because they are small living bodies that matter by living, dying, feeding effects, and moving life. Sacrifice should read as biological exchange: a small death becomes cards, life, counters, pressure, or survival, not merely generic aristocrats math.

### Healing, Harm, Medicine, And Toxins

The same official claim supports healing and harm from natural components and living essence. The 2026 study list strengthens that into medicine, pathology, toxicology, mortuary science, and necromancy. Witherbloom should therefore keep remedy and poison in the same hand.

### Resource Conversion

Resource conversion is the safest project term for Witherbloom gameplay: life becomes cards, bodies become life, death becomes growth, pests become pressure, and life total movement becomes counters or mana. This phrase is Vox Mana architecture, not a named MTG mechanic.

## Gameplay Philosophy

Witherbloom gameplay should feel like practical life/death craft. The deck or placement answer should show what is being fed, what is being spent, and what grows from that cost.

Native or high-fit gameplay signals include:

- Pests that attack, block, die, feed sacrifice outlets, and trigger lifegain.
- Lifegain that turns into drain, counters, cards, mana, or removal.
- Sacrifice engines where small bodies become survival or pressure.
- Graveyard and recursion patterns when they read as compost, future material, or death-to-life craft.
- Food, cauldrons, brewing, specimens, medicine, toxins, and field biology when supported by card or source context.

The operator shape should read as life/death craft -> biological exchange -> resource conversion. It should not read as generic Golgari recursion, generic nature, generic lifegain, or generic sacrifice.
