## Metaphysical Thesis

Project framing boundary: this thesis is provisional. It is strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

Boros metaphysics treats justice as force under ethical constraint.

In this project model, Boros does not begin with violence. It begins with exposure: someone or something is under threat, delay has a cost, and the group must decide whether duty will remain abstract or become motion. Boros is the metaphysical pattern in which moral conviction condenses into organized force.

Because this file extends from evidence into project interpretation, its claims should remain provisional. The canon-backed floor is Boros as martial justice, peacekeeping, honor, disciplined combat, team action, and righteous urgency. The metaphysical layer below translates those patterns into Vox Mana system language.

## Philosophical Foundations

Strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

The foundations below translate direct Boros evidence into project architecture. They are not official Magic doctrine; they are Vox Mana's repeatable way to name the forces already visible in Boros identity, mechanics, and placement evidence.

### Protection

The first Boros metaphysical premise is protection. Force is legitimate only when it is tied to defense, public safety, sworn duty, or the prevention of harm. This premise is supported by the faction profile, placement guidance, commander guidance, and Boros research notes.

### Momentum

Boros action is kinetic. Once a threat is identified, Boros tries to convert recognition into movement: deploy, attack, protect, rally, and strike again if needed. Extra combat and aggressive pressure are safest in Boros when they read as compounding intervention rather than appetite for battle.

### Collective Agency

Boros rarely imagines courage as a solitary abstraction. Its metaphysics are formation-based: squads, battalions, patrols, armies, mentors, commanders, standards, and visible lines of battle. The group multiplies the individual.

### Hierarchy As Transmission

Boros hierarchy is not just command from above. In its best form, hierarchy transmits readiness. Mentor is the cleanest mechanical image: the stronger fighter makes the next fighter stronger.

### Resilience Under Fire

Boros resilience is emergency resilience. It is the ability to hold formation at the moment force arrives: protect the line, save the exposed, preserve the mission, and remain visible under pressure.

### Zeal As Risk

The same metaphysical engine that lets Boros act can overheat. Righteousness can become self-authorization. Momentum can become overcommitment. Formation can become coercion. The model must preserve this risk instead of turning Boros into uncomplicated virtue.

## Structural & Mechanical Architecture

### Battalion: Collective Threshold Node

Battalion models Boros as threshold-based collective agency. One attacker can be brave, but the Boros pattern fully appears when the line commits together. The reward is not merely for attacking; it is for achieving formation.

### Mentor: Hierarchical Propagation Edge

Mentor models leadership as distributed readiness. A Boros leader proves strength by making another combatant stronger. In system terms, command becomes an edge of improvement rather than a static rank.

### Radiance: Field Diffusion Operator

Radiance models Boros conviction as a field effect. A single action can spread across shared qualities, suggesting public moral energy, battlefield standards, and the contagion of visible courage.

### Extra Combat: Temporal Multiplication

Extra combat represents Boros momentum at the level of turn structure. It is the decision that one decisive action is not enough; protection demands a second surge before the danger can recover.

### Protection And Indestructibility: Emergency Resilience

Protection effects, indestructibility, and similar emergency hardiness patterns model the Boros ability to hold the line. These effects are strongest when tied to defense, solidarity, or preserving the mission rather than invulnerability as private dominance.

### Equipment: Embodied Duty

Equipment translates Boros identity into physical commitment. Armor, weapons, and standards make duty legible on the body, which is why equipment is a Commander-safe Boros lane when paired with discipline, justice, and tactical pressure.
