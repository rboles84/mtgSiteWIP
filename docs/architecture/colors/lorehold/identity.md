# Lorehold College - The Relic Historian

Source boundary: this file drafts Lorehold College identity for Vox Mana architecture from approved repo evidence. Lorehold is treated as an expression-level Strixhaven school pilot, not mono White plus mono Red and not generic Boros with archaeology language. Placement, relationship, system-mapping, and operator language is Vox Mana internal architecture where noted; it is not MTG canon.

## Identity Overview

Lorehold College is Strixhaven's red-white College of Archaeomancy. The direct raw profile identifies Lorehold as a history-and-fieldwork college built around ruins, relics, spirits, scrolls, artifacts, living history, and the order-versus-chaos tension of preserving the past while chasing discovery (`data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.profile.json::site_surface.short_summary`).

The direct claims give the core floor: Lorehold is one of Strixhaven's five colleges, is aligned with red and white mana, is the College of Archaeomancy, and studies history through archaeological artifacts, ancient tomes, spirits of long-dead historical figures, diligent research, daring adventure, and fieldwork methodologies (`data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_core_0001].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_core_0003].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_core_0004].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_core_0006].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_core_0007].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_structure_0006].claim`).

Lorehold should therefore read as history made active through evidence. The school does not simply admire the past. It enters ruins, consults records, preserves artifacts, restores context, listens to spirits, and lets recovered truth change present action.

## Core Drive

**Recover the past as living evidence, then steward it into responsible action.**

Lorehold wants contact with what happened: the surviving object, the record, the monument, the ghost, the battlefield, the fossil, the scroll, the restored artifact, or the field site where memory is still dangerous. Its drive is neither bookish nostalgia nor loot-first expedition. The raw placement summary says Lorehold fits people who treat history as a living toolkit: records to verify, relics to protect, ruins to enter carefully, spirits to listen to, and old assumptions to test against evidence (`data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.placement.json::placement_summary.short_description`).

The red side pushes toward direct encounter, dangerous fieldwork, discovery, and the emotional truth of history. The white side pushes toward preservation, records, precedent, care, and institutional memory (`data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_dichotomy_0001].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_dichotomy_0002].claim`; `data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.claims.json::claims[claim_id=claim_lorehold_dichotomy_0004].claim`). Lorehold is the school where those pressures must keep one another honest.

## Vox Mana Read (Core Axiom)

This is a compression-only Vox Mana read. It adds no new nouns, mechanics, or doctrine; it compresses supported Lorehold evidence into a usable operator axiom.

Lorehold says: the past is not inert. It is evidence, witness, relic, warning, precedent, and power, and it becomes most truthful when preserved carefully enough to act now.

## Gameplay Philosophy

Lorehold gameplay should feel like excavation becoming action. The table should see old material become useful without losing its context: a spirit witness summoned, an artifact restored, a graveyard card exiled or returned as a meaningful find, a monument or record translating the past into the present.

Strong Lorehold gameplay signals include:

- Spirit typal play when spirits are tied to history, witnesses, statues, ancestors, or the ancient dead.
- Artifact recursion and artifact copies when artifacts read as relics, evidence, monuments, restored tools, or recovered history.
- Graveyard-leaves triggers when the graveyard reads as a dig layer or archive, not generic death magic.
- Combat or action when it is battle memory, field expedition, recovered precedent, or history fighting back.
- Sagas, scrolls, records, monuments, maps, tomes, and field sites when they point to historical evidence.

The Commander Compass identity basis says Lorehold uses the graveyard not as a place of death but as a library of the past, emphasizing spirits, artifacts, and leaving-the-graveyard triggers (`data/raw-factions/lorehold/lorehold.profile.json::commander_compass.identity_basis.owned_themes[0]`). That is the practical deckbuilding separator from generic Boros combat, generic artifact value, or generic reanimation.
