## Identity Overview

Abzan Houses is Vox Mana's White-centered `WBG` Tarkir wedge expression for family endurance, ancestor memory, house continuity, perennation, and patient defense. The normalized Abzan packet supports a strong but bounded identity floor: Abzan is the White-Black-Green wedge with White as center; its design aspect is endurance/survival; and its Tarkir expression is grounded in family, duty, trust, kin trees, ancestor spirits, house membership, defensive patience, and survival under harsh pressure.

This file keeps Abzan non-live. It does not create a runtime `ABZAN` key, add `WBG` as an alias, create raw-faction JSON, create generated data, alter placement, or authorize route, Home, Maze, schema, Supabase, fixture, or builder changes.

## Core Drive

Abzan wants the family to survive long enough for duty, memory, and the next generation to matter.

In Vox Mana terms:

- White supplies the center: shared duty, family standing, defensive order, house membership, and continuity through obligation.
- Black supplies death-aware continuity: ancestor spirits, duty beyond death, inheritance pressure, political survival, and the cost of disownment or severance.
- Green supplies rooted endurance: Kin-Trees, growth after survival, earth magic, family trees, and life/death continuity through perennation.

The core drive is not generic toughness or generic graveyard value. It is an evidence-bound continuity engine: White makes survival communal, Black refuses to let death end obligation, and Green lets family memory root itself into living practice.
