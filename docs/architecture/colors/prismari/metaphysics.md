## Philosophical Foundations

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

Direct repo evidence supports Prismari as Strixhaven's College of Elemental Arts, a school where magic and art are inseparable and where the central tension runs between technique, theory, and disciplined study on one side and passion, expression, and emotional release on the other (`data/raw-factions/prismari/prismari.claims.json`; `data/raw-factions/prismari/prismari.profile.json`; `data/factions.json`).

### Technique And Feeling Need Each Other

Prismari is not generic impulse and not cold virtuosity. Its center is the tension between craft and release: technique without feeling becomes display without meaning, while feeling without technique becomes force without art.

### Elemental Force Is Artistic Material

Prismari does not use fire, water, wind, ice, stone, lightning, heat, or cold as generic spell payload. It treats them as media. The approved evidence repeatedly frames elemental forces as the means through which artistic expression becomes visible and public.

### Performance Makes Magic Social

Prismari magic is staged. The act matters because it is performed into a room, an audience, a battlefield, or a social space where expression can be witnessed and felt. This keeps Prismari distinct from purely private study or invisible optimization.

### Scale Carries Emotion

Big spells, crescendos, spell-copying bursts, Treasure-fueled turns, and elemental spectacle matter because Prismari wants the work to register. Scale is not merely more power. It is the part of the spell that lets emotion become undeniable.

### Drift Guardrail: Art Is Not Apparatus Or Provocation

Prismari must remain distinct from Izzet invention and Rakdos transgression. Approved placement evidence already suppresses apparatus-first experimentation, dangerous provocation, precise rhetoric, mathematical proof, and archive evidence when they replace expressive elemental art (`data/raw-factions/prismari/prismari.placement.json`).

## Structural & Mechanical Architecture

**Support status:** strongly supported as Vox Mana internal architecture derived from approved evidence; not MTG canon.

This section translates source-backed mechanical and placement signals into project architecture for placement, search, and Commander-facing interpretation.

### Big Spells Become Crescendos

High-mana instants and sorceries, spectacle turns, and spell-sequencing payoffs are Prismari when they feel like culmination. The spell is not just efficient output; it is the point where craft and release become legible.

### Elementals Become Art Bodies

Elementals are Prismari when they read as shaped media: fire, ice, water, wind, stone, or mixed elemental expression given form. They should not collapse into generic creature production or Izzet-style technical residue.

### Spell Copying Becomes Refrain

Cast-and-copy turns, magecraft-adjacent triggers, and repeated spell events fit Prismari when repetition intensifies the performance rather than only optimizing throughput.

### Treasure Becomes Residual Energy

Treasure is strongest in Prismari when it reads as energy left behind by the performance. It is useful because it enables the next crescendo, not because value extraction is the real center.

### Technique And Release Stay In Tension

Prismari architecture must preserve the blue-red split the approved evidence gives it: study, visualization, and technique remain active even at maximum spectacle, and passion remains active even in highly trained execution.
