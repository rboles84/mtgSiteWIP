## Core Drive

Jeskai wants to make action precise enough that body, mind, and community move as one disciplined choice. In Vox Mana terms, Blue supplies the center: perfection, knowledge, tools, training, preparation, and the cunning to find the exact line. Red supplies committed action, timing, passion, and the refusal to let insight remain inert. White supplies structure, restraint, shared discipline, and the form that turns individual mastery into a Way. It is an evidence-bound discipline engine: Blue studies and selects, Red acts when the opening appears, and White gives practice a moral and communal shape.
