# Jund Shard Metaphysics - Appetite As Compass

> Project metaphysics notice: this file is Vox Mana architecture built from VM-176 evidence. It is not MTG canon doctrine, not raw-faction JSON, and not runtime placement behavior. `JUND` is the expression name; `BRG` is color direction metadata only in this phase.

## Metaphysical Thesis

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Jund metaphysics treats appetite as compass: Red trusts the feeling, Black protects the self's hunger, and Green lets instinct become force.

The canon-backed floor is narrower than the image may feel. VM-176 supports Jund as the Red-centered `BRG` Alara shard, Red as self-truth and emotional guidance, Black as personal need and self-interest, Green as unrestrained instinct, and White/Blue as missing pressures. Everything beyond that must either cite the VM-176 packet or remain `Manual fill required`.

Evidence anchors: `docs/research/jund/jund-evidence-ledger.md` rows `JUND-EVID-001` through `JUND-EVID-013`, plus `JUND-EVID-021` for the labeled Vox Mana synthesis line.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Feeling Before Permission

Jund begins from the Red-centered premise that emotion is not noise to suppress. It is information, pressure, and direction. Vox Mana should write this as embodied self-trust, not as mere anger.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-003`, `JUND-EVID-004`, `JUND-EVID-005`, and `JUND-EVID-012`.

### Appetite As Selfhood

Black gives Jund the refusal to apologize for wanting. The supported design frame is self-interest, personal need, contentment, and resistance to White's constraint. This supports appetite language, but not cruelty-as-spectacle or any unsupported figure/tribe doctrine.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-007`, `JUND-EVID-009`, and `JUND-EVID-013`.

### Instinct As Motion

Green gives Jund a body for the impulse: unrestrained, less overthought, and feral. In metaphysical terms, Jund does not wait for theory to domesticate the feeling. It lets the living force move.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-008`, `JUND-EVID-009`, and `JUND-EVID-021`.

### Freedom With Teeth

Jund freedom carries consequence. The source frame allows a world of total freedom where action and survival have costs. Vox Mana may express this as freedom with teeth, appetite under pressure, or survival through action, but it must not add detailed geography, predator hierarchy, or social law.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-006`, `JUND-EVID-010`, and manual-fill rows `JUND-MF-001`, `JUND-MF-003`, and `JUND-MF-007`.

### Absence Of White And Blue Pressures

The metaphysical absence in Jund is bounded. VM-176 allows architecture to say White/Blue pressures are missing from the shard frame, especially imposed conformity/order and detached over-analysis at the design level. It does not allow a leap to "no society," "no intelligence," "no memory," or "no strategy."

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-010`; manual-fill boundaries `JUND-MF-001`, `JUND-MF-004`, and `JUND-MF-006`.

## Vox Mana Read

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Jund is the moment the self stops asking whether hunger is respectable.

It is desire made honest, instinct made mobile, and survival made immediate. Its sacred pattern is not violence for violence's sake. It is Red naming the feeling as true, Black refusing to betray the self's need, and Green letting the answer arrive through the body before the world can cage it.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-004` through `JUND-EVID-010`; synthesis permission in `docs/research/jund/jund-lore-source-packet.md` section `Safe Vox Mana Synthesis`; row `JUND-EVID-021`.

## Primary Tension

**Support status:** `Vox Mana synthesis`; not MTG canon doctrine.

Jund's primary tension is that freedom through appetite and instinct can drift into flattening, overconsumption, or mistaking impulse for truth. The same courage that trusts feeling can start treating every feeling as final. The same appetite that protects the self can begin to consume context, relationship, and consequence.

This is not a claim about Jund society, rulers, locations, creature hierarchy, or chronology. It is a Vox Mana architecture reading from Red's self-truth, Black's self-interest, Green's unrestrained instinct, and White/Blue as missing pressures.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-004` through `JUND-EVID-013`; `JUND-EVID-021` only as labeled `Vox Mana synthesis`.

### Matrix Implications

Jund should rank high for Red-centered freedom, instinct, appetite, embodied action, survival pressure, emotional honesty, combat pressure, and support-only conversion engines. It should rank lower for public honor, bureaucratic order, detached optimization as the primary motive, civilization-rejection as the whole identity, death-world necromancy, lifecycle/rot doctrine, and generic midrange player-culture shorthand. The primary tension is the safety valve: Jund should feel free and alive, but not so flattened that every impulse becomes truth or every appetite becomes identity.

## Summary

Jund metaphysics is appetite made honest: Red trusts the feeling, Black protects the self's hunger, and Green lets instinct become force. VM-178 makes the shadow clearer: appetite can overconsume, and instinct can mistake immediacy for truth. Vox Mana should keep that architecture vivid, bodily, and consequence-heavy without importing unsupported Jund lore or collapsing the shard into Naya, Grixis, Gruul, Rakdos, Golgari, Witherbloom, Riveteers, Modern Jund, or devour alone.
