> Source note: identity.md is evidence-backed from the VM-176 Jund packet. It is architecture documentation only, not raw-faction JSON, generated placement data, or runtime behavior. `JUND` is the expression name; `BRG` is color direction metadata only in this phase.

## Identity Overview

Jund is Vox Mana's Red-centered `BRG` expression for the Alara shard `JUND`. The VM-176 source packet supports a narrow but strong identity floor: Jund is the black-red-green shard discussed in the local official Rosewater Jund article, Red is the center/design lens, and the shard's safe architecture foundation is self-truth, gut instinct, emotion as guide, freedom under consequence, Black self-interest, Green unrestrained instinct, and the absence of White/Blue pressures.

This file does not make Jund live. It does not add `JUND` to placement, add `BRG` as an alias, create raw-faction JSON, create lookup keys, or authorize generated/runtime changes.

Evidence anchors: `docs/research/jund/jund-evidence-ledger.md` rows `JUND-EVID-001` through `JUND-EVID-013`, plus the non-live boundary in `JUND-EVID-023`.

## Core Drive

Jund wants to be fully itself before restraint, theory, or permission can tame the impulse.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Red supplies the center: self-truth, gut instinct, emotional honesty, freedom, and action.
- Black supplies appetite: self-interest, personal need, contentment, and refusal of imposed White constraint.
- Green supplies feral momentum: unrestrained instinct, less overthinking, and natural force.

The core drive is not generic violence. It is an evidence-bound freedom engine: Red listens to the body and the feeling, Black refuses self-denial, and Green turns instinct into motion.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Red-Centered Freedom

Red is the center of Jund's VM-176 evidence floor. That keeps the expression from flattening into generic `BRG` value, generic destructive appetite, or Green-Black nature horror with Red decoration. The supported lens is self-truth, gut instinct, emotional acknowledgement, freedom, and doing what feels right.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-003`, `JUND-EVID-004`, and `JUND-EVID-005`.

### Appetite As Self-Advocacy

Black contributes the willingness to honor personal need and contentment. In the Jund frame, Black supports Red's refusal to let outside moral pressure define the self. VM-177 may describe appetite as self-advocacy, but it must not turn appetite into generic cruelty or Rakdos spectacle.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-007`, `JUND-EVID-012`, and `JUND-EVID-013`.

### Instinct As Momentum

Green contributes unrestrained instinct, less overthinking, and feral force. This lets Jund move from feeling into embodied action. VM-177 may use words like instinct, feral, and momentum as design-language texture, but it must not claim detailed ecology, predator hierarchy, or dragon rule without future evidence.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-008`, `JUND-EVID-009`, and manual boundary `JUND-MF-007`.

### Missing White And Blue Pressures

VM-176 supports White and Blue as the missing enemy-color pressures in the Jund shard frame. Architecture may say this removes imposed conformity/order and detached over-analysis at the design level. It must not expand that into unsupported claims that Jund has no social structure, no intelligence, no memory, no culture, or no planning.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-010`; manual-fill boundaries `JUND-MF-001`, `JUND-MF-004`, and `JUND-MF-006`.

### Not Simply Savage

Jund should not be reduced to "Red violence," generic anger, savage nature, devour midrange, or Modern Jund. The source frame starts with Red explaining the dignity of feeling and the cost of freedom. Vox Mana may write danger, appetite, pressure, and survival, but those terms must stay tied to the source-bound Red-centered frame.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-012`, `JUND-EVID-013`, and `JUND-EVID-019`.

## Shard Separators

These are lightweight contrast notes only. They contrast Jund against Bant, Esper, Grixis, and Naya from Jund's VM-176/VM-177 evidence floor and broad color-structure differences; they do not author positive doctrine for other shards.

### Jund And Bant

**Separator:** Bant-facing order regulates force through public honor and community endorsement. Jund removes White law and Blue planning pressures, then lets Red-centered feeling, Black appetite, and Green instinct answer directly.

Jund should not win when the strongest signals are sanctioned excellence, public honor, institutional harmony, or a champion acting as the accepted face of the whole. Jund should strengthen when the reading centers freedom under consequence, refusal of imposed conformity, appetite as self-advocacy, and instinctive action.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-003` through `JUND-EVID-010`, `JUND-EVID-012`, `JUND-EVID-013`, and comparator row `JUND-EVID-019`.

### Jund And Esper

**Separator:** Esper-facing control seeks designed improvement through structure and information. Jund rejects detached over-analysis as a missing Blue pressure and treats feeling, appetite, and instinct as first signals.

Jund should not win when the strongest signals are artifact perfection, planned alteration, procedural optimization, or information control. Jund should strengthen when the reading wants action before redesign, appetite before optimization, and embodied survival over engineered improvement.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-004`, `JUND-EVID-005`, `JUND-EVID-010`, `JUND-EVID-012`, and comparator row `JUND-EVID-019`.

### Jund And Grixis

**Separator:** Grixis-facing UBR pressure centers Black survival, Blue calculation, and Red immediacy. Jund shares Black/Red danger but replaces Blue calculation with Green instinct and keeps Red as the center.

Jund should not import Grixis death-world, necromancy, vis, or survival-through-exploitation identity. If the reading centers calculated leverage, death magic, hidden weakness maps, or UBR villain coding, keep it away from Jund. Jund should strengthen when the reading is more bodily, appetite-led, and instinctive than calculating.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-003`, `JUND-EVID-008`, `JUND-EVID-010`, `JUND-EVID-013`, and comparator row `JUND-EVID-019`.

### Jund And Naya

**Separator:** Naya is the closest shard-level false positive because it shares Red and Green. From Jund's side, the split is Red-centered appetite and survival pressure against Naya-facing communal/behemoth ecology.

Jund should not win when the strongest signals are awe, communal ecology, large-creature reverence, or Green-centered belonging. Jund should strengthen when Red-centered freedom, Black self-advocacy, and consequence-heavy appetite are doing the identity work.

Evidence anchors: `JUND-EVID-003`, `JUND-EVID-006`, `JUND-EVID-007`, `JUND-EVID-008`, `JUND-EVID-012`, `JUND-EVID-013`, and comparator row `JUND-EVID-019`.
