# Simic Combine - Life As Improvable System

Source boundary: this document treats Simic Combine as an expression-level Ravnica guild, not as mono Blue plus mono Green and not as generic blue-green nature science. The schema shape follows `docs/reference/identity-metaphysics-markdown-schema.md`; the guild-specific content is evidence-backed by the approved repo sources. Vox Mana axiom, mapping, relationship, and operator language are project synthesis unless a sentence explicitly names direct source evidence.

## Identity Overview

Simic Combine is the green-blue Ravnica guild whose identity centers on biological research, adaptation, medicine, experimental improvement, and managed evolution of living systems. The raw faction claims state this directly: Simic is associated with biological research, adaptation, medicine, and experimental improvement.

The direct evidence gives Simic several strong anchors:

- Life is treated as a system that can adapt, heal, and improve when studied deeply enough.
- The guild is organized around scientists, physicians, surgeons, clades, zonots, public health, and biological research.
- The Holdfast and the Upwelling frame Simic through rooted natural connection and constant improvement.
- Adapt is a Simic signature mechanic, and the Commander guidance names biomancy, adaptation, +1/+1 counters, evolve, adapt, graft, creature improvement, Prime Speakers Zegana and Vannifar, and Momir Vig's cytoplast legacy as owned or strongly native themes.

The light Vox Mana synthesis is therefore not "nature science" in the abstract. Simic is the guild expression of life as an improvable system: living bodies, species, clades, and civic biology can be studied, changed, strengthened, and sometimes over-controlled.

## Core Drive

Simic's core drive is to guide living systems toward greater adaptation, resilience, and possibility.

It does not primarily seek communal harmony, death-life reclamation, mathematical proof, volatile invention, legal order, or wild rejection of civilization. Simic asks what a living thing could become if its structure, environment, and evolutionary pressure were understood well enough to intervene.

Direct evidence supports this drive through the raw profile's philosophy, the claims file's Holdfast/Upwelling and public-health claims, the placement file's calibrated primary read of biological adaptation and living-system experimentation, and the Rosewater two-color article's emphasis on improving upon nature, speeding evolution, observation, experimentation, creature focus, and the "build your own monster" feel.

## Vox Mana Read (Core Axiom)

Core axiom: life is unfinished; studied change can make it more resilient.

This is a compression-only Vox Mana reading, not MTG canon and not a new doctrine. It compresses the approved evidence that Simic treats life as adaptable, studies organisms as systems, uses biological experimentation, and makes improvement a practical and philosophical center.

The axiom should be used as an interpretive handle only. It should not be expanded into unsupported claims that Simic endorses Phyrexian compleation, erases selfhood as an ideal, operates like Quandrix mathematics with creatures attached, or treats all growth as good without ethical risk.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Life Is A Modifiable System

Direct evidence: the raw profile states that life is a system that can adapt, heal, and improve when studied deeply enough. The claims file identifies Simic with biological research, adaptation, medicine, and experimental improvement.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic does not treat nature as a finished sacred object. It treats nature as living architecture: complex, adaptive, partially legible, and capable of revision.

### Holdfast And Upwelling

Direct evidence: the claims file says official guidance frames Simic philosophy through the Holdfast and the Upwelling: rooted natural connection and constant improvement.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic's improvement logic is not only invention imposed from outside. It is improvement claimed as rooted in life itself. The Holdfast keeps Simic tied to living continuity; the Upwelling pushes that continuity toward ongoing change.

### Knowledge Accelerates Evolution

Direct evidence: the Rosewater two-color article frames the green-blue conflict as nature and nurture, says knowledge allows change, and presents Simic as striving to improve upon nature by speeding evolution through observation and experimentation.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic reads knowledge as an evolutionary accelerator. Understanding an organism is not just contemplation; it is permission to ask what change could make the organism more fit for what comes next.

### Civic Biology And Public Health

Direct evidence: the claims file says Simic members are scientists, physicians, surgeons, and maintainers of Ravnica's public health. The raw profile presents Simic as clade-organized researchers living around the zonots.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic is Ravnica's biological infrastructure as much as its laboratory. Its experiments sit beside medicine, water-system ecology, health, and adaptive defense.

### Stewardship Versus Intervention

Direct evidence: the raw profile names Simic's core tension: it values natural connection and public health, but its experiments can become invasive, secretive, or improvement-obsessed. `data/factions.json` also frames Simic through preservation, forced improvement, balance, and militarized adaptation.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic's central moral pressure is not whether life matters. It is who gets to change life, when care becomes experimentation, and when adaptation becomes control.

## Gameplay Philosophy

Simic gameplay turns the battlefield into a guided adaptation chamber. Creatures enter, scale, mutate, receive counters, trigger evolve or adapt, become tutoring rungs, or convert size into cards. The player experience is iterative rather than merely explosive: each living object can become evidence for the next improvement.

The Commander guidance says Simic recommendations should favor commanders that make creatures change, scale, or climb an evolutionary chain. It also names counters, pod lines, card draw from creature size, and experimental growth engines as native recommendations.

This is distinct from:

- Quandrix proof, equations, fractals, abstract pattern, and reality-as-math.
- Witherbloom life-force exchange, pestcraft, medicine, toxins, and life/death essence craft.
- Izzet invention, volatile prototypes, spells, explosions, and discovery-for-discovery's-sake.
- Golgari death, decay, rot farms, undercity reclamation, and graveyard civic ecology.

Simic can be careful, clinical, visionary, or dangerous, but its gameplay center is managed transformation of living systems.

## Philosophical Weaknesses

Direct evidence supports several Simic weak points:

- The raw profile says Simic experiments can become invasive, secretive, or improvement-obsessed.
- The placement file says Simic is a poor fit for treating nature as untouchable, rejecting experimentation categorically, or caring more about spectacle than sustained inquiry.
- The Rosewater article names Simic's strength as focus and its weakness as an inability to stop, with experiments leading from one to the next.

Vox Mana synthesis: Simic can mistake change for care. Because it can imagine a better-adapted organism, it may underweight consent, continuity, ecological side effects, or the value of leaving a living form alone. Its public-health role and stewardship language can become cover for invasive optimization.

Unsupported material to avoid: do not claim Simic is Phyrexian compleation in guild clothing, a pure eugenics doctrine, generic mad science, or Quandrix with specimens. Those frames flatten the evidence and erase Simic's specific Ravnica role as biological infrastructure, medical/scientific engine, and adaptive defense system.

## Operator Translation Signals (Maze / Scryfall)

### Maze Signals

Strong positive Simic placement signals:

- Wants to improve living systems rather than merely preserve them.
- Thinks biologically and experimentally.
- Uses adaptation, mutation, biomancy, evolve, specimens, or organisms as primary language.
- Centers clades, zonots, Prime Speakers, public health, medicine, or Ravnica biological infrastructure.
- Treats change as guided growth, managed evolution, or engineered resilience.

Suppress or redirect Simic when the user centers:

- Generic systems thinking without organism/adaptation language.
- Pure proof, equations, fractals, or formal abstraction, which points toward Quandrix.
- Life-force cost, pestcraft, draining, toxins, or essence exchange, which points toward Witherbloom.
- Invention, spell engineering, prototypes, explosions, or volatile discovery, which points toward Izzet.
- Harmony, peace, and shared communal belonging without biological experimentation, which points toward Selesnya.
- Rot, graveyard value, reclamation, and decay infrastructure, which points toward Golgari.
