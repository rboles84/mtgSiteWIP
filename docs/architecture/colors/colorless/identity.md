# Colorless - The Foundation Outside Color

Status: VM-309 / VM-310 architecture
Live state: Colorless remains architecture-only and non-live
Source floor: VM-308 Colorless packet

Colorless is Vox Mana's architecture label for the identity outside the WUBRG color system. It is not White, Blue, Black, Red, Green, a mono-color expression, or a sixth color. It is the non-color condition around the wheel: objects with no color, mana represented by `{C}`, terrain where color has been stripped into Wastes, and beings or tools whose meaning is not generated by ordinary color allegiance. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`, `COLORLESS-EVID-007`, `COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `COLORLESS-EVID-013`)

## Core Identity

Colorless begins by refusing the premise that every identity must be another color-position. The five colors argue through values: order, knowledge, ambition, feeling, and growth. Colorless is not a sixth voice in that argument. It is the machine that can be used by any voice, the absence left after color is stripped away, the alien scale that makes the argument local, and the rare perspective that can act without adopting color allegiance. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`, `COLORLESS-EVID-011`, `COLORLESS-EVID-012`, `COLORLESS-EVID-014`)

Vox Mana synthesis: Colorless is not neutrality achieved by balancing the five colors. It is orthogonality: a condition beside the color debate, outside it, or beneath it. That can feel useful, empty, terrifying, or lucid depending on which branch is active.

## Required Distinctions

Colorless has several meanings that must remain separate.

- A colorless object is an object with no color under Magic's rules.
- Colorless mana is a type of mana represented by `{C}`.
- Generic mana is a cost requirement that can be paid with any mana.
- Artifacts are a major branch of Colorless expression, not the same thing as Colorless identity.

Colorless mana can pay generic costs, but a `{C}` cost specifically requires colorless mana. Generic mana, colorless mana, artifacts, and Colorless identity are not interchangeable concepts. (`COLORLESS-EVID-007`, `COLORLESS-EVID-008`, `COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `COLORLESS-MF-011`)

## Branches

### Artifact / Function

Artifacts express Colorless as function: tools, engines, equipment, constructed bodies, mana rocks, and modular systems that do not carry an inherent color philosophy. This branch is about use, assembly, and instrumental power.

The boundary is essential. Artifacts can be colored, can belong to colored civilizations, and can support any Commander strategy without becoming that strategy's identity. Artifact/function is a Colorless branch, not the whole of Colorless. (`COLORLESS-EVID-011`, `COLORLESS-MF-007`)

### Eldrazi / Void

Eldrazi express Colorless as alienness and outside-color design. Their colorlessness should not be reduced to artifact neutrality, ordinary villainy, or generic large-creature play. Devoid can support Eldrazi colorless-matters design while preserving colored mana costs where needed. (`COLORLESS-EVID-010`, `COLORLESS-EVID-012`)

Vox Mana synthesis: the Eldrazi branch feels like reality encountering a force that does not debate the color wheel because it does not belong to the same metaphysical weather.

### Wastes / Desolation

Wastes express Colorless through land, mana infrastructure, and the residue of color stripped away. This branch is tied to `{C}` support and to the idea of terrain where the familiar colored structure has been damaged or emptied.

Wastes are not a shorthand for every Colorless card. They are one specific desolation branch, and exact card/rules claims require later official verification before raw or public use. (`COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `COLORLESS-EVID-013`, `COLORLESS-MF-002`, `COLORLESS-MF-003`)

### Ugin / Karn / Transcendence

Ugin and Karn can anchor a narrower branch of Colorless as detached agency: beings associated with colorless action, artificial origin, spirit-dragon distance, broad intervention, or perspective beyond ordinary color allegiance.

This branch is support-bound in VM-309. It may carry Vox Mana synthesis, but detailed lore claims about Ugin, Karn, ghostfire, planeswalker motives, or exact story beats remain `Manual fill required` until stronger direct sources are added. (`COLORLESS-EVID-014`, `COLORLESS-MF-005`)

## Commander / Support Boundary

`Eldrazi Unbound` is the only direct Colorless Commander row identified by VM-308. It may support Commander-facing texture around colorless Eldrazi play, colorless infrastructure, and Eldrazi deck identity, but it does not prove broad Colorless Commander doctrine by itself.

`Eldrazi Incursion` is a five-color comparator, not Colorless proof. Commander-facing claims must not extrapolate beyond `Eldrazi Unbound` unless later approved evidence expands the source floor. (`COLORLESS-EVID-015`, `COLORLESS-MF-004`)

## Concept-Boundary Parity

Colorless parity does not use a present-color pair matrix. There is no WG, UB, or shard overlap to map because Colorless is not a color combination. VM-310 parity therefore maps conceptual near-matches: places where another concept can look Colorless-adjacent but is not enough to prove Colorless identity.

### Five-Color / WUBRG Boundary

Five-color is full inclusion of WUBRG. Colorless is non-coloration, absence of colored identity, or operation outside colored identity. They are not opposites, and Colorless must not be framed as superior to, above, or complete mastery of WUBRG. The acceptable frame is outside or orthogonal. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`, `COLORLESS-EVID-016`)

### Mono-Color Boundary

Colorless is not a sixth mono-color and should not inherit mono-color psychology. It can sit structurally beside color docs for architecture reasons, but it is not another color philosophy with its own spot on the wheel. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`)

### Generic Mana Cost Boundary

Generic mana costs, such as costs that can be paid with any mana, are not colorless mana requirements. A card or deck using large generic costs is not automatically Colorless identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-008`, `COLORLESS-MF-011`)

### Colorless Mana Requirement Boundary

Actual `{C}` requirements are stronger Colorless infrastructure signals because they ask for colorless mana specifically. Even then, a `{C}` requirement is a mechanics signal, not the whole identity by itself. (`COLORLESS-EVID-008`, `COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `COLORLESS-MF-011`)

### Colorless Identity Boundary

Colorless identity requires a non-color frame or one of the guarded branches, not only a colorless-looking cost, a gray visual treatment, a neutral tone, or a generic deckbuilding shell. (`COLORLESS-EVID-006`, `COLORLESS-EVID-007`, `COLORLESS-EVID-018`)

### Artifact / Colored Artifact Boundary

Artifacts can be a major Colorless branch, but artifact presence alone is insufficient. Colored artifacts, artifact civilizations, equipment packages, and artifact-heavy colored decks can be adjacent without becoming Colorless identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-011`, `COLORLESS-MF-007`)

### Eldrazi Boundary

Eldrazi colorlessness can signal alien outside-color pressure, especially where Devoid or colorless design support appears. Eldrazi alone is not proof of Colorless identity, and five-color Eldrazi support is comparator material rather than Colorless proof. (`COLORLESS-EVID-010`, `COLORLESS-EVID-012`, `COLORLESS-EVID-015`)

### Wastes Boundary

Wastes are a specific Colorless terrain and `{C}` infrastructure branch. Wastes can support desolation language, but Wastes do not stand for every Colorless object, every Eldrazi card, or every artifact deck. (`COLORLESS-EVID-009`, `COLORLESS-EVID-013`, `COLORLESS-MF-003`)

### Ugin / Karn Boundary

Ugin and Karn can support detached-agency and transcendence readings only while their lore specifics remain manual-fill constrained. They should not be used to claim that Colorless is inherently enlightened, higher, or more complete than colored identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-014`, `COLORLESS-MF-005`)

### Phyrexia Boundary

Phyrexia must not be collapsed into Colorless. Phyrexia usually infects, converts, subjugates, or weaponizes color; Eldrazi colorlessness marks outside-color design or reality pressure. Phyrexia material belongs only as distinction context unless later evidence authorizes a specific claim. (`COLORLESS-EVID-017`)

### Devoid Boundary

Devoid is useful design evidence for making cards colorless despite colored mana costs, especially around Eldrazi. It does not make every colored-cost Eldrazi, every exile mechanic, or every colorless-matters design into the whole Colorless identity. (`COLORLESS-EVID-010`)

### Commander-Support Boundary

`Eldrazi Unbound` remains the only direct Colorless Commander support row identified by VM-308. It can shape Commander-facing texture, but it does not authorize broad Colorless Commander doctrine, product claims, or raw data claims. (`COLORLESS-EVID-015`, `COLORLESS-MF-004`)

## False Positive Risks

- False positive: treating Colorless as a sixth color, hidden mono-color, or superior completion of WUBRG.
- False positive: treating generic mana costs as colorless mana requirements.
- False positive: treating a `{C}` requirement as the whole Colorless identity.
- False positive: treating artifacts, artifact-heavy decks, equipment packages, or colored artifacts as automatically Colorless.
- False positive: treating Eldrazi, five-color Eldrazi, or Devoid as automatic Colorless proof.
- False positive: treating Wastes as all of Colorless rather than a specific land/desolation branch.
- False positive: treating Ugin or Karn support as proof that Colorless is inherently enlightened, higher, or complete.
- False positive: treating Phyrexian corruption, conversion, or artifact body horror as Colorless.
- False positive: treating `Eldrazi Unbound`, `Eldrazi Incursion`, or Commander support rows as lore proof or raw-claim authority.
- False positive: treating VM-310 separator prose as evidence instead of architecture guidance.

## Identity Statement

Colorless is the foundation outside color: function without allegiance, void without familiar values, desolation where color has been stripped away, and rare transcendence that can act without becoming one of the five colors. It is not a sixth color, not generic mana, not simply artifacts, and not all Eldrazi everywhere. It is the architecture of non-color made legible without making it ordinary.
