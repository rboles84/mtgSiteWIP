## Identity Overview

Mardu Horde is Vox Mana's Red-centered `RWB` / `WBR` Tarkir wedge expression for speed, decisive action, martial code, raid momentum, and opportunity seized before the world can settle. The VM-223 packet supports a bounded identity floor: Mardu is the red-white-black wedge; Red is the design center; speed is the design attribute; Khans-era Mardu Horde identity is grounded in battlefield motion, honor, brutality, war names, Edicts of Ilagra, raiding economy, Zurgo's khanate, and lightning-marked locations. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-001` through `MARDU-EVID-012`, plus synthesis permission in `MARDU-EVID-031`.

This file keeps Mardu non-live. It does not create a runtime `MARDU` key, add `RWB` or `WBR` as aliases, create raw-faction JSON, create generated data, alter placement, or authorize route, Home, Maze, schema, Supabase, fixture, or builder changes. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-001`, `MARDU-EVID-032`.

## Core Drive

Mardu wants to turn the opening moment into victory before hesitation can become a rival.

In Vox Mana terms:

- Red supplies the center: action, freedom through motion, speed, directness, and the willingness to commit before the chance closes. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.
- White supplies the war shape: structure, formation, earned names, khan authority, code, and oath-like discipline around total commitment. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.
- Black supplies the ruthless edge: opportunity, advantage, weakness exploitation, and the readiness to spend force now rather than preserve an empire for later. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-020`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

The core drive is not generic aggro, generic `RWB`, or Commander goodstuff. It is an evidence-bound war engine: Red decides that motion is life, White gives the charge its formation and code, and Black turns the enemy's exposed moment into permission to strike. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-001`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-027`, `MARDU-EVID-028`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

## Philosophical Foundations

### Red-Centered Speed

Red is the center of this architecture layer. The supported design floor is speed: action, early pressure, strategic timing, rapid coordinated attack, and all-in aggression across the three colors. Architecture may call this Red-centered only while keeping it tied to the VM-223 evidence floor and non-live boundary. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-001`, `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-032`.

### Code Without Empire

Khans-era Mardu are not a patient state-building project. The Edicts of Ilagra support conquest as sustenance, rejection of long-term rule and infrastructure, and total commitment to attack. The architecture read should frame White's structure as martial code, formation, and name-worthiness rather than bureaucracy or permanent empire. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-008`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Honor Under Motion

Mardu honor is not stillness. VM-223 supports war names earned through battlefield deeds, respect-earning khan authority, and a warrior culture defined by speed, brutality, and honor. Vox Mana may describe this as identity proven through action, but full biographies and card-level proof remain bounded. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-011`, `MARDU-MF-004`, `MARDU-MF-005`.

### Violence As Commitment, Not Randomness

Mardu brutality is structured by code, leadership, roles, magic, and tactics. The supported floor includes archery, concealment magic, war shrieks, wind magic, riders, hordechiefs, wranglers, feudmasters, and battle-tested shamans. Architecture should read this as coordinated force, not undifferentiated chaos. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-009`, `MARDU-EVID-010`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Zurgo-Era Horde

Zurgo Helmsmasher is the safe Khans-era named figure anchor: the Mardu khan at Wingthrone, commanding raiding parties from a stronghold built where an ancient lightning dragon died. VM-224 must not broaden that into a full Zurgo biography, Bellstriker continuity, Stormrender product reading, or later reference chain. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-011`, `MARDU-EVID-019`, `MARDU-MF-005`, `MARDU-MF-006`.

### Alesha As Ancient Bridge

Fate Reforged and story rows support Alesha as a young Mardu khan, rider, archer, swordfighter, war-name judge, and story anchor for identity/name recognition. VM-224 may use Alesha as an ancient bridge for war names, speed, and Mardu self-knowledge, but it must not write a full biography without later capture. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-013`, `MARDU-EVID-014`, `MARDU-EVID-015`, `MARDU-EVID-016`, `MARDU-EVID-017`, `MARDU-MF-004`.

### Kolaghan As Boundary, Not Horde Identity

Kolaghan clan material is contrast and timeline evidence, not Khans-era Mardu Horde identity. It supports a Dragons-era dragonlord clan with little internal structure, no official rank, the crave, and lightning-fast brutality under Dragonlord Kolaghan. Use Kolaghan as an anti-bleed boundary around Mardu, not as proof that Zurgo-era Mardu works the same way. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-018`, `MARDU-EVID-019`, `MARDU-MF-002`.

### Dragonstorm Mardu As Modern Timeline

Modern Dragonstorm-era Mardu is valid only when timeline-labeled. It supports a restored clan after Kolaghan rule and Phyrexian-invasion losses, with Dalkovan Assembly, mobile camp-cities, Decree of Thunder, Lightning Proving, stormsingers, clan dragons, dragonstorm mobility, and modern locations. VM-224 may use this as modern contrast and future-facing texture, not as Khans-era backfill. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-020` through `MARDU-EVID-026`, `MARDU-MF-006`, `MARDU-MF-007`.

## Pair-Overlap Boundaries

These are Mardu-side placement boundaries only. They do not define Boros, Orzhov, Rakdos, or any other expression; they only prevent Mardu from absorbing adjacent color-pair patterns without a positive Mardu evidence cluster.

### Mardu And Boros

Mardu can share Red-White combat speed, martial organization, direct attack, and action under code. The Mardu difference is Black opportunity: the charge is not only disciplined or heroic, it looks for the exposed opening and spends force decisively before the chance closes. If a candidate only shows Red-White combat, training, zeal, or discipline without Black opportunity, Horde evidence, war-name pressure, or total-commitment raiding, keep it out of Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Mardu And Orzhov

Mardu can share White-Black structure, obligation, rank, power, and hierarchy texture. The Mardu difference is Red speed: order does not become institution-first leverage, long obligation, or slow control; it becomes formation for immediate action, earned names, and war-band commitment. If a candidate centers debt, attrition, slow hierarchy, or White-Black control without Red-centered speed and Mardu raid momentum, keep it out of Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-008`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Mardu And Rakdos

Mardu can share Red-Black aggression, appetite, sacrifice texture, and opportunistic pressure. The Mardu difference is White code: the force has formation, war names, khan authority, and martial expectation rather than pure indulgence or spectacle. If a candidate only shows Red-Black appetite, sacrifice, cruelty, or chaos without White-supported code, formation, Horde roles, or earned-name identity, keep it out of Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-006`, `MARDU-EVID-009`, `MARDU-EVID-010`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

Mardu uses White-Black pressure for speed, war names, attack commitment, and opportunity seized now, not house continuity, ancestor duty, or long survival through obligation. If the center is endurance, kinship continuity, or protective inheritance rather than Red-centered motion and raid commitment, keep it away from Mardu. Jeskai-facing overlap appears when Red-White discipline, speed, training, perception, or technique becomes institutional perfection. Mardu requires Black opportunity and Horde war-code: speed is used to end the opening, not to perfect technique for its own sake. If a candidate centers formal discipline, contemplation, or perfected practice more than total-commitment attack and opportunistic edge, keep it away from Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Sultai-Facing Black Pressure

Sultai-facing overlap appears when Black opportunity, advantage, or ruthless pressure begins to read as resource conversion, necromancy, coercive calculation, or hidden exploitation. Mardu opportunity is immediate martial opening: strike while the field is exposed, earn the name in motion, and avoid becoming a slow advantage engine. If the center is conversion of bodies, secrets, death, or resources rather than attack timing and war-bound action, keep it away from Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-002`, `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-008`, `MARDU-EVID-009`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.

### Temur-Facing Red Pressure

Temur-facing overlap appears when Red motion, harshness, survival, or clan life starts to look like immediate force. Mardu speed is war-bound conquest, raiding, code, and total commitment; it is not survival through attunement, terrain listening, or self-sufficient endurance. If a candidate centers land-sense, harsh-terrain survival, or instinctive defense more than martial attack and Horde evidence, keep it away from Mardu. Evidence anchors: `MARDU-EVID-003`, `MARDU-EVID-004`, `MARDU-EVID-005`, `MARDU-EVID-008`, `MARDU-EVID-031`.
