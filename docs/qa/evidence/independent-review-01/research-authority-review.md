The claim register distinguishes official material, broad Commander consensus, and useful heuristics. Public copy is qualified where the evidence is not an official rules source. The Finding-a-Table route uses behavioral signals and provisional language rather than a percentage or compatibility score. Before-the-Game copy describes deck plan, finish, timing, disclosures, and agreements without claiming pod consent. During-the-Game guidance is conversational and table-safe; rules disputes route toward official lookup or an agreed human rules resource rather than inventing a ruling. No source review found public psychographic labels, permanent reputation, threat scoring, target recommendations, or tactical table-politics advice.

The research repository was read-only inspected and not modified. Its unrelated dirty/untracked state was not used as candidate evidence beyond the cited source material.
