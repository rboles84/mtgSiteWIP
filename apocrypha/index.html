Where Vox Mana shows its work.

How These References Are Used

How public references support the site.

This is the short version: what each reference type helps with, and how public sources support the site.

Official Lore

Baseline authority

Official lore is treated as the strongest foundation for named events, faction voice, and continuity.

Community References

Interpretation support

Community references help clarify structure, compare readings, and show how players commonly understand the lore.

Video Lore

Review and cross-checking

Video sources are used as review surfaces and cross-check aids, not as standalone canon.

Archives And Indexes

Navigation and continuity

GitHub archives, story indexes, and public collections help track story material and keep continuity easier to follow.

Working notes may inform drafts, but only reviewed public references are listed here as source support.
